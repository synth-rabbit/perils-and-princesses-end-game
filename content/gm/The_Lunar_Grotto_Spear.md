# The Lunar Grotto Spear

![[items/assets/lunar_grotto_spear.png]]

> *"Forged from glowing sea-glass and bound in silver wire, this spear hums with the tide of subterranean waters and the light of the moon."*

* **Type:** Magic Shortspear (d6 damage, Thrown 30 ft, Versatile d8)
* **Tailored For:** Princess Filly

---

## Passive Abilities

* **Returning Current:**  
  When thrown, the spear dissolves into a stream of pure water upon impact and instantly reforms in Filly's hand.

* **Water Grace:**  
  Spend Gift Dice to modify any **GRACE test** by `[SUM]` when swimming or climbing wet, slick rock surfaces.

---

## Gift Abilities (`[SUM]` & `[DICE]`)

* **Lunar Surge:**  
  When making a melee or thrown attack with the spear, choose to roll 1 or more Gift Dice:
  * Modify your attack roll up to `[SUM]`.
  * If the attack hits, deal `[SUM]` additional **Cold/Water damage**.
  * **Pack Hunt:** If Tawny is engaged with the target, deal an extra `[DICE]d4` damage!

* **Tidal Wave Thrust:**  
  Thrust the spear into the ground or water, spending 1 or more Gift Dice to release a crashing wave of subterranean water:
  * `[DICE]` creatures within a Stone's Throw take `[SUM]` **Cold damage** and must succeed on a **GRACE test** or fall prone and be pushed back `[SUM]` feet.

---

## Gift Dice & Recharge Rules

* **Gift Dice Mechanics:**  
  Roll Gift Dice when activating Gift abilities. On a **1–2–3**, the Gift Die returns to your pool immediately. On a **4–5–6**, the die is consumed.
* **How to Recharge:**  
  Submerging the sea-glass tip of the spear in a pristine natural water source (such as the cyan pool in Area 3, the Blue Orb basin in Area 5, or the Pristine Pond in Location 11) for 1 minute during a **Picnic or Rest** OR exposing the spear to direct moonlight overnight restores all consumed Gift Dice.
