# Session 12 Scene 5: The Key Turns

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 30–40 min · **Foundry:** your existing **Dragon's Cave** map

The rescue, and the dragon the players promised to kill.

## At the cave

> *Filly's CLOSED sign is still on its rope. Behind it the air shimmers: the force field, back as if it had never gone. And behind that, filling the tunnel mouth, one enormous anxious golden eye.*
>
> **Amy:** "You're back! You're back, and you've got — oh. That's me. That's very strange. Hello, Embra."
>
> **Embra:** "...Hello, Amy."

Human-sized people pass the barrier freely. The party walks Embra in.

## The swap

Both wearers **Nearby**. The key goes into Embra's pendant first, then the dragon's. No roll.

> *The key turns twice. Both necklaces open at once with a sound like a held breath let go. For a moment there are two of each of them, overlapping, a girl-shaped dragon and a dragon-shaped girl —*
>
> *— and then Princess Amy sits down hard on the cave floor in a yellow dress, looks at her own two hands, and bursts into tears. Scamp is on her shoulder before the first one falls.*
>
> *Behind her, forty feet of black dragon lifts its head off the stone, stretches until its wings touch both walls, and says, with enormous satisfaction: "**Ah.**"*

**Level up now.** Amy's Powerful Friendship goes off like a struck bell the instant she is herself again, and it lands on the three people who came for her. **Everyone is level 4, immediately**: new ability, +1 Gift Die, +d4 HP, +1 Heart Die, roll Virtue Deepens. All Gift Dice and Heart Dice are restored, and **Filly's Befuddlement ends**. (Rules: [[session12_06_knighthoods]].) Do it at the table in two minutes and come back to the dragon.

## Embra, restored

She is inside the barrier, in her own body, with her own hoard, and the people who robbed her.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Embra|Embra]] (use [[Dragon]]) | 24 | 16 | 3 | Thrice: Bite (d12), two Claws (d8) | Fire Breath: wide area Nearby, 20 damage, 10 on GRACE. Once per Rest |

> **Embra:** "Now. You are standing in my house, wearing my things. I am going to say this once, because she was kind to me and you brought me home. **Put them back.**"

### Three ways this goes

**1. They give it back.** Each returned item ends that PC's curse on the spot: the slit pupils round out. Embra, mollified and watching Amy, **gives one item back freely** to each of them as the price of her freedom from the barrier: "A gift. Witness it. I do not do this." A freely given item carries no curse. Filly must choose between pendant and spectacles. Embra then talks someone outside through releasing the barrier, and leaves the Scorchwoods for somewhere without knights.
> "If you ever need a dragon — no. Don't. But if you do."

That is a **seventeenth Ally Call** for the castle if you want it: one pass of dragonfire along the Thornwall.

**2. They bargain.** She is a dragon; she likes a deal. She will trade *all four* items, freely given, for any of: the Ring of Fire back and the 260 SP; a promise, witnessed by Amy, that Drakenshire's hunts end; or the crystal teeth and horn taken from her mother's bones in the Boneyard, which she did not know they had and which stops her cold.
> "...Where did you get those." *A long silence.* "Keep the axe."

**3. They fight.** The plan the players announced. Run it: they are level 4 as of one minute ago, with full dice.
- **Round 1: Fire Breath.** Everyone in the cave mouth: 20, or 10 on **GRACE**. ⭐ **Marigold's Dragonfire Shield** takes none and stores it. Amy is behind Marigold if anyone thought to put her there; if not, a hero must take it for her.
- She fights in her treasure room where she has space; in the tunnels she can only bite.
- **Tests to evade her have no special advantage now**: she knows this body.
- ⭐ **Vega:** the cave is his element. Shape drops the ceiling (2d6, no Armor), Storm fills the chamber, and at level 4 **falling stone does not hurt him**.
- ⭐ **Filly:** her first **Wild Form**. Whatever she picks, Tawny's reaction to there being two of them is worth a line.
- ⭐ **Marigold:** Smooch needs [SUM] of 24 on four dice. It can happen.
- **Amy** will not fight her. From the tunnel: "Embra, *please*." Each round Amy pleads, Embra tests SAVE or loses one of her three attacks.
- At 8 HP or less Embra offers option 2. If slain, the curse ends for all three (GM ruling, session 11), the barrier falls, and Amy is very quiet for the rest of the day.

**Direction:** do not steer. The GM's stated plan is a fight with Embra's party and then on to the castle; that fight has happened at the camp. This is the coda, and all three ends are fine.
