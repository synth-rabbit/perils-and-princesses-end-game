# Session 10 Scene 3: Defend the Pond

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** remain on **Pristine Pond** (world scene; not included in the module). Stage three Vine Crawlers first, with three more and one Corruption Bloom held for the second wave.

> *The giggling stops. Something dark moves under the northern trees. Grass withers beneath dragging vines, and a tiny voice shouts, “They're coming again!” Thistledown draws a sword no bigger than a thorn. “Please. We can't lose the water.”*

**Direction:** Defend a place and its frightened inhabitants. Show the creatures' approach and give the PCs a moment to choose positions. The monster goal is reaching/corrupting the water; do not spend all attacks punishing the character who was already down last session.

**Pixie aid:** each PC has advantage on their **first attack each round** while near the pond. Do not stack it into extra dice or apply it to every defense and skill test.

**Encounter pace:** begin with three crawlers. Bring the second wave once those are dealt with or the party meaningfully stops them. Telegraph the Bloom as the controller: the vines twitch in time with its glowing center, and Thistledown points it out. Do not add creatures beyond the six crawlers and one Bloom to prolong a successful fight. If players solve the defense through terrain, purification, or another credible plan, honor that result.

**Water-edge hazard:** the location suggests GRACE with advantage when fighting on slippery banks. Use it for an actual risky maneuver near the edge, not a free additional check on every turn. Announce the risk to vulnerable supplies before a deliberate plunge.

⭐ **FILLY:** coordinate with Tawny, guard fleeing pixies, or strike the Bloom. She still has her spike; its actual sheet statistics apply. Reward her choice with the spear after the pond is saved, not only for personally landing the killing blow.

⭐ **VEGA:** use earth/stone gifts and Heartstone to block a vine approach, create a safe crossing, or expose the Bloom. Apply his actual Gift and Vein-Breaker rules; no automatic mass kill merely for owning the pick.

⭐ **MARIGOLD:** Healing Touch and the Staff support the endangered group. The established pond option is **RESOLVE with disadvantage** to purify the Bloom; success ends the attack without spores, failure uses her action. Radiant Wave uses [[Staff_of_the_Sunken_Sanctum]] as written; do not infer a new damage bonus from a descriptive line in old prep.

If the party retreats, keep the pond threat unresolved and reframe their next attempt from their plan. Do not announce the forest healed or award the spear while the outcome is still in doubt.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Describe the battlefield and ask for positions

**GM:** Indicate the northern treeline, clear water, open bank and a sheltered patch of reeds for the pixies on the existing scene. These are usable features, not hidden movement penalties.

**Ask:** “The vines are coming from the north, toward the water. Where do you and Tawny stand, and what are you trying to protect?” Resolve credible preparation before the first attack.

**Thistledown:**

> “We can confuse them. We can't hold them. Please keep them away from the pond!”

### Narration during the fight

**First wave advancing:**

> *Three bundles of roots pull themselves clear of the trees. Their leaves are black on one side and sickly violet on the other. They drag themselves toward the water with the patience of something that does not need to breathe.*

**A crawler grapples someone:**

> *The vine tightens like a wet rope. Tiny white roots begin searching for a grip between the seams of your clothes.*

**Pixie aid:**

> *A dozen false flowers blossom in front of the creature's face. As it lashes at the illusion, you see your opening.*

Grant advantage on each PC's first attack that round near the pond, not extra attacks.

**Second wave:**

> *The treeline bows outward. Behind three more crawlers, a swollen flower opens petal by petal. A violet pulse passes through its center. Every vine on the bank jerks at exactly the same instant.*

Thistledown points: “There! It is telling the others what to do!” The Bloom is an intelligible target; no perception gate hides the solution.

**If they use terrain:** roots diverted by a stone barrier must go around it; a credible trap buys space. Judge the actual Gift used rather than demanding the fight proceed unchanged. Do not assign an unsupported multiplier to crawler fire/slashing weakness.

### Marigold's alternative and endings

If Marigold considers purification, describe a clean green vein still visible inside the Bloom. Tell her the known option: **RESOLVE with disadvantage**; success ends the assault without death spores, failure uses her action.

**Purification succeeds:**

> *The violet light shivers, then fades. For a moment the flower is only a flower. Its roots loosen. All along the bank, the crawlers sink into harmless heaps of leaves.*

**Bloom destroyed:**

> *The center splits. Dust billows from the petals as every vine falls slack.*

Nearby exposed PCs test RESOLVE or become Woozy. Resolve that before the celebration. If the party retreats, let them escape according to the situation; leave the pond unresolved and award no victory loot.

**Staff reminder:** Marigold's Radiant Wave is once per Rest, a 15-foot pulse dealing d6 Radiant to undead, oozes and corrupted beasts; WITS or pushed 10 feet. Her staff's established command word is Aethelgard. Do not invent a second damage bonus or unlimited uses.
