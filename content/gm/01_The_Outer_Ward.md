# Level 1 — The Outer Ward

![[maps/morwennas_castle/painted_originals/map_rosehold_1_outer_ward.png]]

**Keyed GM plan** (room letters, secret doors):

![[maps/morwennas_castle/rosehold_1_outer_ward_gm.png]]

> **Runbook:** [[rosehold_runbook]] · **Tracker:** [[rosehold_tracker]] · **Parent:** [[Rosehold]] · **Foundry scene art:** module `build/art/maps/map_rosehold_1_outer_ward.png` (6144 px on the long side)

The castle's yard and outbuildings inside the curtain wall. Open ground, patrolled, overlooked by the Raven Tower. **Foundry:** the floor plan. 75–100 minutes if explored fully; 20 if the party runs straight for the keep steps.

**Patrols:** one patrol of [[Thornsworn_Guard|Thornsworn]] (4 guards and a sergeant) walks B1 → B5 → B1. A second sleeps in the Barracks. While the ravens in B7 are alive, anything done in the open court is seen.

## B1. The Court of Frozen Fountains

> *A wide yard under a hard white sky. Two fountains stand frozen mid-leap. Between them, life-size and rimed with frost, are statues of people running toward the keep: a cook with a ladle, a stable boy, a knight with her helm under her arm. They are not statues.*

- **The frozen household:** a ring of a dozen of Rosehold's old servants and knights, caught by the curse on the night it broke loose. Alive, asleep, and cold. Marigold's **Tears** thaw one per tear; **True Love's Kiss** is wasted here. If the castle is saved they all wake.
- **Wisps:** d4+2 [[Frostbind_Wisp|Frostbind Wisps]] drift among them and come for anyone who lingers.
- **The patrol** arrives on the second round of any fight here.
- **The fountains:** the one nearer the keep still has liquid water under the ice. Marigold's staff recharges in it.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Frostbind_Wisp]] ×d4+2 | 3 | 12 | 0 | Frost Touch (d4) + RESOLVE or Weary | Immune to ordinary weapons |
| [[Thornsworn_Guard]] ×4 | 4 | 8 | 1 | Halberd (d8) / Crossbow (d6) | Pull the thorn and they surrender. Roar lasts one round |
| Thornsworn sergeant | 6 | 10 | 2 | Twice: Sword (d8) | As above |

**A captured guard**, thorn pulled, is terrified and helpful: *"I don't know why I'm here. I took the Baron's coin to hunt three princesses and then there was a woman in a mirror and then it's — is it winter? It was summer."* He knows the patrol route, that "the wizard" is upstairs, and that "the black knight never sleeps, he just stands on the stair."

## B2. The Kennels

> *Low stone runs with the gates hanging open. The straw is frozen flat. Something in the dark at the back is growling, and it sounds like a hedge in a high wind.*

2d4 [[Briar_Hound|Briar Hounds]]. They hunt as a pack and will follow the party into the court.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Briar_Hound]] ×2d4 | 5 | 8 | 1 | Bite (d6), +1 when two or more on one target | Wounded = Thorn Curse (1 damage per Gift Die spent) |

**⭐ Filly:** they are plants, so she cannot talk to them. But on a hook by the door is the **hound-master's whistle**, and in the last run is one real dog: an ancient wolfhound, grey-muzzled, who has been keeping the briar hounds in line by sheer seniority. His name is **Bramble**. He will tell her everything about the yard for a ration, and he would like very much to leave.

**Secret:** the postern in the west wall (WITS to find, or Bramble shows it) opens onto the mountainside: a way out, or a way in for allies.

## B3. The Stables

> *Twelve stalls. In each one, a horse of clear ice stands with its head down, perfectly made, steam frozen at its nostrils.*

Not monsters; they are the castle's horses, frozen like the household. In the tack room: **four iron horseshoes** and a hayloft where a party can hide from a patrol (not a warm room).

If **Mari Lwyd** is called here, the Night Mare walks the row, breathes on each stall, and the ice horses lift their heads and follow her out. For the rest of the Outer Ward the party is mounted, and the patrol will not fight cavalry: they fall back to the Barracks.

## B4. The Briar Court

> *It was a rose garden. The beds are still laid out in a ring, and the roses are still there: black, the size of cabbages, on briars that have climbed the walls and gone over. In the middle a woman of pale light kneels with a pair of shears, cutting one stem. It grows back as she cuts it. She cuts it again.*

- **The hedge maze:** the briars form lanes. Reaching the centre without Spending Time is a **WITS** test (Sniff or Natural Climber give advantage). Failure: d4 from thorns and a wrong turn into the trees.
- **Guardians:** two [[Awakened_Tree|Awakened Trees]] at the centre, standing over the Echo like sentries. They attack anyone who harms a rose.
- **Thorn pit:** one lane ends in a pit covered by woven briar. **GRACE** or fall: 2d6 and Tangled at the bottom. Careful movers (Marigold; anyone who says they are checking) spot it.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Awakened_Tree]] ×2 | 8 | 8 | 2 | Twice: Bash (d6) | 1 GD Tangle. Wounded = Rootbound Curse |
| [[Echo_of_a_Luminary]]: **Aethelgard the Gardener** | 10 | 14 | 0 | Grief Wail (d6 Nearby, RESOLVE for half) | Can be freed |

**Freeing Aethelgard:** take the shears and help her finish the row: **WITS** test. **⭐ Marigold:** if the bearer of the Staff of the Sunken Sanctum speaks the name *Aethelgard*, no test is needed.

> *The Echo turns. She looks at the staff for a long time. "You came the long way round," she says. "So did I. I called and called. I didn't think anyone was listening." She puts the shears in your hand and closes your fingers over them. "The cold is not her. It is a thing that sits where her heart was. Pull it out by the root."*

Marigold gains **Aethelgard's Shears**, and the staff's **Radiant Wave** is restored at once. The trees stand down. Cold −1.

## B5. The Barracks

The second patrol, asleep in their boots: 4 [[Thornsworn_Guard|Thornsworn]] and a sergeant. Sleeping guards can have their thorns pulled without a test, one per hero per round, **GRACE** to stay quiet. In the sergeant's chest: **60 SP and the castle keys**.

Among the sleepers is a face the party knows: the **"ghoul" guard** they wiped the makeup off and handed to Daisy back at the grave mounds. He escaped custody and ran north to the only employer left. Thorn out: *"Oh no. Oh no, not you three again."* He will do anything they say.

**Secret door** to the Gatehouse stair.

## B6. The Armory and Smithy

> *Racks of halberds, all the same. On the back wall, older and finer, hang five shields with a rose on each. Four are split down the middle.*

- **Trap:** the fifth shield is whole. Lifting it down drops the rack: **GRACE** or d6, and the noise is +1 Cold. It is only a shield.
- **Loot:** a **silvered halberd**, **three frostproof cloaks**, and a cold forge. If Vega Spends Time here he can silver one more weapon from the party's coin (20 SP melted).
- **Secret door** behind the forge leads to the keep steps, out of the ravens' sight.

## B7. The Raven Tower

> *The tower top is open to the sky and every merlon has a bird on it. They are black glass, each one, and they turn their heads together.*

2d4+2 [[Glass_Raven|Glass Ravens]] and a ballista aimed at the causeway. This is where Puca's catapult lands.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Glass_Raven]] ×2d4+2 | 1 | 8 | 0 | Peck (d4) | Any that escape: +1 Cold. Shatter: GRACE or 1 damage |

Killing the flock blinds the Queen to the Outer Ward: the open-court penalty ends. Vega's Blast down the parapet takes most of them; Lady Michelle's song takes all of them.

In the roost: a brass **Courier Raven** statue, the old Society's, with a message tube still on its leg. The message was never sent. *"To any Initiate in the field: Rosehold is lost. Do not come. Tell them she was the best of us. — W."*

**Exit:** the keep steps (C1), by the court or by the Armory's secret door.
