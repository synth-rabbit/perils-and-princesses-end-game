The approach: a frozen causeway up to a wall of ice and thorn, and the gatehouse behind it. **Foundry:** module scene **Castle Approach** as backdrop, or the floor plan. About 60–75 minutes of play.

## A1. The Muster at the Treeline

> *The last pines give out below a ridge of bare rock, and there the whole of your last year is waiting for you. A badger in a waistcoat is arguing with a goat about crackers. A bear is letting two cubs climb a troll. Someone has pitched a striped circus tent. And under a banner with a silver rose on it, Daisy of Silverspire is counting heads and trying not to look as relieved as she is.*

No danger. This is where the party picks its **[[Ally_Calls]]** (two each) and chooses a way in.

**Daisy:** "The Society has forty princesses on this mountain and not one of us can get through that wall. That's your job. Ours is to make sure nothing comes down this road behind you."

**Aurelius**, quietly, to whoever asks why he looks at the castle that way: "I knew this place when its windows were lit."

- **Front gate:** A2 → A3 → A4. Loud, heroic, and what Amy and Aurelius need to do anyway.
- **Bannik's tunnel:** to the Cistern (D1). Quiet. Skips the gatehouse and the Outer Ward, and with them Hilde and Aethelgard, unless the party comes back for them.
- **Puca's catapult:** to the Raven Tower (B7).

Whatever the party chooses, **Amy and Aurelius must still break the Barrier at A3** for the allies to hold the road. If the party goes under or over, they hear it happen from inside (+0 Cold; the Queen expected it).

## A2. The Frozen Causeway

> *A spine of rock a wagon wide, glazed with ice, a long fall either side. The wind comes down it like something poured. Halfway along, two dead trees lean over the path with their roots in nothing.*

- **Ice sheets:** crossing at a walk is safe. Running, fighting or riding calls for **GRACE**; a failure is a slide to the edge: d6, and hanging on until helped up (an Action for a friend). Natural Climber gives Filly advantage. Vega can Shape grit across the ice: no tests for anyone.
- **The two trees** are [[Awakened_Tree|Awakened Trees]]. They let the party reach the middle, then close in from both ends.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Awakened_Tree]] ×2 | 8 | 8 | 2 | Twice: Bash (d6) | Axes, hatchets, fire ignore Armor. 1 GD Tangle. Wounded = Rootbound Curse |

**Vega** can speak to trees. These two were ents of the low forest, marched up here and frozen angry. If he asks them what they want, the answer is *"to go down where it is green"*: a **GRACE** test (his manners; advantage if he mentions the two ents he woke fifty years early) and they simply walk off down the mountain. That is a win and costs no dice.

## A3. The Barrier of Thorns

> *It is not a wall anyone built. Ice, three storeys of it, blue-black and grown through with thorns as thick as your arm, each one frozen mid-reach. Your pins have gone cold against your collars. Behind you, Amy takes a breath, takes Scamp off her shoulder, and hands him to you. "Hold him, please. I've never actually done this on purpose."*

**The set piece.** Amy lays her hands on the ice; Aurelius lowers his horn beside them. The wall begins to sing and crack. It takes **three rounds**, and the Barrier defends itself.

| Round | The Barrier sends | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 [[Vine_Crawler|castle vine crawlers]] (HP 2, SAVE 6, Armor 0, Vine Whip d4) | They go for Amy. |
| 2 | 6 more, and 3 [[Frostbind_Wisp|Frostbind Wisps]] | Wisps go for Aurelius: he is the warmest thing here. |
| 3 | A fist of ice the size of a cart swings down | Everyone Nearby to the wall tests GRACE or takes 2d6. A hero may take it for Amy. |

- Amy has **6 HP**. If she is Wounded the count restarts. If Aurelius is struck by two wisps in a round he staggers and the round does not count.
- **Ally Calls shine here**: Mama Bear guards Amy for a round; the pixies; the wolf pack.
- **Tawny's Blessing** (immune to corruption) died with Tawny in session 12. [[Major_Ursa|Major Ursa]] has no such protection.

**When it breaks:**
> *The crack runs top to bottom with a sound like a bell. For a moment the whole wall is lit from inside, rose-gold, and you can see what it was grown over: a gate, and above the gate, carved in stone and half eaten by ice, a rose. The same rose that is pinned to your collar.*

Let that sit. Somebody will ask. **Daisy does not know.** Nobody here does.

## A4. The Gatehouse

> *Past the broken ice, a gatehouse with its portcullis down and a bar across the inside. Something large is sitting against the bar, as if it has been holding the door shut for a long time. And in front of it, a woman made of blue light stands with a shield on her arm, saying the same thing over and over to no one: "Not yet. Not until they come back. Not yet."*

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Thornheart_Construct]] | 12 | 9 | 3 | Twice: Bramble Fist (d8) | Acts last. Silver and radiant ignore Armor. Called shot at the heart: disadvantage, double damage |
| [[Echo_of_a_Luminary]]: **Hilde the Shieldmaiden** | 10 | 14 | 0 | Grief Wail (d6 Nearby, RESOLVE for half) | Immune to ordinary weapons. Can be freed |

The Construct attacks anyone who touches the gate. **Hilde does not attack unless attacked**, but each round the fight goes on beside her she wails once from sheer distress.

**Freeing Hilde:** show the pin, and tell her the gate can open now, because someone has come. **RESOLVE** test, with advantage if a hero is at that moment standing between her and a friend. On a success:

> *She looks at the pin. Then at you. Then, for the first time in a very long time, at the gate. "Oh," says Hilde the Shieldmaiden. "You're late." And she lifts the bar herself.*

The Construct, with nothing to guard, loses its Armor for the rest of the fight. Hilde gives **Hilde's Buckler** and her whisper: *"She never once asked us to leave. We left."* Cold −1.

**⭐ Vega:** Vein-Breaker was made for this. Shatter Vein strips the Construct's Armor a point at a time, and it is his own element under the briars.

**Loot:** in the guardroom, a brazier (warmth, not a warm room), 20 SP, and a duty roster in a hand the party will see again in the journal. The last entry assigns the night watch to *"Hilde, again, because she won't let anyone else."*

**Exit:** the gate opens onto the Court (B1).
