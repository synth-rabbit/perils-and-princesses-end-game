# Staff of the Sunken Sanctum

![[items/assets/staff_sunken_sanctum.png]]

> *"Carved from ancient silverwood and crowned with a sphere of living water, this sacred fey staff hums with the soothing purity of subterranean springs."*

* **Type:** Magic Quarterstaff (d6 Bludgeoning damage) / Spellcasting Focus
* **Tailored For:** Princess Marigold

---

## Magical Properties

* **Sanctuary Aegis:**  
  Whenever Marigold heals an ally or spends a Heart Die, a protective water bubble forms around the target, granting them **+1 Armor for 1 round**.

* **Radiant Wave (1/Rest):**  
  Strike the staff against the ground to release a 15-foot pulse of holy fey water. Undead, oozes, and corrupted beasts within the area take **d6 Radiant damage** and must make a **WITS test** or be pushed back 10 feet.

* **Sacred Command Word ("Aethelgard"):**  
  Speaking the ancient command word *"Aethelgard"* carved into the Shrine Keeper's journal instantly triggers **Radiant Wave**, automatically purging shadow corruption and pacifying corrupted guardians without a roll.
