> *The forest paths bend around you, but the pull of the pins stays steady. It leads beneath tangled roots, into a narrow passage and cold darkness. Ahead, a squirrel's alarmed chatter echoes through an opening. Something enormous whispers, “Scamp? Is that you?”*

**Direction:** Follow the book's cave opening. Do not send them to camp first merely because the old prep did. If they actively choose another destination before meeting Amy, use the runbook and start the hunt when exploration begins; retain the cave scene for their arrival.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Give the journey a change of texture

> *The trees ahead are older and closer together. A path bends left beneath an arch of roots. When you glance back, the arch is on the other side of the trail. Your pins keep pulling steadily onward.*

**GM:** The forest is disorienting, but the compass works. Do not call for repeated WITS checks just to arrive at the opening. This arrival replaces the book's original briefing/travel hook for this campaign.

### At the hidden passage

> *A narrow opening lies beneath the roots. Cold air carries the smell of coins, old smoke and wet stone. From inside comes the sound of someone trying very hard to cry quietly.*

**Ask:** “Who goes first, what light do you carry, and how are you bringing Tawny through?” Resolve the actual dimensions and their plan consistently with the scene. The hidden passage admits the rescuers, but not the dragon body; do not accidentally imply it is a dragon-sized escape route.

If they call out:

> “Please don't come in with swords. I know that sounds exactly like something a dragon would say.”

If they announce Aurelius sent them:

> “He knows? Oh. Oh, thank goodness.”

**GM:** Her hopeful response does not make her suddenly coordinated. Let the first clear sight of the party start the panic scene, unless their actions have already established a convincing alternative; explain any departure from the prepared procedure openly.

### What they can immediately see

A black dragon wearing a necklace; Scamp nearby; scattered treasure; a larger cave mouth whose air shimmers as the dragon approaches. Their pins point directly to her. No discovery roll is needed for those features. Keep the main exit barrier distinct from the little passage they used.
