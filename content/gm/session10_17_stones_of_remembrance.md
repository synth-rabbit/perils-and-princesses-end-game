# Session 10 Scene 17: Sir Dain and the Stones of Remembrance

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Asset checklist:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]

**Foundry scene:** **Stones of Remembrance** — refreshed backdrop.

## Arrival — the battlefield that never ended (15–25 minutes)

> *The forest stops at a circle of scorched earth. Jagged stones stand where trees should be. Between them, voices whisper commands to soldiers long gone. One voice is clearer than the others: “Hold. Just a little longer.”*

**Direction:** Quiet, sorrowful, haunted. The spectral knight in the art is a visual manifestation of Sir Dain's grief, not a compulsory Wight monster. The players can stop, examine, speak or approach.

## The remembered battle

Anyone who comes Nearby the stones tests **WITS**. On failure they enter Dain's battle-memory:

> *The stones are gone. Smoke lies across a trampled field. A dragon's shadow crosses the ground, and every frightened soldier around you looks toward one armored figure who has not lowered his sword.*

Those who pass remain in the clearing and can sense his shame. Tell them their friends seem caught in something they cannot see clearly. Inside, heat and pain feel real, but **the illusion causes no HP damage**. Don't secretly spend their resources because an imaginary attack appeared to hit.

**Dain, to someone inside or addressing him:**

> “Tell the rear ranks to fall back. We will hold this ground.”
>
> “They never came. We failed them.”

His certainty is grief, not historical fact. Someone outside can physically guide or pull affected friends beyond the stones' Nearby reach. Once free they are **Befuddled**. No mandatory combat or spell is needed to move them out.

## Tell him what his stand accomplished

The cottage records establish that Dain and his companions held Sunder long enough for reinforcements to arrive and save thousands. If the PCs have that history—or obtain equally credible evidence—let them tell him. If they have not found it, reveal his need through conversation rather than forcing a lucky guess.

**Dain:** “Did anyone live? Beyond us. Past the smoke. Did anyone live?”

**GM prompt:** “He isn't asking you to beat the dragon. He needs to know whether holding this ground mattered.” Do not claim that Marigold's old southern whisper was Dain.

If they only offer empty reassurance:

> “I want to believe you. Tell me what became of them.”

If they give the truth:

> *The sounds of battle falter. The commander turns, as though hearing a distant horn for the first time.*
>
> “They came?”
>
> *His sword lowers. “Then we held long enough.”*

Truthful resolution releases him, ends the illusion and its Befuddlement, and leaves the Ring of Heart. Do not add another persuasion roll merely to withhold that earned resolution.

## Reward and release

> *For a moment, other figures stand beside him, too faint to count. The wind passes through them. When it is gone, a silver ring rests among the ashes, its heart-shaped stone catching the light.*

**Ring of Heart:** activate to spend any number of your Heart Dice to recover HP as at a Picnic. Then lose **all use of Heart Dice until three days' rest**. Explain this cost before use. Ask who carries it; no automatic assignment.

If they leave before resolution, Dain remains and affected PCs keep the applicable Befuddlement. They can return after the Cottage. A difficult scene is not automatically wasted time, but prolonged unsuccessful attempts may cost time under the announced hunt procedure.

## Hunt procedure at this scene


Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
