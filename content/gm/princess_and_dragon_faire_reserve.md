# The Dragon Faire — Optional Reserve

> **Not on session 10's critical path.** Source: *The Princess and the Dragon*, printed pp. 44–57 (PDF pp. 46–59). The book presents this as a separate introductory adventure. The current campaign is entering the rescue plot; do not insert a carefree festival while Amy is already trapped and the hunt is active.
> **Main adventure:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]

## When to use it

Keep this in reserve. It could be a clearly labeled earlier one-shot with different characters, or an **adapted post-rescue celebration** if the group wants a lighter session before the castle. The latter requires Amy to be safely herself, Tifa available, and the hunt resolved. Adjust Finnian's presence to his actual outcome. A celebration is not established canon or a compulsory detour.

For a later celebration, offer 10–15 minutes of arrival, 40–60 minutes of chosen booths, a break, then 40–60 minutes for the animated statues and consequences. Pick three or four booths the PCs like rather than marching through all ten. Retain the book's normal statistics; the current party is level 3, so there is no reason to inflate the monsters automatically.

## Opening and people

A runaway paper dragon brings the PCs to Amy and [[Tifa]]. Amy has Powerful Friendship at level 1 and [[Scamp]]; Tifa has Sage Intellect at level 1. The small paper dragon is a catching/problem-solving scene, not a compulsory combat.

> *A paper dragon loops over the crowd, its folded wings snapping like bunting. Behind it, a flustered young woman with a crown calls, “It's meant to stay inside the fairground!” From a nearby tent comes a much smaller voice: “I know!”*

Amy invites the PCs to help release the paper dragons at the stage later and gives each a few tokens. **Book leaves the number open; table choice: five each.** Label that allowance as a GM choice. [[Drakenfall_Name_Bank]], [[Faire_Goer]], and [[Booth_Operator]] supply incidental characters. Use [[Faire_Wrinkles]] when a complication would be fun, not a Scorchwoods hunt tick.

Costs not stated in this table are not specified by the source; decide openly if necessary rather than inventing a book rule. Read item prizes from their existing table and entries.

**Spotlights:** Filly and Tawny can follow the paper dragon or sniff stew ingredients; Vega can solve the maze creatively or make a stone dragon artwork; Marigold can welcome a nervous Tifa and help frightened children. These are invitations, not unique keys that bar another PC from helping.

## Main event: three statues wake

Amy and Tifa call the helpers to the stage. Tifa's animation spell hits the three six-foot statues instead of the hundred paper dragons. The crowd scatters. Let the PCs prioritize protecting people, luring statues, or disabling them.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| [[Stone_Dragon]] ×3 | 8 | 8 | 3 | Bite d8 **and** claws d6 | No breath weapon; can glide, cannot fly; cannot leave the fairground. |

| Statue | Behavior / usable lure |
|---|---|
| Avaricious, green eyes | Wants shiny things; heads toward the Hoard. Prizes are endangered there; WITS to salvage a desired item if the booth is overrun. |
| Voracious, orange eyes | Wants food, especially meat; heads for the stew. |
| Barbarous, red eyes | Wants a fight; remains at the stage, attacking and exploiting vulnerable targets. |

Keep all three objectives visible. Finnian comes when alerted but clings to his one-handed vow until persuaded that protecting people with both hands is consistent with his honor. Do not require a secret exact phrase.

On victory, Amy celebrates the PCs and Tifa gives her journal. **Tifa's Journal:** Spend Time studying it to learn to cast [[Animate]] with the reader's own Gift Dice. If the statues overwhelm them, Amy calls the kingdom's knights while Tifa reverses the spell; danger need not become a campaign-ending fight. In a post-rescue adaptation, replace Amy's original offer of a parental introduction with thanks appropriate to relationships actually established in play.

## Optional assets

Existing faire map, statue illustration, Amy/Tifa/Finnian art, and roll tables suffice for a simple version. Three differently colored statue markers, paper-dragon markers, token/ticket counters, and a journal handout are useful extras. No live Foundry setup is implied.
