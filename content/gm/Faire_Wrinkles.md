# Faire Wrinkles

Roll d6

| Roll | Wrinkle |
|---|---|
| 1 | A small, sticky child gloms onto your legs, crying about a lost parent. |
| 2 | That dragon tail you ate was tasty, but it’s not agreeing with you. You look a little green. |
| 3 | One of your competitors loudly accuses you of cheating and challenges you. |
| 4 | A pickpocket bumps into you, attempting to steal something valuable. |
| 5 | It sure is hot today! Be careful to not become Weary. |
| 6 | Magpies swarm the Dragon’s Hoard, trying to steal prizes! The attendant calls for help. |
