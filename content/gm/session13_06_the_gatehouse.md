# Session 13 Scene 6: The Gatehouse

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 25 min · **Foundry:** module scene **Rosehold 0 — The Thornwall** (A4)
> 🔗 Castle note: [[00_The_Thornwall]], A4 · Founders: [[The_Four_Founders]]

The first of the four founders, and the first **whisper**. This is the scene that teaches the players that not everything in the castle is to be fought.

## Entry

> *Past the broken ice, a gatehouse with its portcullis down and a bar across the inside. Something large is sitting against the bar, as if it has been holding the door shut for a long time. And in front of it, a woman made of blue light stands with a shield on her arm, saying the same thing over and over to no one: "Not yet. Not until they come back. Not yet."*

## Who is here

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Thornheart_Construct]] | 12 | 9 | 3 | Twice: Bramble Fist (d8) | Acts last. Silver and radiant ignore Armor. Called shot at the heart: disadvantage, double damage |
| [[Echo_of_a_Luminary]]: **Hilde the Shieldmaiden** | 10 | 14 | 0 | Grief Wail (d6 Nearby, RESOLVE for half) | Immune to ordinary weapons. Can be freed |

- **The Construct** attacks anyone who touches the gate.
- **Hilde does not attack unless attacked.** But each round a fight goes on beside her, she wails once from sheer distress.
- A fight here is loud: **+1 Cold** if it is not over in two rounds.

## Freeing Hilde

Show her the pin, and tell her the gate can open now, because someone has come. **RESOLVE** test, with **advantage** if a hero is at that moment standing between her and a friend.

> *She looks at the pin. Then at you. Then, for the first time in a very long time, at the gate. "Oh," says Hilde the Shieldmaiden. "You're late." And she lifts the bar herself.*

- The Construct, with nothing to guard, **loses its Armor** for the rest of the fight.
- Hilde gives **Hilde's Buckler** (Shield, Armor +1; once per Rest, as a Reaction, take a hit meant for a friend At Hand, and your Armor counts double against it).
- Her **whisper**: *"She never once asked us to leave. We left."*
- **Cold −1.** Record on [[rosehold_tracker]]: Hilde freed, whisper heard, buckler held by whom. **Each freed Echo also removes one Vow Pillar from the throne room.** You do not need to tell the players that yet.

**If the players destroy her instead:** she can be destroyed (HP 10, immune to ordinary weapons; silver and radiant work). No buckler, no whisper, no Cold change. Do not punish it; just note it.

**If someone asks her who "she" is:** Hilde looks up at the keep and does not answer. *"Not yet."*

## Spotlights

- ⭐ **Vega:** **Vein-Breaker** was made for this. Shatter Vein strips the Construct's Armor a point at a time, and it is his own element under the briars. He cracked a dragon open last week; this is easier.
- ⭐ **Marigold:** standing between Hilde and a friend gives the RESOLVE test advantage. Marigold's bubble on whoever does it makes that a lot less frightening. She is also the one who knows what it is to stand guard over someone (she waited with Amy in the cave).
- ⭐ **Filly:** the Construct is briars, not animal; she cannot talk to it. But Major can sit on it. A called shot at the heart while Major holds it down is at disadvantage only once.

## Loot

In the guardroom:
- A **brazier**: warmth, but **not** a warm room (no Rest here).
- **20 SP** (nobody is counting any more; mention it for the joke).
- A **duty roster** in a hand the party will see again in the journal. The last entry assigns the night watch to *"Hilde, again, because she won't let anyone else."*

## Exit

The gate opens onto **the Court of Frozen Fountains** (B1). If there is time, continue with [[session13_07_the_court_of_frozen_fountains|the optional continuation]]. If not, **end here**, with the gate open and the court beyond it white and silent.

> *The gate swings in. Beyond it, a wide yard under a hard white sky, and in it, people running toward the keep who have not moved in forty years.*
