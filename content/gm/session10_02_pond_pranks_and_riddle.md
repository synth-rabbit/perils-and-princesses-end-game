# Session 10 Scene 2: Pond Pranks and Riddle

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 15 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** your existing world scene **Pristine Pond** — already created, confirmed by the GM; it is not packaged in module 0.5.0. Do not use the book's **Enchanted Pool** as this location.

**Location reference (optional):** [[11_A_Pristine_Pond]]. The riddle and encounter setup are reproduced below for play.

> *Blue butterflies drift over a pond clear enough to show every pebble. Somewhere in the reeds, someone giggles. Then Filly's bootlaces pull together by themselves. Tawny looks from the knots to you with the expression of a creature who has wisely never worn shoes.*

**Direction:** One prank is enough if the table is eager; use all three if they enjoy it. Choose their actual clothing/gear, not an assumed tiara or a replacement hatchet.

- **Bootlaces:** WITS with advantage notices pixie traces. Failure gives a harmless stumble, not surprise damage.
- **Borrowed voices:** Vega sounds like Marigold, Marigold like Vega, Filly like Tawny. Shared laughter ends it; use the existing RESOLVE-with-advantage option if they resist by willpower.
- **Floating headwear:** GRACE with advantage to retrieve or WITS with advantage to spot the pixies. Let an inventive method work.

Joining the fun or showing Silver Rose Pins brings **[[Thistledown]]** into view. Her voice is delighted, then theatrically solemn: “Friends of the forest must still face our very difficult riddle.”

Read the riddle in this note below. Accept **moonlight, moonbeam, starlight, reflected light on water**. If needed, WITS with advantage or the hint “you can see it, but not hold it.” No stalled adventure because nobody guesses the intended wording.

**Reward:** one Dewdrop Elixir of Invisibility for the group; agree who carries it. Invisible for 10 minutes, ending on an attack or spell. It is not permanent invisibility or an automatic undetectable theft.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Give the pixies a little stage time

**Thistledown — grand ceremony from a very small person:**

> “Welcome, enormously tall visitors! We apologize for the laces. They were looking lonely.”

If someone is annoyed:

> “Oh. Not that sort of funny? We can undo it. There. No harm meant.”

**GM:** End the prank when it stops being fun. No character must laugh or surrender an item to earn access to the adventure. Silver Rose Pins or a friendly introduction are enough to begin talking.

If they ask about the forest:

> “The water is still clean. We have kept it clean. But the roots outside the clearing have begun to move again.”

She glances at the northern treeline, then visibly brightens to recover her host's composure.

### Read the riddle here

> *Thistledown lands on a reed and lifts both hands. Every pixie becomes solemn at once. One is solemn while hanging upside down.*
>
> “I am born in the dark of night,  
> I dance upon the water bright.  
> Touch me, and I melt away,  
> I hide from sun, but love the May.  
> What am I?”

**Accept:** moonlight, moonbeam, starlight, reflected light on water, or a comparably sound explanation. A correct answer needs no roll. If stuck, offer WITS with advantage and the hint “You can see me on the pond, but cannot scoop me into a cup.” Let the pixies enjoy a clever alternative rather than litigating the poem.

**On success:**

> “Yes! Exactly! We shall pretend it took you considerably longer. It makes our riddle sound better.”

> *Two pixies carry forward a vial between them. The liquid inside keeps disappearing whenever you look straight at it.*

Give **one** Dewdrop Elixir: invisibility for ten minutes, ending with an attack or spell. Ask who takes it. If the party declines the riddle, save the prize; the defense still happens.

### Turn the mood

> *A third pixie comes racing between the trees. She misses her landing on the reed and catches herself with both hands. “Thistledown.” The leader stops smiling. Beyond the clearing, something drags a long furrow through the leaves.*

Move to the defense before adding more pranks.
