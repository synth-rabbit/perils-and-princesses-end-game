# Session 10 Scene 8: Dragon Panic

## Run the scene

> *The dragon scrambles backward. A claw skids across the treasure, a glass orb tumbles, and Amy gasps, “Oh no—”*

**GM map confirmed:** use your existing Dragon’s Cave map. No replacement map is needed. The module scene named below is a reference/fallback.

**Foundry scene:** **Dragon Cave Lair**. Use Dragon Amy and Scamp; mark the barrier and safe space clearly before resolving hazards.

Amy turns, startled. Show the compass pointing to her, and run up to three panic rounds:

1. **Broken orb vision:** everyone tests RESOLVE. With three PCs, two failures mean initial Knight Tracker **3**, otherwise **2**.
2. **Falling stalactites:** GRACE or d4 damage.
3. **Accidental fire sneeze:** those in the path test GRACE or d6 damage.

After **each round**, one PC may test **GRACE with disadvantage** to calm her. Success stops remaining rounds; otherwise she calms after three. Do not make her deliberately attack friendly rescuers or use her full breath weapon instead of the panic hazard.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Run the panic beat by beat

**Round 1 — the orb:**

> *Her tail sweeps across the hoard. A cloudy glass sphere strikes the stone and bursts. Smoke unfolds into a black-armored knight driving a sword into an oozing heart. He lifts his helm. Beneath it is the face of an old man.*

**GM:** It is Ulrik's face; name him only if the characters can identify him. Each PC tests RESOLVE. If at least half fail (two of three), mark the future hunt start at 3; otherwise 2. The earlier omen does not add another point.

Amy: “I'm sorry! That was not supposed to happen!” Offer one PC **GRACE with disadvantage** to calm her. On success, stop; she does not have to cause all three hazards.

**Round 2 — the ceiling, if still panicking:**

> *Amy jerks upward. Her shoulder strikes a hanging tooth of stone. A sharp crack passes overhead, and white fragments begin to fall.*

GRACE or d4 damage. Amy: “Stay still! No—move! I don't know!” Offer the next calming attempt by one PC.

**Round 3 — the sneeze, if needed:**

> *Dust catches in the dragon's nose. Her eyes water. Scamp darts aside. “Oh, please, not—” A sneeze throws a sheet of flame across the stone.*

Those in its path test GRACE or take d6 damage. This is not her regular breath attack. She calms after this round even if nobody succeeds on the last calming attempt.

### Give kindness something to answer

When a PC reassures her, repeat their character's name in her reply:

> “You aren't afraid of me? You can be a little afraid. I'm a little afraid of me.”

If Filly speaks to Scamp:

> “Her. That's her. Same person. Very wrong fur.”

If someone strikes her, she recoils and apologizes; she does not turn into a hostile boss. Resolve self-defense, retreat or collateral risk from the actual action. Do not force the full hazard sequence after a genuine resolution.

### When the cave becomes quiet

> *Amy folds her claws beneath her chest, carefully, one at a time. Scamp climbs back to her cheek. “My name is Amy,” she says. “And I think I have made a terrible mistake.”*

Record any real damage and the starting tracker value. Let scene 9 begin with a question rather than another speech.
