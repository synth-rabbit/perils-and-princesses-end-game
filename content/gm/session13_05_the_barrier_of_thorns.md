# Session 13 Scene 5: The Barrier of Thorns

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 30 min · **Foundry:** module scene **Rosehold 0 — The Thornwall** (A3); hidden Barrier-wave tokens are already placed
> 🔗 Castle note: [[00_The_Thornwall]], A3

The set piece. Amy and Aurelius break the wall; the party keeps them alive for three rounds.

## Entry

> *It is not a wall anyone built. Ice, three storeys of it, blue-black and grown through with thorns as thick as your arm, each one frozen mid-reach. Your pins have gone cold against your collars. Behind you, Amy takes a breath, takes Scamp off her shoulder, and hands him to you. "Hold him, please. I've never actually done this on purpose."*

Ask who takes Scamp. Whoever does has a squirrel in their collar for the rest of the fight.

> *Amy puts both hands on the ice. Aurelius lowers his horn beside her. For a moment nothing happens. Then the wall begins, very quietly, to sing.*

## Three rounds

| Round | The Barrier sends | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 castle vine crawlers | They go for **Amy**. |
| 2 | 6 more crawlers, and 3 Frostbind Wisps | Wisps go for **Aurelius**: he is the warmest thing here. |
| 3 | A fist of ice the size of a cart swings down | Everyone Nearby to the wall tests **GRACE** or takes **2d6**. A hero may take it for Amy. |

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Vine_Crawler|Castle vine crawler]] ×6, then ×6 | 2 | 6 | 0 | Vine Whip (d4) | Morwenna's own strain: weaker than the [[Vine_Crawler]] entry, and everywhere. Fire and slashing tear through them |
| [[Frostbind_Wisp]] ×3 | 3 | 12 | 0 | Frost Touch (d4) + RESOLVE or Weary | Immune to ordinary weapons (silver works) |

- **Amy has 6 HP** (SAVE 12, Armor 0). If she is Wounded, **the count restarts**.
- If **Aurelius** is struck by two wisps in one round, he staggers and **that round does not count**.
- **Ally Calls shine here.** Mama Bear guards Amy for a round; the pixies give everyone advantage on first attacks; the wolf pack flanks with Major Ursa. A call spent here is a call well spent.
- The **Amulet of the Forest Guardian** (2 GD), if someone hangs it on Amy, is exactly what it was for.
- **Tawny's Blessing** (immunity to corruption) was Tawny's and is gone. Major Ursa can be grabbed like anyone else.

## Spotlights

- ⭐ **Filly:** Major Ursa's first real fight. He plants himself in front of Amy without being told. Tell Filly's player that, and let her decide what that means to her. **Roar** works on crawlers and wisps alike.
- ⭐ **Vega:** the wall is ice over rock. Shape the rock at its foot and the crawlers come up through stone that answers to him: they arrive a round late, or not at all. A **Storm** clears a wave.
- ⭐ **Marigold:** the **Staff of the Sunken Sanctum**'s bubble (+1 Armor when she heals or spends a Heart Die) on Amy turns the cart-sized fist from deadly to survivable. Her **Radiant Wave** works on corrupted things, and these are nothing but.

## When it breaks

> *The crack runs top to bottom with a sound like a bell. For a moment the whole wall is lit from inside, rose-gold, and you can see what it was grown over: a gate, and above the gate, carved in stone and half eaten by ice, a rose. The same rose that is pinned to your collar.*

**Let that sit.** Somebody will ask. **Daisy does not know.** Nobody here does. If the players connect it to the **M.** on the paper, do not confirm or deny.

Behind them, the muster pours up the causeway to hold the breach. **Aurelius stays at the breach**: he cannot climb the stairs or pass the thorns. Before they go in he says, to all three: *"Once, when you need it, call me through your pins. I will give you what I have."* (Once in the castle: **all Heart Dice restored to everyone**.)

**Amy:** ask the players whether she comes inside (HP 6, SAVE 12, brave, useless in a fight, and the best tool in the throne room) or waits at the breach with Aurelius. Record it on [[rosehold_tracker]].

## Exit

Through the breach to the gatehouse: [[session13_06_the_gatehouse|The Gatehouse]].
