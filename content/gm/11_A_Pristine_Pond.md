# 11. A Pristine Pond (The Pixies' Last Stand)

> **Session Prep Link:** [[session9_prep#3c. Location 11: Pristine Pond — The Pixies' Last Stand|Session 9 Prep — Act I]]

A serene, crystal-clear clearing surrounding a glowing pond southwest of Aurelius's grove. The last pure source of water in the forest, guarded by tiny pixies and under attack by corrupted vine crawlers.

---

## 1. Arrival & Read-Aloud

> *The air grows sweeter as you push toward the pond. The sick purple-black leaves give way to luminous gold. Butterflies dance through shafts of afternoon light. You emerge into an idyllic clearing surrounding a still, crystal-clear pond that glows faintly from within.*  
> *Everything seems quiet and peaceful... perhaps a little too quiet.*

---

## 2. Pixie Mischief & Pranks (Before the Attack)

Before the corruption creeps in, the invisible **Pixie Swarm** tests the newcomers with playful fey pranks!

* **Prank 1 — The Untied Bootlaces / Hidden Hammer:** An invisible pixie stealthily ties Filly's bootlaces together or moves Vega's hammer behind a giant toadstool (**WITS test with advantage** to spot tiny giggling trails of golden pixie dust).
* **Prank 2 — The Voice-Swap Illusion:** Invisible pixies sprinkle voice-dust on the party! When Prince Vega tries to speak, he speaks in Marigold's high princess voice; when Marigold speaks, she rumbles in Vega's deep dwarf voice; when Filly speaks, she barks like Tawny! (**RESOLVE test with advantage** to break the voice spell through genuine shared laughter).
* **Prank 3 — The Floating Tiara:** Invisible pixies lift Marigold's tiara / Filly's hat off her head, making it float 10 feet up in the air surrounded by glowing sparkles! (**GRACE test with advantage** to snatch it back mid-air OR **WITS test with advantage** to spot the tiny glittering wings carrying it).

---

### The Pixie Reveal & The Fey Pond Riddle

Laughing along with the pranks or displaying the party's **Silver Rose Pins** causes dozens of glittering pixies to burst out into visible shimmering light! **Thistledown** (the pixie leader with dandelion-fluff hair) lands right on Filly's shoulder and squeaks:

> *"They're friends of the forest! They wear the Silver Rose! But before we share our sacred pond's nectar, you must solve the Fey Pond Riddle!"*

* **Thistledown's Riddle:**
  > *"I am born in the dark of night,*  
  > *I dance upon the water bright.*  
  > *Touch me, and I melt away,*  
  > *I hide from sun, but love the May.*  
  > *What am I?"*

* **The Answer:** **Moonlight / A Moonbeam / Starlight** *(or "Light reflected on water")*.
* **Riddle Reward — Dewdrop Elixir of Fey Invisibility:**
  * Solving the riddle (**WITS test with advantage — auto-pass if PCs guess moon/starlight**) causes the pixies to cheer and present a crystal vial of shimmering dew:
  * **Dewdrop Elixir of Invisibility** *(Magic Potion)* — Drinker turns completely invisible for 10 minutes (or until making an attack/casting a spell).

## 3. The Sudden Corruption Attack

> *Just as the pixies are celebrating and handing over the elixir, the ground shudders.*  
> *A tiny voice shrieks in terror: "HELP! THEY'RE COMING AGAIN!"*  
> *Dark purple-black tendrils of corrupted vine slither rapidly from the northern treeline toward the pristine water...*

### NPC Interaction: Thistledown & The Pixie Swarm
* **Pixie Leader:** Thistledown (dandelion-fluff hair, glowing wings).
* **Fey Blessing:** Pixies recognize the party's Silver Rose Pins and Fey Blessing instantly.
* **Combat Support:** Pixies cast minor illusions to distract enemies, granting the party **advantage on their first attack each round** near the pond.

| NPC | Role | Save / HP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Thistledown** | Pixie Leader | HP 1, SAVE 18, Armor 0 | Grateful, desperate to save the pond. |
| **Pixies** (Swarm) | Allies | HP 1, SAVE 18, Armor 0 | Grant party advantage on 1st attack/round near pond. |

## 3. Hazards & Spotlights

* **Slippery Banks:** Fighting near the pond's edge requires a **GRACE test with advantage** to avoid slipping in (ruins non-magical paper/food).
* **Vega Spotlight:** Earth magic + Heartstone can raise stone barriers or shatter the ground beneath the Corruption Bloom.
* **Marigold Spotlight:** Can attempt to purify the Corruption Bloom directly with healing magic (**RESOLVE test with disadvantage** — bloom withers without releasing spore cloud).
