The upper keep: the Society's private rooms, and the Queen's lieutenant. **Foundry:** the floor plan. 90–110 minutes.

## E7. The Upper Landing

> *The stair ends in a gallery with three doors and a silence so complete you can hear the frost forming. Ahead, through an arch, something glitters.*

Quiet. Tracks in the frost: one set of heavy boots going to and from the east door (Ulrik), and a great many small wet footprints (mudlings). If the Cold is 6 or more, a patrol of [[Thornsworn_Guard|Thornsworn]] waits here behind an overturned table.

## E1. The Gallery of Mirrors

> *Twelve tall mirrors, six a side, each in a gilt frame furred with frost. As you step in, twelve of you step in with you. Eleven of them move when you do.*

One of the twelve is the [[Mirror_of_Broken_Vows]]. It picks **Filly** first.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Mirror_of_Broken_Vows]] | 6 | 13 | 2 | None. Each round one hero tests WITS or is Befuddled and cannot spend Heart Dice | Harmed only by silver or blunt. Broken by answering the vision with a true memory |

**What each hero sees** (read to that player only, if you can):
- **⭐ Filly:** Tawny, walking away without looking back, and behind her Peacock and Pickles, and the mirror says in Filly's own voice: *"They follow you because you feed them. You were never a real princess and every one of them knows."*
- **Vega:** Filly and Marigold at his graveside, already leaving. *"Third time. We can't keep doing this."*
- **Marigold:** her friends opening the diary she bought back at the Night Market, reading it, and closing it. *"Oh. That's why she ran."*

**The answer is a memory.** The player tells a true thing that happened at this table: Tawny blocking a cave mouth for her; two friends dragging Vega off a bridge; anything. No roll. The mirror cracks top to bottom.
- The Spectacles of Discernment pick out the true mirror immediately, so it can simply be smashed: the silver pitcher is both silver *and* blunt. Filly's pitcher has been waiting eleven sessions for this.
- Smashing the wrong mirror is seven years' bad luck: +1 Cold.

Behind the true mirror's frame: nothing. But the wall it hung on is warm. The Luminaries' Rooms are on the other side.

## E2. The Map Room and Library

> *Two storeys of books, a table the size of a barn door, and on the table a map of the whole realm built up in layers of coloured wax: every road, every river, Willowbrook the size of a thumbnail. Pins with tiny flags stand in it. A girl made of blue light is moving one, a finger's width, and frowning, and moving it back.*

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Echo_of_a_Luminary]]: **Wren the Cartographer** | 10 | 14 | 0 | Grief Wail (d6 Nearby, RESOLVE for half) | Can be freed |

- **The wax map** shows every place the party has been. The flags are Society operations, forty years out of date. One fresh flag stands in the Scorchwoods: a thorn.
- **Freeing Wren:** she mapped every road looking for a cure. Show her the one road she never found: tell her how *you* got here. **WITS** test, advantage if the hero names three friends who helped along the way.

> *She listens to all of it. Then she takes the last flag out of the map and turns it over in her fingers. "There was never a cure to find out there," says Wren. "It was always going to be someone staying. I was the one who left furthest." She gives you the map.*

Gives **Wren's True Map**. Cold −1.

- **⭐ Vega:** the library. Geology, alchemy, the Society's field notes on every kind of stone in the realm, forty years of dust. Among them, **Bannock's cure recipe in a clearer hand**, and a note that bagiennik oil can be substituted with *cistern-ooze, purified*. And one more thing: the deed. Rosehold was held in trust by the five founders "for the instruction of those who come after." If the castle is saved, there is a university-shaped hole in it.
- **Grimoires:** two on a lectern, unwarded: **Back to Bed** and **Knock/Lock**.

## E3. Ulrik's Workshop

> *The door is ajar. Inside, six copper vats stand in a row, each full to the brim with red mud, and in the nearest one something is sitting up. At a bench with his back to you, a hooded man is writing. He does not turn round. "You're later than she said you'd be. I've had time to make more."*

**Magus Ulrik**, whom the party has heard named and never met, and his unfinished work.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Magus_Ulrik]] | 10 | 15 | 0 | Staff (d6) | 3 GD: Animate, Restoration, Disguise, Haste |
| [[Mudling]] ×d6+2 | 3 | 6 | 1 | Fist (d6) | Follow his last order. Vega's Shape unmakes one per die. Water is d6 |

**How he fights:**
- Round 1: **Haste** on himself, and orders the mudlings to *"hold them."*
- Round 2: **Animate** on the vats: they tip, and red mud floods the floor (everyone tests GRACE or is stuck fast; RESOLVE as an Action to pull free). Vega is not stuck. It is earth.
- Round 3 on: **Disguise** as one of the heroes and a step into the melee. The Spectacles, Sniff, or simply asking him something only the real one knows, exposes him.
- At 3 HP or less he surrenders and bargains. He is not loyal. He is a contractor.

**What Ulrik knows, and will trade for his life:**
- Damon is on the Stair of Vows and will fight until destroyed. **Ulrik can order him to stand down**, but only from within a Stone's Throw.
- The Queen "does not want anything. That is what makes her tedious to work for. She wants to be shown to be right."
- The necklaces were his. He is rather proud of them.
- He will not go near the well.

**Loot:** **Ulrik's grimoire**; his correspondence with the Queen (her half is four words long: *"Do it. Tell no one."*); and **Tifa's stolen notebook**, which the party can return to a gnome they have not met yet.

**⭐ Vega:** every mudling in the room is his element. Shape with three dice clears three of them, no SAVE, and he takes no damage from any of them.

## E4. The Luminaries' Rooms (warm room)

> *A short corridor, five doors, and a stove at the end with embers in it that have apparently been glowing for forty years. Each door has a small brass plate. One of them has been scratched blank.*

The only room on this floor the curse never took. **A Rest is possible here** (+2 Cold). No monsters.

Each bedroom is a small still life:
- **Hilde's:** spartan. A whetstone. A list titled *Things Morwenna Is Not Allowed To Do Alone*, forty items long.
- **Aethelgard's:** pressed flowers. A sketch for a staff with a note: *"for whoever keeps the Sanctum after me."*
- **Lirael's:** sheet music. A letter from Silverspire: *"Your niece has your voice. She's a terror."*
- **Wren's:** maps on every surface, including the ceiling.
- **Morwenna's**, the scratched plate: neat, untouched, the bed made. On the desk, **[[Morwennas_Journal|her journal]]**. In the wardrobe, the **Robe of the First Luminary**. On the windowsill, four small silver roses, laid in a row: the pins her friends left behind.

**⭐ Marigold:** on the shelf by the bed is a child's diary, the twin of the one she bought back at the Night Market. Morwenna ran away from home too, long before any of this, and wrote about it in the same kind of book. What it says about why is for the GM and Marigold's player to decide together; ask her, before the session, what was in hers.

## E5. The Winter Conservatory

> *Glass walls, glass roof, and every pane cracked. The roses in here never stopped. They have filled the room wall to wall, white as paper, and in the middle of them something large and pale is breathing.*

A winter strain of the thing the party killed at the pond.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Corruption_Bloom]] (winter) | 10 | 6 | 2 | Toxic Burst (d6) | Fire deals double damage. On death: Nearby test RESOLVE or Woozy. Shatter Vein lowers Armor by 1 |
| [[Vine_Crawler]] ×8 | 2 | 6 | 0 | Vine Whip (d4) | All die when the Bloom dies |

The party knows this fight: kill the Bloom and the vines go with it; Marigold can purify it directly (**RESOLVE** with disadvantage) to prevent the spore cloud.

**Loot:** one **winter rose** under a bell jar.

**Door to the Solar**, behind a trellis: the Queen's private way to her garden. The painted map shows it as an ordinary passage; make it a secret door in Foundry if you want the Solar reachable only through the mirrors.

## E6. The Queen's Solar

> *A private sitting room. A cold hearth, a single chair turned to face the window, a tray with one cup. Nothing is out of place. Nothing has been touched in years. It is the loneliest room you have ever stood in.*

- **The hand mirror** on the mantel (a Cursed Mirror; see [[Rosehold_Treasures]]).
- Anything the **Mine!** ward in the portrait hall took is on the table here, neatly, with a note: *"You may have this back. I only wanted to see what you valued."*
- **The dumbwaiter** from the kitchen arrives here.
- **The stair up** begins behind a curtain. From above comes the sound of one pair of armoured feet, shifting weight, once every minute, exactly.

**Exit:** up to the Stair of Vows (F1).
