# The Princess and the Dragon — Handouts

**GM master, not a single player handout.** Share only the selected blockquote when its release condition is met. These are campaign-written paraphrases, not facsimiles of book pages. The dispatch and Ulrik correspondence are campaign additions. Answers and release instructions remain private. See [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]] for adjudication.

## A. Daisy's dispatch

**Release:** Nim delivers this after Aurelius's vision, before entering the Scorchwoods. Replaces the unplayed session 9 letter; it does not assert that the original key was destroyed.

> To Filly, Vega, and Marigold,
>
> A dragon hunt has been proclaimed in Drakenshire. Four parties are entering the Scorchwoods: Sir Finnian the Magnificent, Lady Stella the Daring, Sir Raphael the Lucky, and Sir Damon the Unyielding.
>
> Aurelius believes the life at stake is Princess Amy's. Find her before the hunters do. A royal command is not proof that the person giving it can be trusted.
>
> Finnian is accompanying the princess who issued the hunt. Reports also place Magus Ulrik with Damon. We have reason to suspect Ulrik's involvement in the Queen's affairs, but little that would persuade a proud knight without evidence.
>
> Learn what happened. Protect Amy. Find allies where you can. Your Silver Rose Pins will help once Aurelius has blessed them.
>
> Nim has instructions to deliver this into your hands, not into a conveniently nearby hole.
>
> Daisy of Silverspire — PPS

## B. The compass blessing

**Release:** after Aurelius blesses the existing pins. Use [[Silver_Rose_Fairy_Compass]] for rules. This is not three extra pins on top of those they already have.

> Your Silver Rose Pin points toward Princess Amy, whatever shape she wears. When she is in immediate danger, its silver light turns red. In the shifting Scorchwoods, her position gives you a steady point by which to find your way.

## C. The gnome's stick

**Release:** when the stick is recovered at the Boneyard. Do not share the GM answer with it.

> Gnomish Brilliance Grows Year Over Year

**GM answer:** first letters give green, blue, green, yellow, orange, yellow. This is the stump entrance's button sequence. The stick is one route; observing a gnome or finding the pool entrance also works.

## D. Pippin's offer

**Release:** after the party describes or shows the necklaces to [[Pippin_Cobbleton]]. He recognizes the work; this does not establish that he personally made this pair.

> “I can make a key to undo this. But it needs water from the enchanted pool, and my price is fifty silver. Do me a worthwhile favor and we can discuss the silver. The water, I'm afraid, is not negotiable.”

**GM:** both wearers must be Nearby when the key is used; unlock and remove both necklaces. Give that practical instruction when Pippin examines the problem or hands over the key. The original key on Embra remains a valid alternative. Do not turn a favor into an additional compulsory quest after payment.

## E. Cottage discoveries

**Release separately:** when the party examines each corresponding paper at [[09_Cottage]]. These are short paraphrases of the source clues, not four facts automatically known on entering. Read the runbook's cottage card for the distinction between evidence and an unreliable claim.

### E1. A note on a dragon's treasure

> Treasure stolen from a dragon's hoard carries a curse. What the dragon willingly gives is another matter: a gift may leave the hoard without bringing its curse upon the recipient.

### E2. An account of Sir Dain

> Sir Dain held against Sunder the Dragon. His stand bought the time the reinforcements needed. Thousands lived because he did not give way.

**GM:** this is the truth that can lay Dain's spirit to rest at the stones. His encounter is not automatically a fight with the vault's generic Wight.

### E3. An old family account

> Pitch was a mighty dragon, an ancestor of Embra and larger than she is. Though famed for his strength, he was defeated. His immense skeleton now shelters the wolves of the Scorchwoods.

### E4. An archivist's assertion

> Stories claim that Prince Gemini befriended the dragon Cobalt and left his kingdom to share their adventures. Surely this is a tale for children. It seems far more likely that Cobalt ate him.

**GM:** E4 is the writer's prejudice, **not the truth**. Hesperia can explain the friendship. Do not have the narrator certify this claim, or use it as evidence that every dragon is evil.

## F. Ulrik's correspondence — campaign addition

**Release:** if the party obtains and searches Ulrik's actual papers. This is optional evidence placed with him, not another item required to solve the book. If he escapes with them, the party has not learned their contents. It does not reveal the PPS founder secret.

> The necklaces have reached Embra. Her desire for a human life should do the rest.
>
> Damon must remain close enough to receive my commands. Once the hunt reaches the cave, the Queen's purpose takes precedence over Drakenshire's honors.
>
> Amy must not stand beside Aurelius at the thorns.
>
> — U.

**GM:** the command-distance mechanic is in [[Magus_Ulrik]] and the runbook. This note provides a lead, not the complete technical answer. The campaign connection makes Ulrik the supplier and manipulator; Embra still chose the book's deception. Her possession of the original key is unchanged.
