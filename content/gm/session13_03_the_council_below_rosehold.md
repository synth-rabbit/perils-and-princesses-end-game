# Session 13 Scene 3: The Council Below Rosehold

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 35 min · **Foundry:** module scene **Castle Approach** (or **Rosehold 0 — The Thornwall**, at the muster plateau)
> 🔗 Castle note: [[00_The_Thornwall]], A1 · Ally rules: [[Ally_Calls]]

The GM's "council of everyone you met along the way" and the "Braveheart moment." No danger. Three jobs: **the reunion**, **the choices** (Ally Calls, special items, the way in), and **the speech**.

## The muster

> *There, below the wall, the whole of your last year is waiting for you. A badger in a waistcoat is arguing with a goat about crackers. A troll is letting two bear cubs climb him. Someone has pitched a striped circus tent. Forty women in travelling cloaks, each with a silver rose at her collar, stop what they are doing and turn. And under a banner with a silver rose on it, Daisy of Silverspire is counting heads and trying not to look as relieved as she is.*

**Daisy of Silverspire** meets them.

> **Daisy:** "You brought the princess. You brought the *unicorn*. I brought forty operatives and a strongly worded letter." *She looks up at the wall.* "The Society has been trying to find this place for forty years. Nobody remembers why we started looking."

**Promotion:** she raises all three from Initiate to **Field Operative**, on her own authority, in front of everyone. They may now give orders to the Society's people on this mountain. "The pins you have will do. They've been pointing the right way longer than mine has."

If the players show her the paper signed **M.**, Daisy reads it and goes pale for reasons she cannot name. *"ROSEHOLD. I've heard that word. My great-aunt used to say it in her sleep."* That is all she has.

## Who is here

Let the players spot them. One line each is enough.

- **Nim the Badger:** "You never did call my name. I got tired of waiting."
- **Billy Goat:** "Did you bring crackers."
- **Mama Bear, Pebble and Pit.** Pebble has grown. Mama Bear looks Major Ursa up and down and approves.
- **Thistledown** and a cloud of pixies, already swapping two operatives' voices.
- **Nettie Blackthorn**, with a kettle on. "Old cold magic. I said so. Nobody listens to a hedge witch."
- **Grumbold**, no longer sneezing, with a spare tooth.
- **Bannik**, half out of the ground, singing about a hen.
- **Mari Lwyd**, who bows.
- **Puca**, with a catapult. "Somebody here rolled a twenty last time and I have *never forgotten it*."
- **Pippin Cobbleton**, with an invoice, and a workbench (see "The workshop").
- **Sir Raphael**, now famous, signing autographs he did not ask to sign; **Lady Michelle** and **Sir Parker** sigh at him; the mule says, "It's a living."
- **Sir Finnian**, Robin and the Drakenshire guards, rehearsing a speech of their own. Finnian bows to Amy, then to the party, slightly lower.
- **The crystal wolves.** The pups come straight to Filly, look for Tawny, and do not find her. ⭐ **Filly:** what does she tell them? They will ride Major if he lets them. He lets them.
- **Morgana Twinkleberry**, Fairy Godmother, on a camp stool, knitting.

## The workshop

The GM asked the players to design **special items** after session 12: the party is rich beyond counting, and "this is not Lord of the Rings, so you're actually going to have to win the battles." This is where those items are handed over.

- **Pippin** and **Grumbold** have set up shop in the circus tent. Whatever each player sent in and the GM approved is finished here, with a line from whoever made it. Pippin's line for anything: *"It works first time with fifty silver. I'm told you're good for it now."*
- **Filly wanted teleportation.** If the GM approved something, Pippin made it; if not, **Nim's** call (*Poof!*, whisks the party to any room already visited) is the in-world answer, and Nim is here.
- **Tawny's silver claws and teeth**, if Filly wants them made into something, are Grumbold's job: a pendant, a knuckle-weapon, a collar for Major. He does it without being asked to hurry and without asking for payment.
- **Dragon-scale armour:** the GM allowed one +3 Armor item each, made from Embra. Pippin has them ready if they were not settled at the cave.

**Direction:** keep this to five minutes total. Items not sent in can be described now and approved on the spot, or deferred to a Spend Time inside.

## The choices

1. **Ally Calls.** Each PC picks **two** allies to carry inside as calls. **Amy and Aurelius come regardless.** Read the list aloud or paste the player-safe [[Ally_Calls]] table into chat. Record picks on [[rosehold_tracker]]. Changes since session 12: **Sir Finnian is available** (Amy convinced him); the **crystal wolves** now flank with **Major Ursa**; **Sir Raphael's** ballad is now *about the dragon*.
2. **The way in.** Three options, from [[00_The_Thornwall]]:
   - **The front gate:** the causeway, the Barrier, the gatehouse (scenes 4–6). Loud, heroic, and what Amy and Aurelius must do anyway.
   - **Bannik's tunnel:** under the wall to the Cistern (D1). Quiet; skips the gatehouse and the Outer Ward, and with them the first two Echoes unless the party comes back. *"Not one of mine,"* he will say of what lives in the cistern.
   - **Puca's catapult:** over the wall onto the Raven Tower (B7). GRACE test each; a failure is d6 and a very public landing (+1 Cold). Filly has advantage: she has done this before.
   Whatever they choose, **Amy and Aurelius must still break the Barrier** so the allies can hold the road. If the party goes under or over, see "If they don't take the gate" below.

## The speech

⭐ **The Braveheart moment.** Before anyone moves, ask each player for **one line** their character says to the muster. No roll. Then Amy, who has never given a speech in her life:

> **Amy:** "I don't know her. I don't know why she did this. I know she sent a dragon to sit in my body so she could watch me *try*." *She looks at the paper in Finnian's hand.* "So let's let her watch."

> *Forty princesses, a troll, a goat, four knights, a bear and two cubs, a badger in a waistcoat and a circus all make a noise at once. It is not a cheer. It is bigger than a cheer. Up on the wall, the thorns creak.*

⭐ **Filly:** every animal here came because of her. If her player has ever worried aloud that Filly is not a real princess, Morgana looks up from her knitting at exactly that moment and says nothing at all.

## If they don't take the gate

The allies go up the causeway with Amy and Aurelius while the party goes under or over. The party hears the Barrier break from inside (+0 Cold; the Queen expected it): a bell-like crack, and rose-gold light through every window. Then run the arrival:

- **Bannik's tunnel → D1, the Cistern:** *"A vaulted hall half full of black water, perfectly still. Stone walkways run round the edge. Something has been dissolving the mortar: the water has a skin on it, and the skin is moving."* A [[Black_Ooze]] (HP 10, SAVE 4, Armor 0, Corrosive Touch d6; corrodes wood and metal, **not silver**; fire kills; a Corruption Cure poured in kills it outright). Walkways are slick: fighting on them is GRACE or into the water. End the session here if time is short.
- **Puca's catapult → B7, the Raven Tower:** 2d4+2 [[Glass_Raven|Glass Ravens]] (HP 1, SAVE 8, Armor 0, Peck d4; any that escape +1 Cold; shattering one is GRACE or 1 damage) and a ballista aimed at the causeway. Lady Michelle's song, if called, shatters every raven in earshot. In the roost: a brass Courier Raven with an unsent message: *"To any Initiate in the field: Rosehold is lost. Do not come. Tell them she was the best of us. — W."* End the session here if time is short.

## Exit

The party starts up the causeway: [[session13_04_the_frozen_causeway|The Frozen Causeway]]. **Take the break here** if it has not been taken.
