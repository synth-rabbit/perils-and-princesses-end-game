## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** use the new **Stump Entrance** backdrop, [[locations/The_Scorchwoods/assets/stump_entrance_scene.png]]. Included in module 0.6.0. 🔗 [[03_Stump]]

> *A broad stump rises between four little stone warriors. Red, orange, yellow, green and blue gleam inside five knotholes. Tiny footprints vanish at the bark.*

**Direction:** Give the players the visible colors and let them investigate. The stick's initials yield **green, blue, green, yellow, orange, yellow**. If they lack the stick, they can **Spend Time** concealed to watch a gnome enter. Explain the tracker cost before they wait. Do not require a roll to recognize the exact sequence once they have watched it.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Present the puzzle clearly

Tell players all five available colors when they inspect the knotholes. Repeat the stick's phrase if they recovered it. The answer is **green, blue, green, yellow, orange, yellow**; do not add a sixth button or require a simultaneous press.

**Correct sequence:**

> *A click answers each touch. On the last yellow, the grain of the wood parts along a seam you hadn't noticed. Warm lamplight spills through a doorway at your knees.*

**If they choose to watch:** state the cost first—one Spend Time adds 1 to the hunt and requires a d4 Wrinkle check. On 1, resolve a Wrinkle and add its point. When the gnome arrives, describe the actual sequence, not another hidden observation puzzle.

> *A small figure in a pointed cap checks both directions, touches the knotholes with practiced speed, and disappears through the stump carrying a parcel larger than his head.*

**Wrong sequence:**

> *The colored lights go out. Four pairs of crystal eyes brighten. Stone blades scrape free of stone scabbards.*

Use the four statue profiles above. They guard this entrance; do not invent a forest-wide pursuit. If the party flees, resolve their retreat rather than declaring them trapped by the puzzle.

### The guard after the statues fall

> *A door flies open. A gnome in a crooked helmet stares from one fallen statue to another. “Those were hand-carved!”*

**If they apologize and explain Amy's problem:**

> “A princess in a dragon? That sounds like Pippin's sort of impossible. I'm not saying you can come in. I'm saying you can explain why you should.”

GRACE can persuade the initially hostile guard to admit them. Failure means he refuses for now; let them offer new evidence, make amends, or seek the other entrance. Do not invent a compulsory fine.

**If admitted:**

> “Crawl. Mind your weapons. And if that cat scratches my door, you are explaining it to the carpenter.”

Agree Tawny's route or safe waiting place. No automatic off-screen attack while she waits. Transition to Pippin's village scene when they enter.

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
