# Session 10 Scene 6: Nightmare and Dispatch

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 10 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** keep the pond/grove backdrop; no scene change is needed. Narrate the nightmare, then reveal **A. Daisy's dispatch** and **B. The compass blessing** from the handout pack at their cues.

This is a **transition night's sleep**, before the Knight Tracker begins. If the party prefers immediate departure, frame the same omen as a brief waking vision from Aurelius rather than forcing an overnight stop.

> *In the dream, a black-armored knight walks toward the cave. A thorn-shaped mark stains his breastplate. Behind him, far away, a woman waits on a frozen throne. “Bring me her fire.” You wake to three matching Silver Rose Pins, warm against your skin.*

**Direction:** Omen, not a prediction that removes player agency. No damage, forced trauma, or tracker increase. The book's later orb vision remains its own event with its own roll.

**[[Nim_the_Badger]]** arrives with Daisy's dispatch: “Capital letters urgent. The kind where she does the scary voice.” Give **Handout A** from [[princess_and_dragon_handouts]]. Daisy provides names and a warning, not certainty about every knight or a guaranteed alliance. Preserve the PPS founder secret.

Aurelius blesses the existing pins as [[Silver_Rose_Fairy_Compass|Fairy Compasses]]: always point to Amy regardless of body; turn red in immediate danger. Explain that this fixed reference lets the party navigate where knights become lost. No repeated navigation rolls just to enter each keyed location.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Let the nightmare breathe

After the dream passage above, pause. Ask, “What detail stays with your character when the dream breaks?” Their answers add atmosphere, not compulsory wounds, prophecy or tracker increases. If leaving immediately, make this a waking vision in Aurelius's light.

### Nim's entrance

> *A determined snuffling comes from the roots. Nim backs into the clearing dragging a message case, notices his audience, and turns it into a dignified arrival with considerable effort.*

**Nim:**

> “Hand delivery. Your hands, specifically. Daisy was very clear about that.”

If asked how much he knows:

> “I carry the letters. Reading them would make me a spy, and the paperwork for that is dreadful.”

### Read Daisy's dispatch

> To Filly, Vega, and Marigold,
>
> A dragon hunt has been proclaimed in Drakenshire. Four parties are entering the Scorchwoods: Sir Finnian the Magnificent, Lady Stella the Daring, Sir Raphael the Lucky, and Sir Damon the Unyielding.
>
> Aurelius believes the life at stake is Princess Amy's. Find her before the hunters do. A royal command is not proof that the person giving it can be trusted.
>
> Finnian is accompanying the princess who issued the hunt. Reports also place Magus Ulrik with Damon. We have reason to suspect Ulrik's involvement in the Queen's affairs, but little that would persuade a proud knight without evidence.
>
> Learn what happened. Protect Amy. Find allies where you can. Your Silver Rose Pins will help once Aurelius has blessed them.
>
> Nim has instructions to deliver this into your hands, not into a conveniently nearby hole.
>
> Daisy of Silverspire — PPS

**GM:** This is the text of Handout A. It neither guarantees an alliance nor reveals the key's location or the PPS founder secret.

### Bless the existing pins

> *Aurelius touches his horn to each silver rose. Light gathers between its petals. Each pin pulls gently in the same direction, like a compass finding north.*

**Aurelius:**

> “They will point to Amy, whatever shape she wears. Red means danger close around her. Follow them, and do not mistake a face for the person beneath it.”

Tell players explicitly that the pins provide a fixed reference for navigation in the shifting forest. No routine navigation test is required for every move. These are their existing pins, not three additional rewards. Depart toward the hidden approach once they are ready.
