# Session 12 Scene 6: Knighthoods and Level Four

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 20 min · **Foundry:** **Scorchwoods — The Road Beyond** (module), or the cave exterior

Breathing room. Amy is herself; the party is level 4; the Scorchwoods are done.

## Amy, restored

> *She has washed her face in the pool and borrowed Marigold's comb. She is twenty-two, she is shorter than you expected after a week of talking to a dragon, and she will not stop touching her own hair.*

**She asks**, first, about her household: every guard at the camp, by name. Answer honestly. Then:

> **Amy:** "Somebody did this to me on purpose. Not Embra — Embra just wanted breakfast. Somebody *sent* those necklaces." *She reads the note from the black tent twice.* "She wants to watch me try." *Her chin comes up.* "She did this because she's afraid of me? Of my magic? Good. Let's give her something to be afraid of."

**Honorary knighthoods.** Amy has no sword, so she uses Vega's pickaxe, carefully. *Dame Filly, Sir Vega, Dame Marigold of Drakenshire.* Tawny is knighted separately and at length.

**Reward:** 100 SP each from the hunt's pay chest, and the freedom of Drakenshire. This is the first SP award since session 8; say so.

## Level 4 reference

Applied in scene 5; settle the details here. Core book p. 31.

| Gain | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gift Dice | 4 (d6) |
| Heart Dice | 4 (d4); **Marigold 5** (Big Heart) |
| HP | +d4 max |
| Virtue Deepens | d20 against RESOLVE, then GRACE, then WITS; a roll *higher* than the Virtue raises it by 1 |
| Cap | Level 4 is the last level. Everything after is items and choices |

**Filly — Wild Form.** *Transform into an animal you've seen before for up to [DICE]×10 minutes. You have [SUM] HP in this form. At 0 HP you revert and are Wounded. Being an animal will usually give you a specific advantage.* Settle her list now: see [[rosehold_runbook]] §5 for the animals seen in play and a suggested ruling on the dragon.

**Vega — Summon.** *Summon a small elemental being with [SUM] HP. Friendly, follows directions, amorphous, immune to its own element; all Virtues equal your WITS. Dissolves after [DICE] hours or at 0 HP. Attacks within a Stone's Throw for d6.* And his Resistance becomes **immunity to earth and stone damage**. Ask him what it looks like, and what it is called.

**Marigold — True Love's Kiss.** *Roll four Gift Dice and bestow true love's kiss: remove one Trauma, wake someone from magical slumber, turn them back from a frog, or end another similar deadly Curse, injury or sickness. Works a single time on any individual.* Tell her player, away from the others if you can, that this will matter exactly once more.

## The Dragonfilch Curse, closed out

Record on each sheet how it ended: returned, gifted, bargained, or the dragon's death. Anyone who still holds an ungifted hoard item with Embra alive is **still stage one**, and carries it into the castle (one last test in the Relic Vault).

## Aurelius arrives

> *There are hoofbeats on a path too narrow for a horse. Aurelius steps out of the pines with frost on his mane that was not there a week ago, and he looks at Amy for a long moment before he speaks.*
>
> **Aurelius:** "The wall on the mountain grew a hand's breadth last night. She knows you are free. Daisy has called the Society. Everyone who owes you three a kindness is already walking north." *He lowers his head so Amy can reach his neck.* "I can carry one. The rest of you had better have friends with horses."

Mari Lwyd's Teeth, if the party still has them: skeletal steeds, "like mice pulling a fairy godmother's carriage."
