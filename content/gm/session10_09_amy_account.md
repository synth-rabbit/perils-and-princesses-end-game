# Session 10 Scene 9: Amy's Account and the First Choice

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 20 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

> *Amy lowers her head until one enormous eye is level with yours. Scamp settles against her cheek. For the first time since you arrived, the cave is quiet enough to hear the water dripping.*

**GM map confirmed:** use your existing Dragon’s Cave map. No replacement map is needed. The module scene named below is a reference/fallback.

**Foundry scene:** remain on **Dragon Cave Lair** while Amy explains. Consult **The Scorchwoods (Overworld)** privately when the players choose a destination.

Use Amy's spoken account below, pausing for player questions. Key facts: Embra used friendship to give her the necklace, used a key to lock the swap, sealed the cave, and ordered the hunt in Amy's body.

Give a choice grounded in what they learn:

- Find the false princess and her **original key**; Finnian is escorting her.
- Investigate gnomish craftsmanship and the nearby altar trail; Pippin can offer a second route.
- Seek help from or delay the hunting parties.

**Recommended session end:** Amy is calm, Scamp has reported the hunt, and the PCs choose their first lead. Fill [[princess_and_dragon_tracker]] with the starting value, Amy's HP, the party's destination, and who is staying with her. Do not roll the next session's events in advance.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Amy tells what happened

**Voice:** an adult trying to be a good host in an impossible situation. Give one passage, let the players interrupt, then continue when appropriate.

> “I wanted to meet a dragon. Not a trophy or a story about a knight. A person. Embra talked to me. She asked what it was like to live in the palace, and I thought she was lonely.”

If they ask about the visits:

> “I came back. Secretly. I told her about dinners and clothes and all the little things I thought were boring. She listened as if they were wonderful.”

Then:

> “About a week ago she gave me a necklace. She had one too. A friendship gift. The moment I put mine on, I was looking at my own face from up here.”
>
> “She took a key out of the treasure and held it between us. After that I couldn't take the necklace off. She shut the cave with magic and left wearing my body.”

Pause. Let their reaction matter. Amy is ashamed of being deceived, not stupid for wanting a friend.

### Inspecting the necklace and the exit

The necklace has gnomish workmanship; WITS or a relevant Talent identifies it. Do not require success to hear Amy's story. A failed examination leaves a route through locals who recognize the craft.

**GM solution, ready here:** the original key on Embra or a replacement can unlock the pair when **both wearers are Nearby**. Then both necklaces must be removed. The cave barrier is independent. Embra can remove it from outside in human form; restored to dragon form inside, she is trapped too but can tell someone outside how to dispel it. Do not make opening the barrier a prerequisite to every possible rescue plan.

Outside the cave, the gnomes' tribute altar holds **100 SP in gems**. Those are not yet part of the hoard and are not cursed. WITS investigation reveals tiny footprints toward the stump. Describe the actual trail if discovered:

> *Among the larger tracks are little heel-marks, no longer than your thumb, leading away beneath the roots.*

### If they take treasure

**Warning:** the cave hoard feels possessive; WITS examination can detect a curse, not all its stages. Taking treasure out triggers Dragonfilch stage 1; each later treasure temptation calls for WITS or advances a stage. Returning stolen treasure cures it. Voluntary gifts from Embra are exempt; Amy's permission alone does not waive another dragon's curse.

1. Reptilian eyes and covetousness.
2. Lose innate abilities; gain wings and a d8 bite.
3. Lose special abilities; gain 2 Armor (maximum 3) and Gift-Dice elemental breath dealing [SUM], GRACE halves.
4. Become a dragon.

**Amy, if asked for treasure:** “It isn't mine. I don't even know what all of it does.” Ordinary wealth need not become an unplanned magic-item handout. The altar gems are a useful, uncursed way to pay a craftsman.

### End with a plan

Ask: “Who stays with Amy? Does Scamp come? Are you following the gnome trail, looking for the knights, or doing something else?” Start the hunt at the recorded 2 or 3 when exploration begins. The pins turning red will mean an immediate threat, not that the PCs automatically failed.

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
