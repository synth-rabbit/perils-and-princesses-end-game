# Rosehold — Castle Runbook

> **Start:** [[session13_prep]] carries the unplayed muster forward from session 12 and runs Level 0. This runbook then stays open for as many sessions as the castle takes.
> **At the table:** [[rosehold_tracker]] · [[Rosehold]] (six level notes) · [[rosehold_asset_list]]
> **Tables:** [[Ally_Calls]] · [[Rosehold_Wrinkles]] · [[Rosehold_Whispers]] · [[Rosehold_Treasures]]
> **Source:** campaign original. The core book gives only the Evil Queen's stat line and two minions; everything else here is built on printed stat lines and the companion module's castle roster.

## 1. The shape of it

Six levels, about thirty keyed rooms, three ways in, and one question at the top. The party arrives at **level 4** with everything it has collected since Willowbrook, and with every friend it has made camped at the bottom of the road.

| Level | Note | What it is for | Play time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | [[00_The_Thornwall]] | Muster, the Barrier set piece, the first Echo | 60–75 min |
| 1 | [[01_The_Outer_Ward]] | Guards, hounds, ravens, the second Echo | 20–100 min |
| 2 | [[02_The_Hall_of_the_Rose]] | **The reveal**: the Society was hers. Third Echo. A warm room | 75–90 min |
| −1 | [[03_The_Undercroft]] | Optional. Prisoners, the vault, the Well | 75–100 min |
| 3 | [[04_The_Galleries]] | The mirror, Ulrik, the journal, fourth Echo. A warm room | 90–110 min |
| 4 | [[05_The_Frozen_Spire]] | Damon, then Morwenna | a whole session |

## 2. Session plan

Session 12 ended the Scorchwoods in the dragon's cave; the muster and Level 0 moved to [[session13_prep]]. The GM expects the castle itself to take **about two sessions** after the council, which is the **two-session** row, shifted: session 13 is the council and Level 0, session 14 Level 1 to Level 2, session 15 Level 3 (E1, E3, E4) and Level 4. The table below is the original plan:

| Plan | Session 13 | Session 14 | Session 15 | Session 16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Four sessions** (everything) | Level 0 + Level 1 | Level 2 + Undercroft | Level 3 | Level 4 + epilogue |
| **Three sessions** (recommended) | Level 0 + Level 1 | Level 2 + either Undercroft or half of Level 3 | Rest of Level 3 + Level 4 | — |
| **Two sessions** (fast) | Level 0, straight through B1 to Level 2 | Level 3 (E1, E3, E4 only) + Level 4 | — | — |

**To shorten:** Bannik's tunnel or Puca's catapult skips most of Level 1. The Undercroft is wholly optional. On Level 3, only E4 (the journal) is needed for the ending.
**To lengthen:** run every Echo as a full scene, let the players explore the Undercroft, and give each Ally Call its moment.

Each castle session should **end in a warm room or at a door**: the Kitchens, the Luminaries' Rooms, the Oath Door, the foot of the Stair of Vows.

## 3. The Cold

One track for the whole castle, **0 to 8**, kept on [[rosehold_tracker]]. It is the Queen's attention and the curse's grip together. **Tell the players it exists** and say the cost before they choose.

| Change | Trigger |
|---:|---|
| +1 | A **Wrinkle** (d4 on every Spend Time; a 1 means roll [[Rosehold_Wrinkles]]) |
| +1 | A loud fight in the open, a forced door, a triggered trap |
| +1 | A glass raven escapes |
| +2 | A **Rest** inside the walls (warm rooms only) |
| −1 | An Echo is **freed** (not destroyed) |
| −1 | The Raven Tower flock is destroyed (once) |
| −2 | The Frostbind Well is purified |

| The Cold | What changes |
|---:|---|
| 0–1 | The castle is asleep. Patrols as written. |
| 2–3 | **She notices.** Patrols double. Morwenna begins to speak ([[Rosehold_Whispers]]) whenever the Cold rises. |
| 4–5 | **She prepares.** Gargoyles wake on the Grand Stair. Two Sentinels join her in the throne room. Wisps appear in any room where a Heart Die is spent. |
| 6–7 | **The Barrier regrows.** The muster outside is cut off and fighting. No further Ally Calls can be made until the Cold drops below 6. Amy, if outside, is in danger. |
| 8 | **She comes down.** Morwenna leaves the throne and meets the party wherever they are, with Damon. The finale happens there, without the throne's protection for her, and without the party's preparation. |

Floor 0. The Cold does not reset between sessions.

## 4. Allies

Everyone the party has helped is at the muster (A1). Rules and the full list: [[Ally_Calls]]. Two picks per PC; **Amy and Aurelius are free**.

- **Amy** comes inside if the party wants her. HP 6, SAVE 12, Armor 0. She is brave, useless in a fight, and the single best tool in the throne room (d8 friendship damage a round). Scamp rides on whoever she trusts most. If the players would rather not escort her, she waits with Aurelius and arrives at the Rose Window on Mari Lwyd, or on the unicorn's light, at the worst possible moment.
- **Aurelius** cannot enter: the stairs, and the thorns. He holds the breach. Once, at any time, any hero may call on him through their pin: **all Heart Dice restored to everyone** (his full-power trait).
- **The Society.** Daisy and about forty princesses hold the road. She is the players' window on the PPS: let her be shaken when the rose over the gate is uncovered, and let her ask them afterwards what was inside.

**Promotions.** At the muster, before the wall, Daisy raises all three PCs from Initiate to **Field Operative**: they may give orders to the Society's people on the mountain. If Rosehold is taken, the surviving Luminaries offer **Knight-Protector**. If Morwenna is saved, there is a fifth chair, and it is hers to give.

## 5. Level 4 at this table

**Applied at the table in session 12.** Kept for reference. From the core book, p. 31, each PC gains: the level 4 Gift ability, **+1 Gift Die (4 GD)**, **+d4 max HP**, **+1 Heart Die**, and Virtue Deepens (roll d20 against each Virtue in order; higher than the Virtue raises it by one). Level 4 is the cap.

| PC | Level 4 ability | Rulings to make before the castle |
|---|---|---|
| **Filly** | **Wild Form:** become an animal she has seen, for [DICE]×10 minutes, with [SUM] HP. At 0 HP she reverts and is Wounded. | Agree the list. Seen in play: giant cicada, jaguar, lioness, bear, billy goat, giant spider, serpent, wolfhound-sized crystal wolf, squirrel, badger, goldfish, mule, unicorn (GM call), **dragon (GM call)**. Suggested: yes to the dragon, at [SUM] HP, with a bite (d12) and no breath; it is the finale. |
| **Vega** | **Summon:** a small earth elemental with [SUM] HP, Virtues equal to his WITS, attacks within a Stone's Throw for d6, lasts [DICE] hours. Also: **immune to earth damage.** | Decide what counts as earth: mudlings and the vats (yes), Damon (recommended yes), falling masonry (yes), ice (no). |
| **Marigold** | **True Love's Kiss:** roll all four Gift Dice; end a deadly Curse, Trauma or enchanted sleep. Once per individual, ever. | It is the rules-as-written key to saving Morwenna. Four dice make a Mishap likely; let it happen and let it be worth it. |

**What level 4 does to the opposition:** a four-die Blast or Storm averages 14 damage (7 on a SAVE), and Smooch calms anything with HP at or under [SUM] with no SAVE. Every printed stat line in the castle falls to one full-power Gift. That is fine for the castle's rank and file. **Morwenna is the exception: by GM ruling she is much harder than the book** (30 HP, two actions a round, unkillable while a Vow Pillar stands; then a 45–65 HP second phase that takes no damage on a passed SAVE). Her numbers and dials are in [[05_The_Frozen_Spire]]. The rest of the castle's difficulty is **attrition** (Gift Dice do not come back without a Rest, and a Rest costs +2 Cold), **things Gifts do not solve** (Echoes, mirrors, doors, the Well), and **the last room**, where the only damage that gets the good ending cannot be rolled on a Gift Die.

## 6. The four whispers and the two endings

The save-her ending needs the party to understand what Morwenna needed. Each freed Echo gives one **whisper**; the journal counts as two.

| Whisper | From |
|---|---|
| "She never once asked us to leave. We left." | Hilde, A4 |
| "The cold is not her. It is a thing that sits where her heart was. Pull it out by the root." | Aethelgard, B4 |
| "She could still hear me. She told me so, the last night. She said it hurt." | Lirael, C3 |
| "There was never a cure to find out there. It was always going to be someone staying." | Wren, E2 |

- **Each freed Echo also removes one Vow Pillar** from the throne room and 5 HP from the second phase. Freeing all four is the difference between a hard fight and a nearly unwinnable one.
- **All four held:** Marigold's True Love's Kiss ends the curse outright. Without Marigold, friendship damage works at double value.
- **Some held:** each one, quoted to her, costs the Frost-Crowned a spell. The ending must be earned on friendship damage.
- **None held:** the fight is a fight. It can still end in mercy if the last blow is a Heart Die and a memory.

Neither ending is the wrong one. The players said in session 11 that they intend to kill a dragon to keep their loot; this table may well choose the sword. Play Aurelius's grief honestly and give them their victory.

## 8. Epilogue menu

Run whichever the table has earned. Ten minutes each, at most.

- **The thaw.** The frozen household in the court wakes. The ice horses shake themselves. Skillet's goblins ask if they still have jobs.
- **The Society.** Daisy climbs the Stair of Vows herself and stands in the great hall for a long time. The fourth line of the oath is cut back into the door.
- **Morwenna, if saved.** Old, powerless, and entirely herself. She does not ask to be forgiven. She asks what happened to each of the four, and the party can tell her. What becomes of her (trial, exile, a chair at the table, a room with the door left open) is the players' decision.
- **Morwenna, if slain.** Aurelius carries her down the mountain himself. The Society buries its founder under her own rose.
- **Filly.** Morgana Twinkleberry, in person, with an opinion about imposter syndrome and a list of every animal Filly has ever rescued, read aloud.
- **Vega.** The deed in the library. Rosehold was held in trust "for the instruction of those who come after." The Royal College of Geological Studies needs a founder, and a troll has opinions about the syllabus.
- **Marigold.** Aethelgard's Sanctum needs a keeper. So does the question of what she ran from, and whether, with these two beside her, it is time to go back and look at it.
- **Amy.** The knighthoods were given in session 13; in the epilogue, a standing invitation to Drakenshire for the bear.
- **Major Ursa.** Gets the swan from the banquet. Filly may set a place for Tawny.
