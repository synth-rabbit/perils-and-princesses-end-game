# Session 10 Scene 16: Hesperia and the Crystal Orchard

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Asset checklist:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]

> **Run from this note:** narration, conversations, clues and scene rules are included. Other note links are optional background or continuation scenes.

**Foundry scene:** **Crystal Orchard** — refreshed backdrop.

## Arrival — ask before taking (15–25 minutes)

> *Apples shine beneath the leaves as though someone has trapped sunset inside them. Ruby-red fruit grows on the oldest tree at the center of the grove. Beside its roots stands an older woman in a gown of living moss. She looks at your hands, then at your faces.*

**Hesperia — dignified, patient until ignored:**

> “How pleasant. Visitors who have not yet taken anything.”

**GM:** Give them that warning before someone accidentally triggers a fight. She is a person defending her home, not a hidden trap. The new backdrop depicts her as an older woman, not the child in the former illustration.

## Permission and the wormy apple

If they introduce themselves, she returns the courtesy. Grand titles delight her:

> “Lady of the Ruby Boughs? That will do very nicely. Now, what brings you beneath my branches?”

If they explain Amy's rescue or ask for a wormy apple:

> “The fish sent you. Of course he did. He always forgets to mention that these trees belong to someone.”
>
> “You may take what you need. Choose gently. The worm has as much right to its breakfast as you do.”

**Resolution:** a respectful request can earn the fresh apple with its live crystal worm. No automatic second fetch quest or persuasion tax. Record who carries it. The Wish Fish needs it fresh; candying it removes the live-worm solution.

If someone reaches without asking, offer a visible warning first:

> *A root curls over the toe of your boot. Hesperia's smile goes very still. “Were you forgetting a word?”*

A sincere apology can reopen the conversation. Deliberate theft can provoke her spells; allow retreat or restitution instead of forcing a fight to the death.

## Fruit rules ready at the table

Fresh or candied crystal apples make a living knight captain pleasantly hazy, reducing the hunt **by 1** when accepted. The captain realizes afterward and will not willingly repeat it. Damon is not living and is unaffected; Ulrik can be affected if tricked into eating one. The fruit becomes dull stone outside the Scorchwoods, so it is not unlimited portable gemstone wealth.

**Hesperia if asked:** “Their sweetness softens a hard thought for a little while. Do not mistake that for changing a heart.”

## A friendship the records got wrong

If they inspect the old trunk, give the actual clue verbally or as a separate note: **G+C**. It need not be printed on the scene art.

> “Gemini and Cobalt. A prince and a dragon. They sat here arguing about which road to take next. Friends do that, you know. Disagree.”

If they have read the cottage's hostile account:

> “The writer preferred a comfortable lie. I was here. They left together because they wanted to.”

This challenges the assumption that every dragon is a monster without excusing Embra's betrayal. Hesperia can ask trees about activity they actually observed; she is not an all-knowing map. She is bound to her grove and cannot escort them to the cave.

## Hunt procedure at this scene


Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
