# Session 13 Scene 4: The Frozen Causeway

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 15 min · **Foundry:** module scene **Rosehold 0 — The Thornwall** (A2)
> 🔗 Castle note: [[00_The_Thornwall]], A2 · Tracker: [[rosehold_tracker]]

The first step inside the Queen's reach. **Start the Cold at 0** and tell the players it exists: *"From here on, the castle notices noise and time. I'll keep a count. When it rises, bad things wake up."* (Rules: [[rosehold_runbook]], section 3.)

## Entry

> *A spine of rock a wagon wide, glazed with ice, a long fall either side. The wind comes down it like something poured. Halfway along, two dead trees lean over the path with their roots in nothing.*

Amy rides Aurelius behind the party. The allies wait at the foot of the causeway until the wall is broken.

## The ice

- Crossing **at a walk** is safe.
- **Running, fighting or riding** calls for **GRACE**. A failure is a slide to the edge: **d6**, and hanging on until helped up (an Action for a friend).
- Filly's **Natural Climber** gives advantage. **Major Ursa** is a polar bear: he does not slide, and anyone holding on to his fur has advantage too.
- ⭐ **Vega** can Shape grit across the ice: no tests for anyone.

## The two trees

They are [[Awakened_Tree|Awakened Trees]]: ents of the low forest, marched up here and frozen angry. They let the party reach the middle, then close in from both ends.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Awakened_Tree]] ×2 | 8 | 8 | 2 | Twice: Bash (d6) | Axes, hatchets and fire ignore Armor. 1 GD Tangle. Wounded = Rootbound Curse |

⭐ **Vega spotlight.** He can speak to trees (Elemental Connection, and the **Rosewood Crown** if he wears it). If he asks what they want:

> **The trees, together, like a floor settling:** *"Down. Where it is green."*

A **GRACE** test (his manners; **advantage** if he mentions the two ents he woke fifty years early in session 6), and they simply walk off down the mountain, past forty astonished princesses. **That is a win and costs no dice.**

**If it comes to a fight:** it is loud. **+1 Cold** (a loud fight in the open). Blitzenaxt ignores their Armor.

## Exit

The wall is ahead: [[session13_05_the_barrier_of_thorns|The Barrier of Thorns]].
