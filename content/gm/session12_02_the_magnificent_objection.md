# Session 12 Scene 2: The Magnificent Objection

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 15–20 min · **Foundry:** **Knight Camp** map

The parley. It can avert the fight with Finnian. It cannot avert the fight with Embra.

## Arrival

> *Every conversation in camp stops when the lioness walks into the firelight. A squire drops a mirror. The knight behind it does not flinch: he turns, sees three royals and a lion, and smiles like a man whose audience has finally arrived.*

> **Sir Finnian:** "Hold! No — don't hold, come closer, the light's better. Sir Finnian the Magnificent, champion to Her Highness the Princess Amy. You'll have heard of me. You have news of the dragon?"

**Direction:** Finnian is vain, loud, and sincerely brave. He is not a villain. Play him as the knight who would be the hero of this story if he were not on the wrong side of it.

## What he says

- **About the hunt:** "Her Highness was most insistent. The beast is to be slain, not driven off. Slain. She has asked me twice whether I am sure I can do it."
- **About Amy lately:** a pause. "She has been... tired. She no longer feeds the cats. One doesn't comment."
- **If told the truth:** "Then *show* me. I will not gamble her life on a stranger's claim, whichever way that claim points."

## Evidence that works

Two pieces convince him. Each is a moment at the table, not a roll.

| Evidence | What he sees |
|---|---|
| **The Silver Rose pins** | Blessed to point at Amy. They point north, at the cave, away from the carriage. |
| **Pippin's compass** | "Tracks the princess." It points at the carriage. *Both cannot be right* unless there are two of her. |
| **Scamp** | Amy's squirrel since childhood. He will not go near the carriage and bares his teeth at it. |
| **The necklace** | She wears one. The party can describe its twin exactly. |
| **A question only Amy could answer** | Finnian supplies it himself: "What did Her Highness name the kitchen cat?" Embra does not know. (Cream. Marigold can know this from Amy.) |
| **Sir Raphael**, if fetched | "Finn. She used to cry when people were sad. When did you last see her cry?" |

If the players offer only words, call for **GRACE** (Vega's manners have advantage; Filly's bluntness does not) to earn the chance to show something.

## Embra cuts in

The carriage door opens when the second piece of evidence lands, or when anyone gets within reach of it.

> *She steps down as if the grass has been laid for her. Amy's face, Amy's yellow dress, and an expression Amy has never worn in her life: bored, and counting.*
>
> **"Princess Amy":** "A bewitched trinket. A trained rodent. A very *useful* story. Sir Finnian, these people are wearing things that belong to me."

She has noticed the pendant, the spectacles, the axe, the shield. **They are hers and she can smell them.** Every cursed PC feels the pull of the hoard answer her: no test, only the description. Their eyes and hers are the same.

> **Embra, quietly, to the party alone:** "You've been in my house. You took my things. And you've come to ask *me* for a favour."

## Getting close (sneak route)

GRACE tests to reach the carriage unseen; one failure brings a guard's "Oi." At the window: Embra is awake, looking straight at the intruder. "I could hear your heart from the road. Sit down. You have until I finish this plum."

## A private bargain

If the party offers before blades are drawn, she listens. She wants: her hoard back, **all of it**; never to be hunted; and not to go back in the cave. She offers: the key, and the swap, and to give them **freely** one item each of what they took (which ends the curse on that item's bearer).

- Accepting means returning the rest. Filly holds two items and would keep one.
- This avoids scene 3 entirely. It is allowed. **The GM's plan is a fight, so Embra asks for one thing too many:** "And the lioness. I've never had a lioness."

## How the fight starts

Whichever comes first:
- The party refuses, or laughs.
- Finnian is convinced and says, "Your Highness, I must ask you to step away from the carriage." Embra: "Guards. Sir Finnian has been bewitched. Kill the lion first."
- Finnian is *not* convinced: "Then I am sorry. Stand down, or stand fast." He fights for her (scene 3, variant B).
- Anyone touches the necklace.
