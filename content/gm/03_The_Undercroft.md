# Level −1 — The Undercroft

![[maps/morwennas_castle/painted_originals/map_rosehold_u_undercroft.png]]

**Keyed GM plan** (room letters, secret doors):

![[maps/morwennas_castle/rosehold_u_undercroft_gm.png]]

> **Runbook:** [[rosehold_runbook]] · **Tracker:** [[rosehold_tracker]] · **Parent:** [[Rosehold]] · **Foundry scene art:** module `build/art/maps/map_rosehold_u_undercroft.png` (6144 px on the long side)

Under the keep: water, cells, the honoured dead, the Queen's vault, and the thing in the well. **Optional but rewarding**: it holds the prisoners, the best loot, and the only way to weaken the last fight. **Foundry:** the floor plan. 75–100 minutes.

**Reached by** the Servants' Stair (C6), the kitchen dumbwaiter (to D3), or Bannik's tunnel (to D1).

## D0. The Stair Foot

Cold and dark. Light is needed below. Water sound to the south.

## D1. The Cistern

> *A vaulted hall half full of black water, perfectly still. Stone walkways run round the edge. Something has been dissolving the mortar: the water has a skin on it, and the skin is moving.*

A [[Black_Ooze]] lives in the cistern. It is the same corruption the party met in the mine, grown fat.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Black_Ooze]] | 10 | 4 | 0 | Corrosive Touch (d6) | Corrodes wood and metal, **not silver**. A blade splits it in two at half HP. Fire kills; freezing makes it brittle |

- The walkways are slick: fighting on them is **GRACE** or into the water (the ooze gets a free touch).
- **A Corruption Cure** poured in kills it outright and clears the water. The party has two. "Anything black and bubbly."
- **Bannik's tunnel** surfaces in the south-west corner. If he brought them, he says, of the ooze: *"That is not one of mine."*

## D2. The Cold Cells

> *A corridor of iron doors. Frost on every bar. From the third cell on the left, a man's voice, hoarse and cheerful: "If that's supper, I'd like to register a complaint about lunch."*

Three prisoners.

**Bran the Forager.** The man whose note, snare, net and fishing pole the party has been carrying since session 8. He went "deeper," found the road north, and was picked up by a patrol. Thin, unbroken, extremely glad to see his fishing pole. He knows the mountain paths: with Bran guiding, the allies outside can reach the **postern** (B2), and the Cold's effect on the muster is delayed by one step.
> **Bran:** "You found my snare? Did it catch anything? No? Thirty years I've been setting that snare."

**Lady Vespera.** The Baron's would-be bride, whose journal the party stole in session 5. She came north to make her own bargain with the Queen, unicorn or no unicorn, and the Queen had no use for her. She has been here a month. She is cold, furious, and for the first time in her life has nothing to trade.
> **Vespera:** "You. Of course it's you. Well? Gloat, or open the door."
- Freed, she is not an ally, but she is honest for once: she tells them the Queen "is not a fairy and not a monster. She is a woman who stopped. I looked her in the eye and there was nobody in." She knows the vault's glyph (D5).
- She can be left, escorted out to Daisy's custody, or handed to Grumbold.

**"The girl."** In the last cell, a young princess in a torn travelling cloak, with a rose pin, who says she is an Initiate captured on the causeway. She is frightened, grateful, and asks a great many questions about the party's friends.

It is **Morwenna**, wearing **Disguise**. She wants to look at them before the end.
- **Spectacles of Discernment** see through it at once. So does Sniff (she smells of nothing, like the gargoyles) and Major Ursa, who will not go near her.
- Otherwise a **WITS** test with disadvantage, or she walks with them for a room or two, asking: *"And you'd go back for each other? Every time? Even when it's not worth it?"*
- Unmasked or not, she leaves with **Poof!** when it suits her. If someone was kind to her without knowing, **note who**: in the throne room that hero has advantage on tests to reach her. *"You gave me your cloak. You didn't know who I was."*
- She does not fight here. Attacked, she casts Bubble and goes.

## D3. The Jailer's Guardroom

The jailer is a [[Troll]] in a turnkey's apron, asleep by a cold stove with the keys on his belt. His name is **Mortar**, he is Grumbold's cousin, and he has a streaming cold.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Troll]] (Mortar) | 15 | 7 | 3 | Bash (d12) | Sneezes: on waking, everyone Nearby tests GRACE or is knocked prone |

The party has cured exactly this before: **the corruption causes the sneezing.** A Corruption Cure, Marigold's Tears, or Nettie's call cures him, and a cured Mortar hands over the keys and asks whether Grumbold still has the mine. Stealing the keys from a sleeping troll is **GRACE**; fighting him is a hard, loud fight (+1 Cold).

**The dumbwaiter** from the kitchen arrives here. **The door north** is sealed with thorns: Aethelgard's Shears, fire, Pippin's key, or Spending Time with an axe.

## D4. The Crypt of the Knight-Protectors

> *Three stone biers under a low roof. On each lies a knight in old armour with a rose on the breastplate, hands folded on a sword. The frost on them is thicker than anywhere else in the castle, and it is growing as you watch.*

Three [[Wight|Wights]]: the Society's first Knight-Protectors, risen cold. **They rise one at a time**, one per round, starting when someone crosses the middle of the room.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Wight]] ×3 | 12 | 14 | 2 | Sword (d8) | Silver and enchanted weapons ignore Armor. 2 GD Bind and Fog. Wounded = WITS or Doom Curse |

Three wights is a wall. Ways through that are not a fight:
- **The oath.** They are still Knight-Protectors. A hero who speaks the oath and names their own rank ("Initiate of the Society") is challenged instead: *"Who holds the gate?"* The answer is **Hilde**. If she was freed, say so, and they lie back down.
- **Back to Bed** (Songkeeper's Chime, the Rosewood Crown).
- **⭐ Marigold's Death Knell:** rung here, it lets her ask one of them three questions before the reapers come for all three. Good questions: *What is in the well? How did the curse get in? What did she ask you to do?* (Answers: "The cold that was here before the castle." "She went down alone to face it, so that none of us would have to." "To leave her. We obeyed. We should not have.")
- Sir Dain's **"Hold, just a little longer"** is carved over the door. The party laid Dain to rest; a hero who says so is believed.

**The passage south** from the crypt leads to the Relic Vault. On the painted map it is an open corridor; on the keyed plan it is secret. To keep it secret, wall off both ends in Foundry and reveal it when found (WITS, Wren's True Map, or a wight's answer).

## D5. The Relic Vault

> *Shelves. Crates with the Baron's seal on them, some never opened. This is where it all went: everything dug out of the grave mounds, everything carried north. In the middle of the floor, on a stand, a glass orb with something moving in it.*

- **Glyph trap** on the main door (not the secret stair): a **Fireball** sigil. Opening it without the word: everyone Nearby tests **GRACE**, 3d6, half on a success. Vespera knows the word (*"Mine."*). Parker disarms it. The Spectacles spot it.
- **The second control orb.** **⭐ Filly:** it is aimed at Major Ursa the moment the party enters (or at Filly herself, if she is in Wild Form): Major SAVEs (10) or turns on the nearest hero for one round. Filly grabbing or smashing the orb is one Action, no roll: she has done this before, on a bridge.
- **Loot:** see [[Rosehold_Treasures]]: the grave relics (300 SP), **Aurelius's horn-shavings** (three doses of 2d4+4), the **red dragon statue** (1,000 SP).
- **Dragonfilch temptation:** none. The curse ended for everyone when Embra died (session 12). Describe the pull they no longer feel.

## D6. The Frostbind Well

> *The room is round and there is no frost in it at all. It is too cold for frost. In the centre is a well-head older than the castle, older than the mountain road, and the dark in it is not the dark of deep water. Something down there breathes out, and your Gift Dice feel heavy in your hands.*

This is where the curse came from. Morwenna went down alone to face it, forty years ago.

- **While in this room**, Gift Dice return only on a 1–2.
- **The well speaks** to one hero, in their own voice, and asks what they are afraid of. Answering honestly out loud is brave and costs nothing. Refusing is a **RESOLVE** test or Weary.
- **Purifying the well** weakens the final fight (Frost-Crowned loses her Armor and cannot cast Bubble). Any one of:
  - **⭐ Marigold, Guardian of the Sanctum:** Radiant Wave from the staff into the well, then a **RESOLVE** test with disadvantage (advantage instead if Aethelgard was freed).
  - **A Corruption Cure** poured in, with Aurelius's horn-shavings.
  - **The Golden Unicorn Tear**, which does it with no test, and is spent.
- **It fights back once:** 2d4 [[Frostbind_Wisp|Frostbind Wisps]] boil up as the purification begins.

When it is done:
> *The breathing stops. Far above you, through four floors of stone, somebody cries out: not in pain. In surprise.*

Cold −2.
