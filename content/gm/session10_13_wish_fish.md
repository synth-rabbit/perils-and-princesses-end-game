# Session 10 Scene 13: Optional: The Wish Fish

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 10–15 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** **Enchanted Pool**, the book's separate Wish Fish location. 🔗 [[05_Pool]] · [[Wish_Fish_Quests]]

> *As your cup reaches the surface, the water pulls away. A golden fish peers up at you. “We haven't discussed payment.”*

**Direction:** Play him as smug and delighted with bargaining. He wants a crystal apple, actually for the **live worm inside**. Curious questions can uncover that catch before a wasted return journey. Candied apples will not do. He cannot be harmed while in his water; threats do not automatically produce the ingredient.

**Choice:** bargain, ask where the fruit grows, or inspect the large rock beside the pool. Its lock opens on WITS, with advantage for a relevant Talent; failure allows Spending Time to force it. Instructions from a friendly gnome bypass the test. The tunnel leads to the village, so this can also introduce [[session10_12_pippin_bargain|Pippin’s Bargain]] from the opposite direction.

**Next lead:** **Crystal Orchard** and Hesperia; a ready-to-run orchard arrival is included below. Ask permission before picking. If the party already has a suitable apple, honor the bargain immediately; additional quests are for additional water.

**Good stopping point:** the bargain is understood and the party chooses the orchard or the original-key alternative.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Negotiate with something that enjoys being difficult

**Wish Fish — every sentence sounds like a favor:**

> “You want water. I have water. This is already going splendidly for one of us.”

If they mention Pippin:

> “How is the old hammer-botherer? Still sending people instead of visiting? Charming.”

If asked for the price:

> “A crystal apple. Fresh. And don't let anyone clean out the worm. The worm is the interesting part.”

**GM:** He need not volunteer the worm immediately, but direct questions about what counts as acceptable must get usable answers. Do not force a wasted trip after a careful negotiation. One fresh wormy apple buys the needed water. He withholds the warning about Hesperia unless their questions draw it out.

**If they offer a candied apple:**

> “A sculpture of my lunch. How thoughtful. Unfortunately, I prefer my lunch alive.”

**If threatened:**

> *He opens his mouth, unimpressed. The water draws back from your hand as neatly as a curtain.*

He is invulnerable while in the water. If the players propose changing that circumstance, adjudicate their real plan; this is not permission to invent universal immunity outside the pool or force a fight. He grants access to water, not magical wishes.

### Where to go next

He can direct them toward the Crystal Orchard. Candy or Pippin may already have warned them to ask Hesperia's permission. If not, that is something they can discover at the orchard; no compulsory ambush occurs before they can speak.

The large rock has a gnomish lock. **WITS**, advantage with relevant Talent, opens it. On failure, they may Spend Time forcing it; warn about the hunt cost first. Friendly gnome instructions bypass the roll. Inside is the tunnel to the village.

### If they return with the apple

> *The fish circles once, examining the fruit with great seriousness. Then he snaps up the worm. The water rises beneath your empty vessel as though it has been waiting to help all along.*

Give the water; do not make another quest mandatory. For **additional** water, choose or roll d4: Finnian's helmet plume; a flattering poem performed for him; gems for the pool; or an insult delivered to Pippin. The book gives no fixed gem amount—agree the bargain before departure.

### A quick orchard arrival if they continue immediately

**Switch Foundry scene to Crystal Orchard.**

> *Fruit shines like cut glass beneath the leaves. A woman steps from an old tree and rests her hand on its bark. “Visitors,” she says. “How lovely when they remember to introduce themselves.”*

**Hesperia:** “Ask, and we may discuss it. Take, and we shall have a very different conversation.” Respectful introductions and explaining Amy's need can earn permission to pick a wormy apple; do not automatically add a new task. She knows the fish is troublesome. Return to the Pool bargain above once they have the fruit.

If they steal, she can use **Tangle or plant-only Animate, 3 GD**. Profile: [[Hesperia]] HP 10 / SAVE 15 / Armor 0, unarmed 1. Prefer a visible magical warning and an opportunity to apologize over forcing combat. She is bound to her grove. Fresh or candied fruit reduces the hunt by 1 if a living captain willingly eats it; they will not willingly repeat it. Damon is unaffected; Ulrik must be tricked. Outside the forest the fruit becomes dull stone.

**Hesperia's spell details:** Tangle (Stone's Throw) creates grasping vines in a [DICE]×10-foot radius for [SUM] rounds; RESOLVE to avoid entanglement. Plant-only Animate brings [DICE] Nearby plants to obedient life for [SUM] minutes. [DICE] is dice rolled, [SUM] their total. Use her 3 GD; 1–3 returns a die and 4–6 consumes it. Do not invent a free extra attack when describing the plants.

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
