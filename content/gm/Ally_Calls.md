# Ally Calls

Everyone the party has helped since Willowbrook turns up at the muster below [[Rosehold]]. Most of them hold the line outside. A few can be **called** inside the castle: each ally answers **once**, then goes back to the fight below.

**How many:** each PC picks **two** allies at the muster to carry as calls (so four to six in all). **Princess Amy and Aurelius come regardless** and do not use up a pick. Calling an ally is free on your turn; they arrive at once, do their one thing, and leave. An ally who was never befriended in play is not available.

|---|---|---|
| **[[Daisy_of_Silverspire|Daisy of Silverspire]]** | Sessions 2, 5, 8, 10 | **Lullaby.** One group of living enemies Nearby (up to 6) falls asleep without a SAVE. Works on Thornsworn, not on thorn or ice things. |
| **[[Nim_the_Badger|Nim the Badger]]** | Freed in session 5; "call my name" | **Poof!** The whole party and anyone they are carrying are whisked to any room of the castle they have already visited. |
| **[[Billy_Goat]]** | Crackers, session 6; freed from the orb, session 8 | **The run-up.** Destroys one door, gate, barricade or portcullis outright, or hits one enemy for d10+d10. Demands crackers afterwards. |
| **[[Mama_Bear]]**, Pebble and Pit | Cub cured, session 8 | **One furious round.** Bite d10 and Claws d6 against up to two enemies, and whoever she is defending takes no damage until her next turn. |
| **[[Thistledown]] and the pixies** | Pond saved, session 10 | **Pranks.** For one whole fight, every hero has advantage on their first attack each round, and one enemy leader speaks in someone else's voice (its orders are ignored). |
| **[[Hedge_Witch|Nettie Blackthorn]]** | Tea and grave dust, sessions 6 and 8 | **Dissolve.** She uncorks something black and bubbly: ends one Curse, Ailment or corruption effect on everyone present, and hands over one fresh Corruption Cure. |

| **[[Bannik]]** | Soap and a black hen, session 7 | **Under the wall.** Before the assault, he tunnels the party into the Cistern (D1), skipping the gatehouse. During it, one burst of nostril spray: everyone hostile Nearby tests GRACE, d8 on a failure, d4 on a success. |
| **[[Mari_Lwyd|Mari Lwyd]]** | Rheumatism cured, session 7 | **The Night Mare rides.** Skeletal steeds for everyone: cross the causeway, the courtyard or the Stair of Vows in one move, ignoring hazards on the way. |
| **[[Puca]]** | The circus, session 7 | **The catapult.** Before the assault, launches the party over the wall onto the ramparts (B7). GRACE test each; a failure is d6 and a very public landing (+1 Cold). Filly has advantage: she has done this before. |

| **The Boneyard crystal wolves** | Rations and fetch, session 11 | **The pack.** For one fight, four [[Crystal_Wolf|crystal wolves]] flank with [[Major_Ursa|Major Ursa]]; he and they attack with advantage. The pups ride in on his back. (Tawny, whom they loved, died in session 12.) |

| **Morgana Twinkleberry, Fairy Godmother** | Session 1 onward | **Bibbidi.** One d20 anyone has just rolled becomes a 1. Once in the whole castle. |
| **[[Sir_Finnian_the_Magnificent|Sir Finnian]]** | Fooled in session 12; convinced by Amy afterwards (session 13) | **A magnificent entrance.** He and his surviving guards hold one room against all comers for the scene, announcing themselves at volume (+1 Cold, and worth it). |

**The muster outside** is run by Daisy and the PPS. If the Cold reaches 6, the Barrier begins to regrow and the allies below are in trouble: see [[rosehold_runbook]].
