# Rosehold Wrinkles

The castle is an enemy stronghold: whenever the party **Spends Time** inside [[Rosehold]], roll d4, and on a 1 roll here. Each Wrinkle also raises **the Cold** by 1 (see [[rosehold_runbook]]).

Roll d6

| Roll | Wrinkle |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Patrol.** Four [[Thornsworn_Guard|Thornsworn Guards]] and a sergeant round the corner. They have not seen the party yet: one round to hide, bluff or strike first. |
| 2 | **Wisps.** d4+1 [[Frostbind_Wisp|Frostbind Wisps]] drift through the wall toward whoever last spent a Heart Die. |
| 3 | **The thorns move.** Black briars creep across the nearest door. Cut through (Spend Time again), burn through (any fire), or find another way round. |
| 4 | **A mirror wakes.** The nearest reflective surface shows the Queen's face. She says one line to one hero (use [[Rosehold_Whispers]]) and that hero tests WITS or is Befuddled. |
| 5 | **Glass ravens.** 2d4 [[Glass_Raven|Glass Ravens]] at a window. Any that escape raise the Cold a second time. |
| 6 | **Deep frost.** The temperature drops. Everyone tests RESOLVE or becomes Weary until they reach a warm room. Anyone carrying a lit torch or wearing Mama Bear's amulet has advantage. |
