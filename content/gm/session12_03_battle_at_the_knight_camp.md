# Session 12 Scene 3: Battle at the Knight Camp

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 40–45 min · **Foundry:** **Knight Camp** map; fire, mule line and carriage markers

The fight that closes the Scorchwoods. **The goal is not to kill Embra. It is to take her alive with the key.**

## Sides

**Variant A, Finnian convinced** (two pieces of evidence shown): Finnian and Robin stand aside in round one, shouting at the guards to stop; from round two **Finnian fights beside the party**, and **four of the eight guards** obey him and lay down arms. The other four obey "the princess."

**Variant B, Finnian not convinced:** all of them fight. He can still be turned mid-battle: each piece of evidence shown during the fight is an Action, and the second one turns him.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Embra|"Princess Amy" (Embra)]] | 8 | 15 | 0 | Crystal Dagger (d6); on a 5–6 it shatters for +d6 | Defending against her has advantage. Ring of Fire (1 GD Fireball). Pixie Dust. Healing potion. **Must not die** |

| [[Drakenshire_Royal_Guard]] ×8 (or ×4) | 4 | 8 | 1 | Sword (d8) or Spear (d8) | Four at the carriage, four arrive from the fire in round 2 |

Three attendants hide under the carriage and are not combatants.

## The battlefield

- **The carriage:** cover; climbing on top is GRACE. The team of horses is still in harness.
- **The cook-fire and its kettle:** anything shoved into it takes d6.
- **The mule line:** four mules. ⭐ **Filly** can speak to them. *"Loose"* is one word in mule; four panicked mules through the guards is an antic, no dice: each guard tests SAVE or is knocked down and loses a turn.
- **Tent ropes:** Vega's Shape pulls the pegs; a collapsed tent holds everyone under it for a round.
- **The black tent** at the edge is empty. Nothing comes out of it.

## Round by round

**Round 1.** Guards form on the carriage. Embra, from the step, spends her **Ring of Fire**: Fireball at the largest clump of heroes. Roll 1 Gift Die; **GRACE** for half. ⭐ **Marigold's Dragonfire Shield** negates it for her outright and stores it: as a Reaction she may throw it back for d6. Describe Embra's face when her own fire comes back.

**Round 2.** The four guards from the fire arrive. Embra orders: *"The lion."* Two guards go for Tawny.

**Round 3.** **Embra runs.** She throws the Pixie Dust over herself and **floats**: up off the step, over the tents, toward the trees, at a walking pace she cannot steer well. From now on she is a Stone's Throw up and moving a Nearby distance each round. In three rounds she is Over Yonder and gone.
If Sir Raphael was fetched, this is when he arrives, takes in the floating princess, and says: "Well, that settles *that*."

**Rounds 4–6. Catching her.** Any one of these works; reward the first good idea:
- ⭐ **Filly:** the Lunar Grotto Spear, thrown to pin a trailing sleeve to a pine: **GRACE**, no damage on a success. Or *Give a Little Whistle*: every crow in the Scorchwoods mobs her down.
- ⭐ **Vega:** Shape a pillar under his own feet and simply take her by the ankle; or a Blast *past* her to bring down the branch she is drifting toward.
- ⭐ **Marigold:** **Smooch**. Embra has 8 HP; with two or three dice it almost certainly beats that. No SAVE. She loses the will to run, and floats there looking embarrassed until someone fetches a rope.
- A net (the party still has Bran's), a lasso from the mule line, Tawny's leap from the carriage roof (**GRACE** for Filly to stay on).

**If she is about to be killed:** remind the table of Pippin's second point, once. If they do it anyway, see "If it goes wrong".

## The guards

They believe they are defending their princess. They surrender when Embra is taken, when Finnian orders it, or when half are down. A level 3 **Storm** will clear most of them in one go; that is fine, but **say what it costs**: these are Amy's own household. Amy will ask about each of them by name in scene 6. Offer non-lethal results freely: Roar (they run), the mules, Glitter Spray, sinking them to the knees.

## When it ends

> *The dust settles. She hangs in the net, or sits in the grass with a lion's paw on her skirt, in a borrowed face, breathing hard. Round her neck, on a fine chain, is a pendant with a keyhole in it. And in her fist, held so tightly her knuckles are white, a small gold key.*
>
> **Embra:** "Two hundred and twenty years. I had *six weeks* of being brought breakfast."

She does not give up the key. It has to be taken: no roll once she is held. **The party now has the key and a prisoner.**

## Loot

- **The necklace key** (story item).
- Embra's **healing potion** (2d4), if she did not drink it.
- The **Ring of Fire** (1 GD Fireball; recharges at dawn if left overnight in hot coals). It is *hers*, not hoard treasure taken from the cave, but taking it from her by force is still stealing from a dragon: **WITS** test for whoever claims it, failure advances the curse a stage. Left on her finger, no test.
- The carriage strongbox: 80 SP, the hunt's pay chest. Finnian will object to this.

## If it goes wrong

- **Embra dies in Amy's body.** Amy cannot be restored by the necklaces. Do not end the campaign. Pippin: "There's one thing older than gnome-work. A true love's kiss. But your healer won't have that for a while yet, and it only works the once." Amy stays a dragon through the castle (she can break the Barrier as a dragon; it is her friendship, not her shape), the party reaches level 4 at the Barrier instead, and **Marigold must choose in the throne room** between Amy and Morwenna. Note it on [[rosehold_tracker]].
- **Embra escapes.** The compass still points at her. She goes to ground at the Stones; pick up there, short one scene.
- **The party loses.** They wake tied to the carriage wheel, on the road to the cave, where Finnian intends to slay the dragon at dawn. Scamp has the knots half chewed.
