# Session 13 Scene 1: The Dragon Killer's Feast

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 25 min · **Foundry:** your existing **Dragon's Cave** map (module fallback **Dragon Cave Lair**)

Open in the cave, the same evening. Four things happen here, all carried over from the unplayed half of session 12: Tawny is laid to rest, **Sir Finnian** finds out the truth and brings **the paper from the black tent**, Amy knights the party, and **Aurelius** arrives to take them north.

## Entry

> *The dragon's treasure room is full of knights. Someone has lit every lamp in the hoard. Four hunting parties are toasting a man in a feathered hat who keeps saying "honestly, it was mostly luck," and getting cheered louder every time. Nobody is toasting you. In the corner, out of the way of the boots, there is a lioness-shaped space where nobody is standing.*

**Direction:** let the celebration be loud and slightly insulting, and the corner quiet. The players lost Tawny an hour ago in fiction. Give them the room before anything else happens.

## Tawny

⭐ **Filly spotlight.** Ask Filly's player what Filly wants for Tawny. Do not suggest; wait. Then offer, if they want help:

- ⭐ **Vega spotlight:** the cave is stone. He can raise a cairn with Shape, no dice needed for something this small, or carve her name into the cave wall above the hoard so every knight who comes to gawk reads it.
- **Amy** sits down beside Filly without being asked. *"She stood in front of my door for you. Scamp told me. I'd have liked to meet her properly."*
- **Major Ursa** sniffs the place where Tawny fell, then lies down there, enormous and careful, and does not move until Filly does. *(Stats: [[Major_Ursa]].)*
- Tawny's silver claws and teeth are in Filly's purse. If she wants to make something of them, that is a job for the council (scene 3), where Pippin and Grumbold are waiting.

## Sir Raphael, Dragon Killer

He is mortified and thrilled in equal parts, and composing.

> **Raphael:** *(strumming)* "'Raphael the Lucky, with his sword so bright' — no. 'Raphael the Lucky, who wasn't really trying' — closer. Princesses! You were there. Did she roar before or after I stabbed her? It matters for the meter."

- He knows the princesses did the work. He will say so to them, quietly, and never to the room. "I'll put you in the second verse. Everyone remembers the second verse."
- He is quitting knighthood tonight with the money and the fame. **He will still come if called** at the castle ([[Ally_Calls]]): "A ballad needs a sequel."

## Sir Finnian finds out

The wolves let him go at sundown. He arrives last, muddy, with wolf-pup tooth marks in his gauntlet, and stops dead when he sees Amy sitting with the party.

> *Sir Finnian the Magnificent walks into the treasure room, sees his princess on the floor in a borrowed cloak with a squirrel on her shoulder, and his whole face changes. "Your Highness. You — you're smiling. You haven't smiled in six weeks."*

**Amy tells him.** Let her do it, in her own words, and let the party fill in what she does not know (the necklaces, the purse, the back entrance). Finnian takes it badly, then well:

> **Finnian:** "I guarded a dragon in a dress for six weeks and called it devotion. She asked me *twice* whether I was sure I could kill the beast." *A long breath.* "Princesses. Prince. I owe you an apology, and I am told I give a magnificent one."

He is now an ally. **Sir Finnian is available as an Ally Call** at the muster (he was not convinced at the camp; Amy convinced him here).

### The paper under the thorn

Finnian went back to the camp first, and found the black tent empty.

> **Finnian:** "The black knight and his wizard. Gone north the night before you came, the men say. On foot. No lantern. Nobody would go near the tent. I did." *He holds out a folded sheet of paper, and a thorn as long as your hand.* "This was on the table, pinned under that."

> **Read aloud: the paper**
>
> *U.*
> *The girl is found. They will undo it. Let them.*
> *Come home. Bring the knight. I want them to reach the wall. I want them to bring her to it.*
> *I want to watch her try.*
> *— M.*
>
> *On the back, in a different, hurried hand: a sketch map. The Fangtooth peaks. A road. A castle drawn as a rose inside a ring of thorns, and one word written beside it:* **ROSEHOLD.**

This is the first time the players see the initial **M** and the castle's name. **Do not explain it.** Nobody here knows who M is. *U* is Ulrik, if the players remember him from Daisy's suspicions; Finnian only knows "the wizard."

- ⭐ **Vega:** the thorn has dried **red mud** on its root: grave-earth and river clay, bound with something that is not water. Whatever stood in that black armour was made, not born.
- ⭐ **Filly** (Sniff, or just smelling the thorn): wet earth, old blood, and something she has met before only in a dream. **It is the knight from Tawny's nightmare.** Major Ursa does not know it. Filly does.
- **Amy** reads it twice. *"'The girl.' That's me."* Then, about the necklaces: *"They were a present. A man in a hood brought them to Drakenshire, a pair, and said a lady in the north admired my work. I thought it was so* nice *of her."*

**If the players ask Finnian about Damon:** "Sir Damon the Unyielding. Never takes his helm off. Never eats. I took him for a very devout man."

## Knighthoods

> *Amy stands up, borrows Vega's pickaxe because nobody has given her a sword, holds it very carefully by the wrong end, and the room goes quiet without being asked.*
>
> **Amy:** "I'm told I can do this. Kneel? Or — you don't have to kneel. Dame Filly. Sir Vega. Dame Marigold. Of Drakenshire, for as long as there is one."

- **Major Ursa** is knighted separately and at length, because Amy cannot help herself: *"Sir Major Ursa, Bear of the Realm, Protector of — oh, he's lying down. That's fine. Protector of the Floor."*
- **The freedom of Drakenshire:** any door in the kingdom, any table, any smith. The GM is not counting money any more; this is the formal version of "you can buy anything."
- If someone wants Tawny named too: Amy does it at once. *"Dame Tawny. Posthumously. Is that a word? It's a word now."*

## Aurelius

> *There are hoofbeats on a path too narrow for a horse. Aurelius steps into the lamplight with frost in his mane that was not there a week ago, and he looks at Amy for a long moment before he speaks.*
>
> **Aurelius:** "The wall on the mountain grew a hand's breadth last night. She knows you are free." *To the party:* "Daisy has called the Society. Everyone who owes you three a kindness is already walking north." *He lowers his head so Amy can reach his neck.* "I can carry one. The rest of you had better have friends with horses."

- He notices the empty space where Tawny should be, and says nothing, which is kinder.
- **Mari Lwyd's Teeth**, if the party still has them: skeletal steeds for everyone, "like mice pulling a fairy godmother's carriage." Marigold has one too, whether she remembers it or not.
- Amy, to Aurelius: "You knew her. The lady in the north." Aurelius: *"I knew this place when its windows were lit."* That is all he will say tonight.
