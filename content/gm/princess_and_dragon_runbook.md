# The Princess and the Dragon — Adventure Runbook

> **Start:** [[session10_prep]] closes the pond and introduces Amy. This runbook then remains in use for however many sessions the rescue takes.
> **At the table:** [[princess_and_dragon_tracker]] · [[princess_and_dragon_handouts]] · [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Primary source:** `books/The Princess and the Dragon - Digital Pages.pdf`, main adventure printed pp. 4–42. **PDF page = printed page + 2.** The supplied `books/extracts/the_princess_and_the_dragon.md` is an item inventory, not the full adventure.

## 1. What to run, and what changes for this campaign

Run the book's rescue, competing knights, gnome bargain, and ten locations. The group is already level 3 with tailored items, whereas the book expects 2–5 level-2 Princesses. Start with printed opponents. Pressure comes from the hunt, social complications, and protecting Amy; do not silently increase every enemy's defenses to counter the party's gifts.

**Two different waters:** the preceding campaign arc's **Pristine Pond** is Enchanted Realm location 11. The book's **Enchanted Pool** is Scorchwoods location 5, guarded by the Wish Fish. Pippin needs water from the latter; possession of pond water or a healing potion does not automatically complete his request.

The book's optional Faire is a prequel. Do not insert it into the middle of an active hunt. See [[princess_and_dragon_faire_reserve]] if you later want that material after rescue.

## 2. The adventure in one page

**Situation:** Embra enjoys Amy's body and royal privilege. Amy is trapped inside Embra's dragon body, penned in the cave, while Amy's own knights hunt her. The PCs must save her, reverse the swap, and stop hunters long enough to do so. Knowing the truth does not make the knights believe it.

**Three useful objectives:** keep Amy alive; deal with Princess Embra and the necklace lock; neutralize or redirect the hunt. These can be pursued in any order. A spare key is one route, not the adventure's only route.

| Route | What it needs | What still remains |
|---|---|---|
| Bargain with Embra | Locate Finnian's retinue; offer a believable way to gain safety/luxury without keeping Amy's life | Bring both wearers Nearby, use her key, remove both necklaces; settle the cave barrier and hunters. |
| Take the original key | Locate Embra, overcome her guards/deception/escape tools, recover the key | Possessing it is insufficient until **both wearers are Nearby**. Use persuasion, escort, capture, or another plan to reunite them. |
| Commission a replacement | Pippin + enchanted pool water + 50 SP **or** earned favor | Still requires Amy and Embra Nearby. A new key does not remotely restore Amy. |
| Another clever magical plan | A concrete proposal that fits established magic and resources | Decide its risks openly. The book expressly allows clever alternatives; do not require an invented immunity to block them. |

**Clues need not be locked behind a single roll:** Amy describes the swap and the key; the necklaces bear gnomish marks; gnomes identify Pippin; Scamp knows landmarks; the cave altar trail suggests gnomes. Roll for concealed details or risky actions, not to decide whether the players are allowed to learn the central problem.

## 3. Cave opening and Amy's account — book pp. 6–9

🔗 [[01_Cave]] · [[Dragon_Amy]] · [[Princess_Amy]] · [[Scamp]]

> *A black-scaled dragon turns toward the light. Her necklace flashes over a mound of gems. A squirrel clings to her shoulder. “Please don't scream,” she says, already sounding close to tears. “I know how this looks.” Behind you, your Silver Rose Pins pull steadily toward her.*

**Direction:** Sweet adult voice from an enormous awkward body. Amy is frightened, not a boss monster. She does not intentionally attack the PCs, even if struck. Do not resolve the opening as her normal 10-damage breath attack.

Run **up to three panic rounds**. After each round, **one PC may test GRACE with disadvantage** to calm her immediately. If nobody succeeds, she becomes calm after round three. A convincing personal connection can inform the attempt; do not assume the PCs met her at an unplayed Faire.

| Round | Hazard | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amy breaks a fog-filled orb. Vision: black-armored knight plunges a sword into an oozing heart; removing the helm reveals Ulrik's old face. | Each PC tests RESOLVE. If at least half fail, the Knight Tracker will start at **3**, rather than 2. With three PCs, this means **two or more failures**. |
| 2 | Falling stalactites | GRACE test; failure d4 damage. |
| 3 | Accidental sneeze of flame | Those in its path test GRACE; failure d6 damage. |

The earlier campaign nightmare is foreshadowing; it does **not** replace round one's roll or secretly increase the tracker. If Amy is calmed early, skip remaining hazards. Continued attacks after she calms provoke frightened evasive accidents, not a change to a hostile personality.

Once calm, let Amy explain in her own words:

- She sought friendship with a dragon who did not initially raid gems and cattle.
- Embra encouraged secret visits and asked about royal life.
- About a week before the book's opening, Embra gave her a matching necklace; wearing it swapped their bodies.
- Embra used a key between them to lock the necklaces, then sealed the entrance and left in Amy's body.
- Scamp can pass the barrier. Amy cannot; the hidden tunnel is too small for the dragon body.
- Scamp reports that the apparent princess has ordered a hunt and the knights are learning the paths.

**Evidence:** compass points to Amy, Scamp recognizes her, matching necklaces and a coherent account support her story. Filly can talk with Scamp. Amy can lend him as a scout, though she would rather keep him for comfort. Her hope of reconciliation with Embra should remain visible.

**Before leaving:** ask who watches Amy, what she will do if threatened, whether Scamp accompanies the party, and how the PCs will recognize a red compass alarm. Record Amy's current HP (maximum 24), not just “fine.” Allow the group to choose its destination.

## 4. Travel and the hunt — book pp. 10–11, 25

Use the detailed sheet in [[princess_and_dragon_tracker]]. These procedures continue across sessions; an out-of-game session ending does not advance the tracker.

1. Name destination, route, purpose, and companions. Use the existing Scorchwoods map; do not invent compulsory detours.
2. When traveling to a new keyed location, roll **d4 for a knight encounter** (1 = encounter) and **d4 for a Wrinkle** (1 = roll [[Scorchwoods_Wrinkles]]). These are separate book checks. Spending Time also calls for the d4 Wrinkle check.
3. For a deliberate search for a named hunting party, test WITS: success puts that party at the next keyed location unless impossible; failure produces a Wrinkle there. Do not undo a successful search with the random knight roll.
4. Resolve encounters and record tracker changes. An ordinary successful move does not inherently add a tracker point; actual triggers do.
5. At 6, select among still-active hunters and use their lair response. Warn through red compass light and other available evidence. The hunt has become an immediate rescue problem.

**Table rulings for ambiguous overlaps:** one instance of wasted time is not charged repeatedly under different names. A separate Wrinkle is an additional trigger; Spending Time with a Wrinkle is +2. An overnight Rest is +2 rather than also charging it as ordinary Spending Time. If both encounter rolls yield knights, bring two parties into the same scene or successive encounters; do not replace them with a forced fight. Clearly state costs before elective repairs, rests, or long distractions. These overlap rulings are this prep's procedure, not additional printed rules.

**Routing help, not a mandatory itinerary:**
- Book map surface links visibly connect Cave–Boneyard–Pool and Cave–Stump–Orchard–Stones–Cottage–Camp, with Pool–Flowers–Cottage on the east. Consult the actual map when counting rescue moves.
- Dashed underground links: Stump–Gnomes–Pool. The Pool entrance can bypass the stump puzzle entirely.
- Pippin → Pool → Orchard → Pool → Pippin is a logical ingredient loop, not a requirement if the original key route is working.
- Look for chances to hinder or ally with knights while doing the key work. Do not suspend the hunt to guarantee every location is visited.

## 5. The four hunting parties — book pp. 12–22

Choose an encounter activity that makes their personality clear before asking for a roll. Use [[Whisper_Table]] for overheard information. Treat the following as social approaches, not automatic successes.

Loud, polished, patronizing, sincerely protective. | Give him evidence he can personally check: compass, Scamp, necklace, Amy's memories. Offer a heroic rescue role. **Finnian and Embra:** she is usually with his party, so finding Finnian is a practical route to the original key. She is not obliged to confess when a pin points away from her. Let her call the PCs charlatans, play injured royalty, or quietly offer a bargain. Her attendants and guards are people to deceive or convince, not additional statless obstacles that must all be slain.

**Stella's sword:** 1 GD allows a d20 reroll by any combatant, friend or enemy. Recharge through a sincere wish on the evening star. Use [[Lady_Stella_the_Daring]] and the item extract for details.

**Ulrik and Damon:** Ulrik commands only within a Stone's Throw; outside that range Damon continues the last command. Separating them does not instantly switch Damon off. Only his head looks human under the armor. A gem apple affects Ulrik if tricked into eating it; it does not affect Damon. If exposed, Ulrik is willing to order murder. His 3 GD spells are [[Animate]], [[Restoration]], [[Disguise]], [[Haste]]. Read his grimoire with WITS at disadvantage; failure Befuddles. Its servant recipe is information, not an instantly usable extra combat ally.

## 6. Ten locations, ready in any order

Run descriptions and keyed detail from the linked location files. The following cards add an immediate table cue, the meaningful choice, and the next lead. **Read-alouds below are new paraphrases for this table.** Book details win over legacy additions unless labeled as a campaign connection above.

### 1. Cave — printed pp. 26–28

🔗 [[01_Cave]]. Start with Dragon Panic in section 3. Return here for the rescue finale.

- **Choice:** protect Amy, investigate the necklace, inspect treasure, or find a route out. The front barrier stops the dragon body; it does not stop the other PCs, humans, or Scamp.
- **Leads:** altar outside holds **100 SP of gems not yet part of the hoard**, therefore not Dragonfilch-cursed. WITS investigation reveals gnome footprints toward the stump. Necklace maker's mark points to gnomes. Scamp knows landmarks and knights.
- **Hoard:** WITS investigation can detect a curse, not its exact nature. [[Magic_Items_In_The_Hoard]] lists six possible finds. Warn with an unsettling pull or a dragon-lore clue before a consequence the PCs could reasonably recognize.
- **Barrier:** Embra can undo it from outside while human. After returning to her dragon body she is trapped too, but may instruct someone outside to dispel it. Powerful intervention is a GM option. Necklace reversal does not itself remove the barrier.
- **Campaign clue:** a thorn sigil matches the nightmare; it suggests Morwenna's involvement. Ulrik's correspondence provides the stronger documentary link. The sigil adds no new immunity.

### 2. Boneyard — printed p. 29

🔗 [[02_Boneyard]] · [[Crystal_Wolf]]

> *Flowers climb the ribs of a dragon large enough to shelter a house. Diamond teeth flash between the bones. Behind the watchful adults, wolf pups tug at a walking stick with a bright jeweled knob.*

- Roll **3d4 wolves** for the den. They protect their family; leaving, bargaining, distracting, and trading are valid responses.
- **Filly spotlight:** talk to the adults and pups; offer food or another toy. No need to “tame” the whole pack or win combat first.
- **Reward/lead:** Pippin's stick earns his favor and carries the color mnemonic. A language/code Talent recognizes it as a code; give the actual phrase as a handout.
- Seen crystal wolves can be recorded for Filly's future Wild Form discussion, without granting level 4 or a ruling that all magical creatures are valid forms now.

### 3. Stump — printed p. 30

🔗 [[03_Stump]] · [[Gnomish_Warrior_Statue]]

> *Four small stone warriors watch a broad stump. Tiny feet have worn a path through the grass. Five knotholes catch the light in different colors.*

- Correct sequence: **green, blue, green, yellow, orange, yellow**. Knotholes available: red, orange, yellow, green, blue. Human-sized visitors crawl.
- The stick's phrase supplies the code. Alternatively **Spend Time** hiding to watch a gnome enter; apply the hunt procedure openly.
- Wrong sequence animates **four statues**. If defeated, a guard emerges angrily; GRACE can gain entry. This is one possible route, not a mandatory fight.
- If the party cannot solve it, the Pool's hidden rock entrance is a separate access route. Do not place the crucial key behind this puzzle alone.
- Check Tawny's route rather than assuming a lioness fits every gnome passage. Leaving her outside or negotiating help is a logistical choice, not an automatic separation ambush.

### 4. Gnomes — printed pp. 31–33

🔗 [[04_Gnomes]] · [[Pippin_Cobbleton]] · [[Tifa]] · [[Gnomish_Shops]] · [[Gnome_Names]]

> *Market lights sparkle below the roots. A voice shouts, “Mind the ceiling!” A second voice adds, “And the roof!” You realize the second speaker is standing beside a house at your knee.*

- Market gnomes direct necklace questions to **Pippin's Custom Creations**. He explains both key routes honestly.
- Replacement price: **enchanted pool water + 50 SP**. Returning his stick/earning his favor waives the fee only. He dislikes dealing with the Wish Fish.
- **Book leaves crafting duration open. Table ruling:** once supplied, crafting takes one Spend Time; state that before committing and apply the tracker. A clever means of expediting it can change this; it is not a printed multi-day wait.
- Repairs at Tough Breaks take Spending Time. Gnomish greatswords function as human daggers: 10 SP ordinary, 20 SP crystal. Do not re-award the mine's consumed raw silver to pay this bill.
- Candy's apples retain the knight-pacifying effect but have no live worm for the fish. Candy can warn about Hesperia.
- **Campaign Tifa:** offer reassurance and evidence-sharing; she can identify Ulrik as the commissioner and help interpret the enchantment. Neither her trust nor a “better key” is required in addition to Pippin's printed bargain.
- Exit tunnel leads to the Pool's false rock. Ask about this shortcut before the party leaves.

### 5. Pool — printed pp. 34–35

🔗 [[05_Pool]] · [[Wish_Fish_Quests]]

> *You lower a cup toward the shining water. The whole surface leans away. A golden fish rises beneath it, looking delighted with himself. “Oh, were you trying to get something?”*

- Water evades collection until the fish allows it. He is immune to harm **while in his water**, not an all-purpose wish machine.
- He asks for a crystal apple; he actually wants a **live crystal worm inside**. A candied apple will not do. Let careful questions uncover the catch before the return trip.
- He knows about Hesperia but enjoys not warning visitors. A respectful orchard visit avoids his intended ambush.
- Extra water can cost another quest from the d4 table: Finnian's plume, praise poem, gems, or an insult for Pippin. The core deal needs one suitable apple, not all four tasks.
- False rock: inspect to find a lock; WITS opens, with advantage for relevant Talent. Failure can be overcome by Spending Time forcing it. A friendly gnome's instructions open it without a test. Leads directly to the village.

### 6. Flowers — printed p. 35

🔗 [[06_Flowers]] · [[Snapdragon_Jokes]]

> *Yellow flowers turn toward you. One whispers to its neighbor, “Wait for it.” The field begins to giggle.*

- Hearing the jokes: RESOLVE or laugh helplessly for an hour. Properly muffled ears give advantage.
- Lost time getting someone through the field advances the tracker **once** for this setback, not once per affected PC.
- Make the flowers laugh first: an appropriate player joke, WITS pun, or relevant Talent. This can prevent their attack of jokes entirely.
- Table pacing: a quick comic obstacle, not a compulsory hour of real-time roleplay.

### 7. Orchard — printed p. 37

🔗 [[07_Orchard]] · [[Hesperia]]

> *Apples glint like gems under the leaves. A woman in a moss-green gown steps from an old tree and clears her throat with the patience of someone who has been ignored for centuries.*

- **Ask before picking.** Hesperia likes elaborate respectful titles. Theft provokes a magical reprimand; she is otherwise peaceful. She can cast Tangle and Animate on plants, using 3 GD.
- Ask for a fresh apple with a live worm for the fish. She can explain the fruit and fish's trick; she is not required to become another fetch quest.
- Eating fresh **or candied** fruit makes living captains pleasantly hazy: reduce tracker by 1 when a knight captain accepts it. They remember afterward and will not willingly eat another. Damon is excluded; tricking Ulrik is possible under his party rules.
- Fruit becomes dull stone outside the Scorchwoods. Do not assign unlimited gemstone resale value.
- “G+C” carving: Hesperia confirms Gemini and Cobalt's friendship, contradicting the cottage's anti-dragon bias. She may ask the trees about activity, but only what they noticed; no omniscient GPS.

### 8. Stones — printed p. 38

🔗 [[08_Stones]]

> *The birds stop singing. Between scorched standing stones, voices whisper orders to soldiers who are no longer there. Beneath it all is one exhausted thought: “We failed.”*

- Nearby PCs test WITS. Failure traps them in Sir Dain's remembered battle until removed from the stones; the illusion causes pain and fear **but no damage**. Those freed are Befuddled.
- Even successful PCs sense shame. Physically lead friends out rather than attacking an illusory dragon.
- **Marigold spotlight:** bring the cottage's history or another credible account that Dain's stand allowed reinforcements to save thousands. Compassion plus truthful evidence releases him, ends the illusion/Befuddlement, and leaves **Ring of Heart**.
- The ring permits spending any number of Heart Dice for healing as at a Picnic, then blocks all Heart Dice use until three days' rest. Explain that cost before activation.
- The knight is not a required Wight combat encounter. Do not use a hostile wight actor simply because one exists in the module.

### 9. Cottage — printed p. 40

🔗 [[09_Cottage]] · [[princess_and_dragon_handouts]]

> *Rain has softened the papers beneath a broken roof. Sketches of dragons cover the walls. One bundle of notes is tied shut so tightly the string has cut the paper.*

- Give the four research leads on inspection: Dragonfilch and voluntary gifts; **Sir Dain vs Sunder**, whose stand saved the kingdom; **Pitch**, Embra's larger ancestor whose bones house the wolves; **Gemini and Cobalt**, dismissed by the biased archivist.
- The history resolves the Stones; Hesperia verifies the friendship. Both help the party argue that “dragon” does not mean “unthinking monster.”
- No extra guardian, secret fight, or mandatory search roll for plainly available notes. If the group conducts prolonged research, state Spending Time and the clock cost.
- This is not confirmation that Marigold's old southern whisper was Dain. That unresolved campaign thread stays unresolved unless deliberately developed later.

### 10. Camp — printed p. 41

🔗 [[10_Camp]] · [[Whisper_Table]]

> *Banners hang over muddy tents. Someone is polishing armor; someone else is badly playing a heroic tune. Every conversation stops when the lioness walks into the firelight.*

- At least one knight party is here. Choose based on established movements or roll among plausible parties; do not resurrect eliminated hunters to satisfy the line.
- Gossip gives leads without announcing the solution. Mules, guards, squires, and attendants provide grounded social approaches.
- Find Finnian/Embra, seek Raphael's help, negotiate with Stella, or observe Ulrik. Respect ordinary privacy and suspicion; displaying a Silver Rose Pin is an introduction, not mind control.
- Resting overnight advances an active tracker by 2, even at a friendly camp. Make that tradeoff clear.

## 7. Resolving the swap and the cave crisis

Use this checklist when the plan reaches its decisive scene:

- [ ] Identify who has the **key**, who wears each necklace, and the location of both bodies.
- [ ] Establish that Amy and Embra are **Nearby**. Resolve any transport, restraint, bargaining, or distraction in play.
- [ ] Use the key to unlock the enchantment; remove both necklaces. Record who returned to which body.
- [ ] Check the **cave barrier separately**. Embra in her restored dragon body is trapped if it remains; she can instruct an outside helper or bargain for release.
- [ ] Stop uninformed allies attacking the newly restored dragon. Tell players who is visibly where; do not use body confusion as an untelegraphed gotcha.
- [ ] Resolve active hunters and any bargain with Embra. Do not require Damon to be last or guaranteed to reach the lair.
- [ ] Determine what treasure was **freely given**, taken from the **altar**, or **stolen from the hoard**.

**Dragonfilch (book p. 28):** taking hoard treasure out of the lair triggers stage 1; each later treasure temptation requires WITS or another stage. Stage 1: reptilian eyes and covetousness. Stage 2: lose innate abilities, gain wings and d8 bite. Stage 3: lose special abilities; scales add 2 Armor to maximum 3 and gain Gift-Dice elemental breath ([SUM], GRACE halves). Stage 4: become a dragon. Returning stolen treasure cures it. Embra's voluntary gift does not trigger it; do not assume Amy can freely waive another dragon's curse. Full reference: [[01_Cave]].

### Outcomes that matter

| Outcome | Book result | Campaign continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Both restored; Embra unwilling | Restored Embra remains behind her own barrier and can bargain with treasure for freedom. Amy offers honorary knighthoods. | Bring Amy to Aurelius; use recovered evidence to plan Morwenna's fortress approach. Honors do not require detouring through a full court adventure now. |
| Embra cooperates | A genuine dragon ally and voluntary hoard reward become possible. Amy still hopes for reconciliation. | Honor the bargain. A dragon ally contributes fictionally to the finale without replacing the PCs' roles. |
| Amy dies while a dragon | Swap becomes permanent; Embra may order the PCs arrested. Amy may linger as ghost, gloomwisp, or wight; decent knights are horrified. | Morwenna's preferred outcome occurs. Do **not** retroactively prevent the death or declare the whole campaign over. Stop for a consequence/next-goal discussion: restoration quest or a changed assault plan requires fresh prep. No instant replacement key undoes death. |
| Hunt stopped, swap unresolved | Amy is safer but still in the wrong body. | Continue the key/Embra problem without artificially inventing new hunters to keep the clock alive. |
| Swap reversed before hunters arrive | The rescue succeeds through planning. | Let it succeed. Resolve Damon/Ulrik as surviving threats through choices, not a forced last fight. |

**Campaign evidence after Ulrik:** his papers can confirm Morwenna funded the scheme and mark her mountain stronghold. Keep the PPS-founder revelation for the castle. Amy's aid against the barrier is the next campaign hook, not a demand to play the castle in this packet.

## 8. Preparing subsequent sessions

At every session end fill [[princess_and_dragon_tracker]] and record only what happened. At the next opening, recap Amy's safety, the clock, key/water status, alliances, and the next chosen destination. Use the relevant location cards; there is no fixed session 11/12/13 itinerary.

Useful stopping points: calm Amy and choose a plan; earn an alliance or learn the key price; acquire water/key; compass flares red; complete the rescue and agree on Embra's future. Prefer a resolved choice over a forced mid-sentence cliffhanger.

**Level advancement:** do not silently level the PCs during prep. They remain level 3 until awarded advancement in play. The pond completion is a sensible GM checkpoint under [[05_Character_Building]], but no advancement is assumed here. Track animals seen for Filly; verify the Night Market pet and any new abilities against sheets before using them.

**Next prep needs:** [[session10_asset_checklist]] is the single asset and future module handoff for this packet. The module is intentionally unchanged.
