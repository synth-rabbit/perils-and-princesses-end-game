# Gnomish Shops

Roll d4

| Roll | Shop | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tough Breaks | A shop specializing in repairs of all types. Breaks, rips, malfunctions—all taken care of here! Getting a fix requires Spending Time. |
| 2 | The Crown Jewel | A pub serving up traditional gnomish ales. For the Princesses, the ale mugs are the size of shot glasses. |
| 3 | Glorious Greatswords | The finest purveyor of gnomish greatswords in the land. Of course, a gnomish greatsword is about the size (and effectiveness) of a dagger to a human. Regular greatswords cost 10 sp. Crystal greatswords cost 20 sp. |
| 4 | Candy’s Apples | A stall selling candied apples, manned by a gnome named Candy. She picks the apples from the crystal grove and Candy can tell the Princesses of the spirit there. |
