# Session 10 Scene 18: The Archivist’s Abandoned Cottage

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Asset checklist:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Use when reached:** this is reserve material for the entire rescue. Move an unplayed note to a later session folder without changing its basename; update its session field/title then. Nothing here is established as played.
> **Run from this note:** narration, conversations, clues and scene rules are included. Other note links are optional background or continuation scenes.

**Foundry scene:** **Abandoned Cottage** — refreshed backdrop.

## Arrival — someone left in a hurry (15–20 minutes)

> *A cottage leans beneath the branches as though the forest has been slowly taking it apart. One shutter hangs by a hinge. Inside, rain has curled the edges of papers left across a desk. Nobody has swept the floor in a very long time.*

**Direction:** Give them room to investigate without inventing a guardian. This was a field archivist's home. Shifting paths impeded the work, and the haunted stones finally frightened the writer away. There is no current resident to interrogate or hidden compulsory fight.

**Ask:** “Who checks the papers, and who looks over the rest of the cottage?” Plainly visible notes need no roll. If they choose an extended study, announce **Spend Time** and its hunt cost first.

## Four discoveries — reveal separately

### The hoard notes

> *A bundle of pages is tied with a faded ribbon. A careful dragon sketch fills the top sheet; beneath it, a warning has been underlined again and again.*

**Read as the note:**

> “Take from a dragon's hoard and the treasure's hunger goes with you. What is freely given is different. A gift carries no thief's curse.”

**GM:** Taking hoard treasure out triggers Dragonfilch: 1 reptilian eyes/covetousness; 2 lose innate abilities, gain wings/d8 bite; 3 lose special abilities, +2 Armor to maximum 3 and Gift-Dice breath [SUM], GRACE halves; 4 become a dragon. Later treasure temptations require WITS or advance a stage. Returning stolen treasure cures it. Embra's voluntary gift is exempt; Amy cannot simply waive a different dragon's curse. The cave's outside altar gems are not yet hoard treasure.

### Dain's stand against Sunder

> *A drawing shows standing stones on scorched earth. The writing around it is more careful than the other notes, as if this was the discovery that finally made the work worthwhile.*

**Read:**

> “Sir Dain and his companions held against Sunder. Their stand bought the time the reinforcements needed. Thousands lived because they did not give way.”

That is usable evidence for [[session10_17_stones_of_remembrance]]. It is not a claim that the fallen knights survived. Let a player copy or take the note.

### Pitch and the great bones

> *One dragon drawing dwarfs all the others. Its head and sweeping horns resemble the body Amy now wears.*

**Read:**

> “Pitch, ancestor of Embra. His size and strength did not save him. What remains of his skeleton now shelters the wolves of the Scorchwoods.”

This connects the Boneyard to Embra without inventing a wolf named Pitch or a separate resurrection quest.

### Gemini and Cobalt — a biased author

> *A different page has impatient corrections in its margins. The writer seems angrier with the story than interested in whether it is true.*

**Read:**

> “Stories claim Prince Gemini befriended Cobalt and left his kingdom to share a dragon's adventures. A tale for children. Far more likely that Cobalt ate him.”

**GM truth:** this is the archivist's prejudice. Hesperia knew them and can confirm their friendship. Narrate it as a claim someone wrote, never as authoritative history. Do not require the players to believe every document just because it was a handout.

## Help the players use the clues

If conversation stalls, ask: “Which of these changes what you want to do next?” Dain points to the Stones; the friendship points to the Orchard; the curse points back to how they handle Embra and her property. Do not turn all four into required quests before a rescue.

**For Marigold:** “The account names people saved by someone who believed he had failed.” **For Filly:** “The den had a history before it became the wolves' home.” **For Vega:** “The notes distinguish an enchantment's conditions from what people assume about it.” These are observations, not dictated conclusions.

## Exit

> *The papers rustle as you leave. Behind you, the cottage returns to silence. You carry a little less uncertainty than when you entered—and perhaps something a dead man has been waiting to hear.*

## Hunt procedure at this scene


Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
