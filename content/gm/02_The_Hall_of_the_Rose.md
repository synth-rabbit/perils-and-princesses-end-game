![[maps/morwennas_castle/rosehold_2_hall_of_the_rose_gm.png]]

> **Runbook:** [[rosehold_runbook]] · **Tracker:** [[rosehold_tracker]] · **Parent:** [[Rosehold]] · **Foundry scene art:** module `build/art/maps/map_rosehold_2_hall_of_the_rose.png` (6144 px on the long side)

The keep's ground floor: the old Society's public rooms. This is where the castle tells the players who built it. **Foundry:** module scene **Castle Great Hall** as backdrop for C2, the floor plan for the rest. 75–90 minutes.

## C1. The Vestibule and the Oath Door

> *Double doors of white oak, twice your height, with no handle, no lock and no hinges you can see. Carved across both leaves, in letters worn soft by hands, are four lines. The last line has been gouged out with something sharp.*

> **On the door:**
> *Where there is peril, I will go.*
> *Where there is sorrow, I will stay.*
> *What I am given, I will share.*
> *~~And no one stands alone.~~*

It is the **PPS oath**, the one the party swore when Daisy pinned them. The door opens for anyone wearing a pin who speaks all four lines, including the one somebody tried to remove.

- If nobody remembers the fourth line: a **WITS** test recalls it, or Daisy (called, or asked beforehand) supplies it. Pippin's key, Billy Goat, or Vega's Shape through the wall beside it also work; forcing it is +1 Cold.
- **Trap:** speaking only three lines and pushing makes the gouged line bleed frost: everyone Nearby tests **RESOLVE** or becomes Weary.

Let the players notice that the oath on the Queen's front door is theirs.

## C2. The Great Hall

> *The hall is the size of a church and as cold. Banners hang from the beams, stiff with frost, and on every one, faded almost to nothing, is a silver rose. In the middle of the floor stands a round table with five chairs. Four are pushed back as though people left in a hurry. The fifth is wrapped so thickly in black thorns you can barely see the wood.*

Two [[Thornbound_Sentinel|Thornbound Sentinels]] flank the far door. They step forward when anyone touches the table or passes its midpoint.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Thornbound_Sentinel]] ×2 | 8 | 10 | 3 | Twice: Thorned Halberd (d8) | Fire ignores Armor. On death: Nearby test GRACE or grappled. Back to Bed works |

- **The salute:** a hero who speaks the oath to a Sentinel is not attacked first by it, and if *every* hero does so, both Sentinels ground their halberds and stand aside. They were Knight-Protectors. They remember.
- **The table:** five places, five names cut into the wood in front of the chairs: **HILDE · AETHELGARD · LIRAEL · WREN · MORWENNA**. In front of four of them is a small tarnished hollow where a pin once sat. Morwenna's place is under the thorns.
- **The banners** bear the Society's ranks in thread: *Initiate, Field Operative, Knight-Protector, Luminary.*

Give the room a moment. The reveal is not a speech; it is five chairs. If a player says it out loud ("she was one of us"), confirm it: *"She was the first of you."*

**Morwenna speaks** here for the first time, from the frost on the table top (roll or choose from [[Rosehold_Whispers]]; number 2 is written for this room).

## C3. The Banquet Hall

> *A long table laid for forty. The food is all still there: a roast swan, a tower of sugared plums, wine in the glasses, every bit of it under a skin of ice. At the head of the table a woman of blue light is singing, very softly, to a closed door that isn't there any more.*

- **The frozen feast:** eating or drinking anything is a **WITS** test or Befuddled (Poison Apple rules for the plums: RESOLVE; success Weary, failure enchanted sleep). The **gold coupe with emeralds** (200 SP) is at the head of the table.
- **⭐ Filly and Villum:** if Filly so much as rearranges a plate, the bargain triggers: Villum appears in the next chair, appalled. *"Darling. You invited me to* this*?"* He will, as a professional courtesy, thaw one dish into something safe: a hot meal for the party.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Echo_of_a_Luminary]]: **Lirael the Songkeeper** | 10 | 14 | 0 | Grief Wail (d6 Nearby, RESOLVE for half) | Can be freed |
| [[Frostbind_Wisp]] ×4 | 3 | 12 | 0 | Frost Touch (d4) + RESOLVE or Weary | Hang over the table like candles; attack if the song stops |

**Freeing Lirael:** she sings three verses and stops before the fourth, every time. Somebody has to finish it. Any honest attempt: **GRACE** test, with advantage if two heroes sing together. (Filly answered a brownie's riddle with a song once. This is the same trick.)

> **Lirael's song, the three verses she sings:**
> *Open the door, my dear, my dear,*
> *the fire is lit and the table's laid.*
> *Open the door, there's no one here*
> *but the four of us, and we're not afraid.*
>
> *She stops. The fourth verse is yours.*

> *When you finish, she turns. "Silverspire," she says to no one, smiling. "They'll have my voice in the family for years." Then, to you: "She could still hear me. She told me so, the last night. She said it hurt."*

Gives **the Songkeeper's Chime**. Cold −1. If **Daisy** is called here, she hears her great-aunt's voice; no test is needed, and Daisy keeps her call.

## C4. The Kitchens (warm room)

> *Heat. Actual heat. A hearth the size of a carriage with a fire in it, a pot the size of a bathtub over the fire, and, frozen in the act of stirring it, six goblins on a stepladder, all staring at you.*

Six **goblin scullions** ([[Forest_Goblin|forest goblins]], HP 6, SAVE 10, Armor 1; armed with ladles, d4). They are the goblins whose journal the party read in session 3: the ones "fleeing an evil queen from up north." They did not flee far enough.

They do not want to fight. Their head cook, **Skillet**, wants three things: to not be reported, to know if it is true that the Barrow goblins are dead (it is), and for somebody to taste the stew.

- **Friendly goblins** offer the stew (a hot meal; ends Weary), the run of the kitchen, and the **dumbwaiter**: a rope hoist that goes up to the Solar (E6) and down to the Guardroom (D3). One hero at a time; **GRACE** to ride it silently.
- **A Rest is possible here**, once. It costs +2 Cold.
- They know the castle: "Wizard's on the third floor with his mud babies. Black knight on the tower stair. Her Majesty don't eat. We cook anyway. Nobody told us to stop."

## C5. The Hall of Portraits

> *A long gallery. Four portraits on one wall, each slashed corner to corner. Opposite them, a single large canvas, untouched: five young women in travelling clothes, arm in arm, laughing at whoever was painting them.*

- The four slashed portraits are the founders, with name plates. Under each, something left on the floor: a shield strap, a seed packet, a tuning fork, a pair of dividers.
- **The group portrait:** the one in the middle, with her arm around two others and a rose pinned to her collar, is unmistakably the woman from Tawny's nightmare, forty years younger and happy.
- **Take it.** Carrying the portrait into the throne room gives advantage on the first test made to reach her.
- **Trap:** the frames of the slashed four are **Mine!**-warded. Taking one down: **RESOLVE** or it flies from your hands and the hero's most valuable carried item flies with it, up to the Queen's Solar (E6).

**Door** to the Servants' Stair.

## C6. The Servants' Stair

A plain stone stair **down to the Undercroft** (D0). An [[Ice_Gargoyle]] crouches on the newel post at the bottom of the first flight.

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Ice_Gargoyle]] | 8 | 8 | 3 | Twice: Bite (d8), Claws (d6) | Fire ignores Armor. Blunt and Vein-Breaker +1 per die. Wakes to warmth |

**Secret passage** to the Grand Stair (C7): a servant's shortcut behind a tapestry. **WITS** to find, or Wren's True Map, or ask a goblin.

## C7. The Grand Stair

> *A staircase wide enough for six abreast sweeps up into the dark. Every step is sheathed in ice so clear you can see the carpet under it, and the banister is one long frozen thorn.*

- **Ice slide:** climbing is a **GRACE** test each; a failure slides the hero to the bottom for d6 and a great deal of noise (+1 Cold, once). Vega's Shape (grit, or stone treads raised through the ice), Spider Touch, Natural Climber, or a rope from the top make it safe.
- **Two more gargoyles** on the upper newels if the Cold is 4 or higher.
- **The banister** is alive. Anyone who grabs it to steady themselves takes d4.

**Exit:** up to the Landing (E7).
