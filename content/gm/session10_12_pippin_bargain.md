# Session 10 Scene 12: Optional: Pippin's Bargain

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 15–20 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**GM map confirmed:** use your existing Gnome Village map. No replacement map is needed. The module scene named below is a reference/fallback.

**Foundry scene:** **Gnome Village**. 🔗 [[04_Gnomes]] · [[Pippin_Cobbleton]] · [[Gnomish_Shops]]

> *Under the roots, tiny shop windows shine in rows. A gnome standing on a stool peers at your description of the necklace. “Oh, that sort of trouble. Yes, I can help. No, you won't like the ingredient.”*

**Direction:** Market folk direct the party to Pippin. Let them present the problem and, if they have it, return his walking stick. He can explain the locks and make a replacement; he need not be the original maker.

- **Terms:** enchanted water from the Wish Fish's Pool **plus 50 SP**. Returning the stick or earning a favor waives the fee, not the water. Give **Handout D** from [[princess_and_dragon_handouts]].
- **Practical instruction:** both wearers must be Nearby to unlock and remove the necklaces. A key cannot restore Amy remotely. The original key on Embra still works.
- **Next lead:** ask about the Pool and the village's shortcut to it. Pippin dislikes dealing with the fish. Candy's candied apples can affect captains, but are unsuitable for the fish's live-worm demand.
- **Clock:** brief conversation is not automatically Spend Time. Repairs do take it; replacement crafting takes one Spend Time under this prep's stated table ruling once supplied. State costs before commitment.

⭐ **Vega:** discuss the craft and the key's operation. ⭐ **Marigold:** if the players approach nervous Tifa, kindness may draw out her campaign testimony about Ulrik; this is helpful evidence, not a mandatory extra key quest.

**Good stopping point:** the group knows the price and chooses either the water route or Embra's original key. Do not assign them both routes as required chores.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### From the market to the workshop

> *The ceiling rises just enough for you to straighten your backs. Lamps glimmer between roots overhead. Someone has hung a sign at human eye level: PLEASE NOTICE THE PEOPLE BELOW YOU.*

A market gnome points them toward Pippin without a test when they describe magical necklaces.

> “Custom Creations. Follow the hammering. If you hear an argument, he's probably talking to a hinge.”

**Pippin — curious first, businesslike second:**

> “Tell me exactly what happened. What she wore. What the key touched. No, begin before the screaming.”

After the explanation:

> “Two wearers. Two necklaces. One locked enchantment. Yes, I can make a key. First, though: if the dragon still has the original, it would save you a great deal of running about.”

### His offer, in full

> “Water from the enchanted pool, and fifty silver for the work. Bring me the water and I can begin. Both wearers must be close together when you use the key; unlock the enchantment, then remove both necklaces.”

**If shown his stick:**

> “My stick! Did those wolves—oh, look at the tooth marks. Never mind. No fee. Bring me the water and we'll call the silver settled.”

**If they cannot pay and lack the stick:**

> “The wolves near the great bones have something of mine. A jeweled walking stick. Bring it back intact and I shall owe you a favor.”

That is a suggested favor already supported by the encounter, not an additional quest after payment. Returning the stick never replaces the water.

**About the fish:**

> “He has all the water in the world and behaves as though each drop has a sentimental value. Ask what he wants. Then ask what he actually wants.”

Pippin shows them the tunnel to the false rock beside the Pool. If they use it with his instructions, opening that entrance needs no test. Once supplied, crafting takes **one Spend Time** under this campaign's prep ruling; explain the clock cost before they commit.

### Tifa, if they seek another witness

**Tifa — hesitant until someone takes her seriously:**

> “Ulrik commissioned them. I recognized the work, but I didn't understand what he meant to do with it. I should have asked more questions.”

If encouraged:

> “I can tell you what I know. You don't have to believe a frightened princess just because she is frightened. You can show the knights how it happened.”

**GM:** This testimony is the campaign connection, not printed proof that Tifa personally witnessed the swap. She can explain the mechanism and commissioner, not every private exchange between Embra and Amy. Her help is optional; do not add a second key or trust gate.

### Four useful shops without a lookup

| Shop | Say / offer |
|---|---|
| Tough Breaks | “Broken? We can mend it. Dissolved? You may need to buy a new one.” Repairs Spend Time; do not recreate the PCs' lost weapons for free. |
| The Crown Jewel | “Three ales? Human size or sensible size?” Gnome mugs are shot-glass sized to PCs. No automatic drunkenness rule. |
| Glorious Greatswords | Gnomish greatsword works as a human dagger; 10 SP ordinary, 20 SP crystal. |
| Candy's Apples | “Ask the lady of the orchard before you pick. I do, and I still have all my fingers.” Candied fruit affects living captains but lacks the live worm the fish wants. Agree unstated prices openly. |

Do not charge Spend Time for each line of conversation. Charge actual repairs, crafting or prolonged shopping as announced. Record payments from real party funds, not unconfirmed mine loot.

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
