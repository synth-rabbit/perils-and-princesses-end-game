# Session 10 Scene 14: Optional: Allies at Camp

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 15–25 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**GM map confirmed:** use your existing Knight Camp map. No replacement map is needed. The module scene named below is a reference/fallback.

**Foundry scene:** **Knight Camp**. 🔗 [[10_Camp]] · [[Whisper_Table]]

> *Armor shines beside a smoky fire. From one tent comes a hopeful tune, followed by a wrong note and an apologetic cough. Beyond the guy ropes, hunters are still arguing about which path leads to the dragon.*

**Direction:** Use established movements to decide who is here. If none are established, Raphael is a useful choice for a short social scene; that is a GM staging choice, not a guaranteed encounter. If the PCs specifically seek Finnian/Embra, use the WITS search procedure below and honor success. Do not substitute Raphael to delay their chosen plan.

**Raphael present:** he already suspects something is wrong with the princess and readily considers the swap. Michelle and Parker support his musical ambitions. Let the PCs ask for a concrete contribution: a distraction, assistance or healing supplies from Adagio's **d4 potions**. Record what he actually gives or promises. A genuine alliance lowers the tracker by 1 and removes his party from active hunters; do not award that reduction repeatedly.

**Finnian and Embra present:** show the carriage, attendants, eight guards and Robin. Finnian needs evidence, not just an accusation or Daisy's letter. Embra has the original key and her own motives; use her dialogue and profile below if approached. A quiet bargain may be more useful than a fight with the retinue.

⭐ **Marigold:** a conversation with Michelle about choosing one's own life can build trust. Give each PC room to contribute evidence, scout, or propose a plan.

**Good stopping point:** an alliance or bargain is settled, or the group has a concrete plan for reaching Embra. Record knight dispositions, supplies, tracker changes and Amy's safety before ending.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Choose who is present, then open that conversation

Use known movements first. If the party deliberately searches for a named hunting party, test **WITS**: success places that party at the next keyed location unless impossible; failure produces a Wrinkle. Do not nullify a success with another random roll. Otherwise use the travel checks or choose a plausible camp party; Raphael makes a short, friendly introduction when no movement is established.

### Raphael, Michelle and Parker

**Raphael — relieved to be useful without killing something:**

> “I was beginning to think I was the only one who noticed. The princess used to care when someone was unhappy. Now she mostly cares whether her carriage is comfortable.”

On hearing about Amy:

> “Then we have been sent to kill the person we swore to protect. Right. No. We are not doing that.”

**Michelle, when Marigold or another PC asks why she keeps her visor down:**

> “There are people who think they should choose my whole life for me. Sir Raphael disagreed. I would like to do the same for someone else.”

She need not reveal her history to strangers automatically. If trust develops, she explains that Raphael helped her escape an arranged marriage. Parker, given a second chance after theft, keeps the tone light:

> “A rescue. Excellent. Much better than a hunt. Fewer songs rhyme with 'wrongful execution.'”

Ask what aid the PCs want. Adagio carries **d4 healing potions**, each restoring d6 HP. Roll stock once and record any transfer. An alliance can supply a distraction or assistance; don't assume Raphael can control all other knights. Genuine alliance: **−1 hunt**, remove his party from active hunters. Repeating the promise earns no further reduction.

### Finnian and the false princess

> *Eight guards stand near a polished carriage. A squire is straightening a banner that is already straight. Beside him, a gleaming knight smiles as though he has been waiting for an audience all morning.*

Finnian has Robin, eight guards, four mules, Embra and three attendants. Approach can be social; all thirteen people need not become combatants.

**Finnian:** “You have news of the dragon? Splendid. You may have caught us at the beginning of a very important deed.”

If accused of hunting Amy:

> “Then show me. I will not gamble her life on a stranger's claim, whichever way that claim points.”

Offer him checkable evidence: the compass points away from the carriage; the same necklaces; Scamp's recognition; Amy's personal account; Tifa's testimony if actually learned. Daisy's dispatch alone is not automatic success. If he accepts, give him a way to act honorably:

> “Then my duty is to bring her home. Tell me what you need guarded.”

**Embra interrupts:** “A bewitched trinket, a talking animal, and a very useful story. Is that all it takes to overturn my orders?” She retains agency; no single clue makes her confess.

### A private bargain with Embra

If they secure privacy or make a credible offer:

> “Do you know what it is to hear music every night instead of hunters? To be brought food without having to frighten someone first? I am not eager to go back.”

If asked about Amy:

> “She was kind to me.” A pause. “I know what that makes this.”

Offer a believable path to safety, comfort or friendship outside the stolen life. She may cooperate; do not demand a particular speech or declare her redeemed. She **has the original key**. Both wearers must be Nearby to unlock the pair; remove both necklaces, then handle the cave barrier separately. If the barrier is still active after restoration, dragon Embra is trapped and can instruct an outside helper to dispel it. Taking the key alone does not remotely restore Amy.

If threatened publicly, she can invoke her guards and seek escape using pixie dust. Do not make her spend all resources before the PCs act, or quietly teleport her away to preserve the plot.

### Stella or Damon arrives instead

**Stella:** “Evidence first. Then tell me how you intend to keep everyone alive.” She needs convincing evidence and demonstrated competence. Respect her achievements; comparing her to her sisters worsens the conversation. A sincere alliance earns −1 and removes her party from active hunters.

**Ulrik:** “An extraordinary tale. Perhaps we should examine this dragon together.” His cooperation can be a ruse to locate Amy. Damon stands close enough to receive commands. Separating them beyond a Stone's Throw leaves Damon continuing his last order, not deactivated. Damon cannot be talked out of his purpose or pacified with an apple; Ulrik is living and can be tricked with one. Do not treat his false alliance as removing the threat.

### Spell and escape quick reference

For Ulrik's spells, [DICE] is the number of Gift Dice rolled and [SUM] their total. He has 3 GD; 1–3 returns a die, 4–6 consumes it. Casting consumes his normal action; no extra action merely because he is a villain.

- **Animate:** [DICE] Nearby objects obey for [SUM] minutes.
- **Restoration:** at Hand, restore [SUM] HP or cure [DICE] Ailments/injuries; also mends a broken object.
- **Disguise:** alter [DICE] creatures at Hand into another humanoid appearance for [SUM]×10 minutes; WITS save where applicable. It does not change stats, abilities or bodies as the necklaces do.
- **Haste:** visible target within a Stone's Throw doubles speed and gains an extra Action and Reaction each round for [SUM] rounds; Weary afterward.
- **Embra's Ring of Fire:** 1 GD Fireball within a Stone's Throw, 20-foot radius, [SUM] damage / half on GRACE. Recharge in hot embers until they burn out. She risks her guards and cover if she uses it indiscriminately.
- **Embra's Pixie Dust:** Float for d4 hours; fly in any direction at running speed, descend gently when it ends. The item supplies this duration rather than the normal spell's rounds.
- **Her healing potion:** restores d6 HP; no automatic use before players can act.

### Gossip to give without opening a table

Use one line when they mingle: “She's the princess. We follow orders.” / “Do not compare Stella with her sisters.” / “Raphael brought a lute to a dragon hunt.” / “That hooded fellow scares me more than the beast.” / “Gnomish ale means gnomes somewhere nearby.” / “Don't steal from a hoard.” / “Stay away from the laughing flowers.” / “There's enchanted water in the forest.” / “The haunted stones aren't worth any purse.” / “Damon doesn't fight like a man.” Rumor is a lead, not proof of all its implications.

### End on a concrete commitment

**Ask:** “What have you agreed to do, and what have they agreed to do?” Record supplies, payment, promises and who knows the cave's location. Let a good plan succeed. If the pins turn red, interrupt leisurely negotiation with the immediate rescue problem.

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
