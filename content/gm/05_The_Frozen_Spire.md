> **Runbook:** [[rosehold_runbook]] · **Tracker:** [[rosehold_tracker]] · **Parent:** [[Rosehold]] · **Foundry scene art:** module `build/art/maps/map_rosehold_4_frozen_spire.png` (6144 px on the long side)

The top of the castle: the stair, the champion, the throne. **Foundry:** the floor plan for F1–F2; module scene **Castle Throne Room** for F3. This is a whole session: 2½ hours with the ending.

**Before climbing:** this is the last chance to Rest (E4), spend calls on preparation, or go back for anything. Say so.

## F1. The Stair of Vows

> *A spiral stair in a tower with no glass in its windows. The wind comes through like knives. On each landing a line is carved into the wall, and you know them now without reading.*

Four landings, four lines of the oath. On each, the wind rises and the stair tests whoever is climbing:

| Landing | The line | The test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Where there is peril, I will go.* | **RESOLVE** against the wind, or pushed back a landing and d4. |
| 2 | *Where there is sorrow, I will stay.* | The stair shows each hero the easy way down. **WITS**, or Befuddled. |
| 3 | *What I am given, I will share.* | Ice underfoot. **GRACE**, or slide; **a friend may spend a Heart Die to catch you, and should.** |
| 4 | *And no one stands alone.* | No test, if the heroes arrive together. Anyone who went ahead alone tests all three Virtues. |

Roped together, or mounted on Mari Lwyd's steeds, the party has advantage on all of it.

## F2. The Antechamber of the Thorn Mark

> *A bare round room. Across the far door, as still as the wall behind him, stands a knight in black armour with an axe in each hand. On his breastplate is a throne made of thorns. Filly has seen him before, in Tawny's dream.*

| Monster | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Sir_Damon_the_Unyielding]] | 8 | 10 | 3 | Twice: Battle axe (d10) | Follows his last order: *let no one pass.* Cannot be persuaded |
| [[Thornbound_Sentinel]] ×2 | 8 | 10 | 3 | Twice: Thorned Halberd (d8) | Fire ignores Armor. On death: GRACE or grappled |

**Finale tuning for Damon (optional, recommended for level 4):** HP 16, and **the first time he drops to 0 he gets back up at 8** as the blood-clay knits. He does not get up a second time, and does not get up at all if the blow was silver, or if Vega's Shape or Shatter Vein dealt it. He is blood and *earth*.

- **⭐ Vega:** this is his fight. Vein-Breaker ignores a point of Damon's Armor; Shatter Vein strips it; and a level 4 earth elementalist is **immune to an earth construct's damage** if the GM rules the axes as part of him. Rule it that way.
- **⭐ Filly:** Tawny dreamed this knight, and Tawny did not live to meet him. If Filly carries anything made from **Tawny's silver claws or teeth**, it strikes him with advantage, and the first time it hits, tell Filly's player that Tawny has been waiting. Major Ursa, who never knew Tawny, attacks him anyway, because Filly does.
- **Ulrik**, if spared and brought along, can order him to stand down. Damon lowers his axes, and then says the only full sentence he has ever spoken: *"What do I do now."* Nobody has an answer prepared. Let the table find one.
- **The Sentinels** will answer the oath as in the Great Hall.

The door behind him is not locked.

## F3. The Throne Room

> *The floor is ice, so clear and so still that the whole room is doubled in it. Rose petals hang in the air, hundreds of them, frozen where they were falling. At the far end a throne of black thorns has grown up and over an older, smaller chair, and on it sits a woman with frost-white hair and a tarnished pin at her collar, half swallowed by briar. She has been watching the door.*
>
> *"You came all this way together," says Queen Morwenna. "Good. I wanted you to be together when I showed you."*

### Who is in the room

**Finale stats (GM ruling 2026-10-05: much harder than the book).** The printed Evil Queen is HP 10; a level 4 party ends that in one Gift. These replace it. Full entries: [[Evil_Queen]], [[Morwenna_Frost_Crowned]].

| | HP | SAVE | Armor | Attack | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Evil_Queen|Queen Morwenna]] | 30 | 15 | 1 | **Acts twice a round.** Dagger (d8) and a spell on each | 6 GD, up to 3 per spell. Cannot drop below 1 HP while a Vow Pillar stands |
| **Vow Pillar** ×4 (fewer if Echoes were freed) | 10 | — | 2 | None | Each standing pillar: she regains 3 HP at the start of the round |
| [[Thornbound_Sentinel]] ×2 | 8 | 10 | 3 | Twice: Thorned Halberd (d8) | Always present. Four if the Cold is 4+ |
| [[Morwenna_Frost_Crowned|Morwenna, Frost-Crowned]] | 45 (+5 per unfreed Echo) | 16 | 2 | **Acts twice a round.** Two Ice Shards (d10, ranged) or one and a spell | A passed SAVE means *no* damage. Winter's Breath. Aura of cold |
| [[Vine_Crawler]] ×6 per round | 2 | 6 | 0 | Vine Whip (d4) | From the throne, while the Frost-Crowned stands |

**Amy** is here if the party brought her (6 HP; keep her alive). **Aurelius** cannot climb the stair; his light comes through the Rose Window if someone opens it (F4).

**The four Vow Pillars.** Round the dais stand four columns of black ice, each with a founder's shape frozen inside: shield, shears, lute, map. They are the broken promises the curse feeds on. **A pillar whose Echo was freed is already dark and cracked: it does not count.** So a party that freed all four faces none, and a party that freed none faces all four.
- While any pillar stands, **Morwenna cannot be reduced below 1 HP**, and she **regains 3 HP per standing pillar** at the start of each round.
- Pillars have HP 10, Armor 2. Silver, fire, Vein-Breaker and blunt weapons ignore the Armor. A hero holding that founder's **keepsake** or speaking that founder's **whisper** shatters her pillar with a touch.
- When a pillar breaks, everyone Nearby to it tests **GRACE** or takes d6 from flying ice.

### Phase one: the Queen

She talks first. She wants to be understood. She will answer three questions honestly before anyone draws steel, and if attacked mid-sentence she finishes the sentence.

- *Why?* "Because I built a whole Society on a promise, and when I needed it kept, four people I loved stood outside a door and discussed what to do about me."
- *What do you want?* "To be right. If I am right, then it was not my fault that I could not open the door."
- *Why Amy?* "Because she has what I had. I wanted to see it fail in someone else's hands. It is failing rather slowly."

**She acts twice each round:** once before the heroes who passed their initiative WITS test, once after everyone. On each action she may move, strike with the dagger (d8) and cast one spell of up to 3 Gift Dice. She has **6 Gift Dice**; they return on 1–3 as usual, and all return if she spends a whole action seated on the throne.

**The throne protects her.** While she is on the dais, all damage to her is halved (round down) and she casts **Bubble** as a free Reaction at 1 die every round. Getting her off the dais (antics, the pixies, Billy Goat, tipping the dais with Shape) or cutting the thorns (the Shears, fire, Vein-Breaker; three Actions' worth) ends this.

**Her spells** (heroes test the Virtue shown):

| Spell | Effect at 3 dice |
|---|---|
| **Frostfall** (Fireball) | 20-foot radius, [SUM] damage, half on **GRACE**. Her opener, at the largest group. |
| **Mine!** | **RESOLVE** or a held item flies to her hand: Blitzenaxt, the spear, the staff. It lands on the dais. |
| **Thorns** (Tangle) | 30-foot radius of briar for [SUM] rounds. **RESOLVE** or held fast. |
| **Splinters** (Magic Dart) | 3 darts, each dealing its die face, **cannot be dodged**. She uses these on whoever is protecting Amy. |
| **Bubble** | +[DICE] Armor for [SUM] rounds. Reaction. |
| **Disguise** | Becomes Amy, or one of the heroes, when someone is about to strike. **WITS** to see through; the Spectacles see through at once. |

**Other habits:** she targets Heart Dice. When a hero spends one to help a friend, she answers with Splinters at the helper. She drinks her potion (2d4+4) at 10 HP or less. She never attacks Filly's animal companion first.

**Smooch and other mercies:** Marigold's Smooch cannot calm 30 HP outright. But if its [SUM] is at least **half her current HP**, *Morwenna* lowers the dagger and says, puzzled, *"Oh."* She loses both actions next round and the throne's protection drops for that round. If no pillar stands when this happens, the curse, which was never her, stands up without her: go straight to phase two, and it counts as reaching her.

**Phase one ends** when she is at 0 HP (only possible once every pillar is down), or by the Smooch above.

### Phase two: the Frost-Crowned

> *The throne splits. Ice goes up through her like a tree growing in a single breath: a crown of it, jagged, taller than she is. Her eyes are open and there is nobody in them. Somewhere under all of it, very small, a woman's voice says, "I can't — it won't let me —" and then the room is full of thorns.*

Everyone Nearby tests **GRACE** or takes 2d6. She has full HP in the new form and her Gift Dice are restored. Six [[Vine_Crawler|vine crawlers]] rise from the throne each round.

**She still acts twice a round.** Each action: two Ice Shards (d10, ranged, GRACE to avoid), or one Shard and a spell.

- **The ice takes it.** When she passes a SAVE against a Gift or spell, she takes **no damage** instead of half. Weapons work normally against Armor 2.
- **Aura of cold.** A hero who starts their turn Nearby to her tests **RESOLVE** or becomes Weary. Frostproof cloaks and Liquid Courage give advantage.
- **Winter's Breath.** Once, and again when she first falls below half HP. **She inhales for a full action first: tell the players.** On her next action, everything in a wide cone a Stone's Throw long takes **16 damage, 8 on GRACE**. The Dragonfire Shield does not stop cold. A wall of stone does. So does being behind the throne.
- **The floor.** The ice-mirror floor cracks as she moves. From round three, moving more than Nearby in the room is a **GRACE** test or fall prone.
- **She cannot leave the room** and will not strike a hero who is, at that moment, telling her a true memory.

**Two ways to end it.**

**Fight.** Reduce the Frost-Crowned to 0 by any means. The ice shatters; Morwenna falls with it and does not get up. The curse dies with her, the castle begins to thaw, and in the window Aurelius lowers his head. *"She was my friend once. I wish I could have saved her."* The party has won. It is a true ending and should not be played as a failure.

**Reach her.** Friendship damage is the only damage that **breaks the ice without harming the woman under it**, and it is the only damage she cannot SAVE against. If the *last* point of damage is friendship damage, or the kiss is given, she lives.

| Tool | Effect |
|---|---|
| **A Heart Die and a true memory**, told aloud | **2d6** to the curse, ignores Armor, no SAVE. Any hero, once per round each. |
| **Amy's Powerful Friendship** | 2d8 the same way, once per round. |
| **Each whisper the party holds** (0–4) | When a hero quotes it to her: she loses one of her two actions that round. Once per whisper. |
| **The group portrait** from C5 | Shown to her, she stops attacking for a round. |
| **The Queen's hand mirror**, turned on her | It shows her worst quality, and what it shows is a frightened girl. She loses a full round. |
| **The hero who was kind to "the girl" in the cells** | Advantage on everything they do to reach her; she will not target them. |
| **The First Silver Rose Pin**, pulled from the thorns and held out to her | She can speak for one round, and Winter's Breath does not recharge. Ask her something. |
| **⭐ Marigold's True Love's Kiss** | All four Gift Dice. If the party holds **all four whispers** (the journal counts as two), it ends the curse outright: "another similar deadly Curse." If not, it deals 6d6 friendship damage instead. Works once, ever. |
| **Aurelius's light**, if the Rose Window is open | The vine crawlers stop coming and the aura of cold ends. |
| **The Frostbind Well purified** | She has Armor 0, no Bubble, and no second Winter's Breath. |

**What this adds up to.** A party that explored (freed Echoes, purified the Well, found the journal and the mirror) meets a hard fight with a dozen levers. A party that ran straight up the stairs meets a Queen who cannot die, heals 12 a round, and then turns into something with 65 HP. That is the intended gap. If they arrive unprepared, Morwenna says so: *"You came straight here. You didn't look at any of it. That's what they did, too."* Wren's True Map and Nim's Poof! both exist so that a party can leave and come back.

**Dials, if it is still too soft or too hard on the night:**

| Dial | Softer | As written | Harder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Queen HP / Frost-Crowned HP | 20 / 30 | 30 / 45 | 40 / 60 |
| Actions per round | 1 | 2 | 2, plus Splinters as a free Reaction |
| Pillar regeneration | 2 each | 3 each | 5 each |
| Winter's Breath | 12 / 6 | 16 / 8 | 20 / 10, as a dragon's |
| Sentinels | none | 2 | 4, and Damon gets up a second time and follows the party in |

**⭐ Filly:** the Frost-Crowned is not an animal and Filly cannot talk it down. But in **Wild Form** she can be the thing the Queen's nightmare sent Damon to kill: a dragon in the throne room, if the GM allows the form, with [SUM] HP. The curse wanted "her fire." Let Filly bring it. Filly's Roar also still works on the Sentinels.

**⭐ Vega:** the throne is thorn, but the dais is stone. Shape can tip it, split it, or raise a wall that stops Winter's Breath cold. A summoned earth elemental can simply hold Amy and refuse to let go. Vein-Breaker ignores the pillars' Armor.

**⭐ Marigold:** she is the party's Heart Dice. In this room Heart Dice are the only damage that gets the good ending, and Marigold has five. Aurelius's one-time restoration of all Heart Dice (through any pin) is meant for exactly this fight.

### If she is saved

> *The crown comes apart like spring ice on a river: all at once, and with a sound like applause. The petals that have hung in the air for forty years finish falling. And the woman on the floor, who is not young any more and never got to be anything in between, looks up at three strangers wearing her pin.*
>
> *"You came," says Morwenna. "After all this time. Someone came."*

She has 1 HP and no magic. She remembers everything she did. What happens to her is the table's to decide; see the epilogue in [[rosehold_runbook]].

## F4. The Rose Window

A balcony behind a great round window of rose-coloured glass, iced shut. Opening it is an Action and a **RESOLVE** test (or one blow from anything blunt). It lets in Aurelius's light, a view of the whole realm, and, if the party is leaving in a hurry, Mari Lwyd.
