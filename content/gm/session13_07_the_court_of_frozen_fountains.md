# Session 13 Scene 7 (Optional): The Court of Frozen Fountains

> **Hub:** [[session13_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session13_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 15–20 min, only if the session is running early · **Foundry:** module scene **Rosehold 1 — The Outer Ward** (B1)
> 🔗 Castle note: [[01_The_Outer_Ward]], B1

Run this only if the gatehouse finishes with time to spare. It is a good place to stop: the party sees the curse's victims for the first time and catches its first guard.

## Entry

> *A wide yard under a hard white sky. Two fountains stand frozen mid-leap. Between them, life-size and rimed with frost, are statues of people running toward the keep: a cook with a ladle, a stable boy, a knight with her helm under her arm. They are not statues.*

## What is here

- **The frozen household:** a dozen of Rosehold's old servants and knights, caught by the curse the night it broke loose. Alive, asleep, and cold. ⭐ **Marigold's Tears** thaw one per tear; **True Love's Kiss is wasted here**. If the castle is saved, they all wake.
- **Wisps:** d4+2 Frostbind Wisps drift among the frozen people and come for anyone who lingers.
- **The patrol** (four Thornsworn guards and a sergeant) arrives on the **second round** of any fight here.
- **The fountains:** the one nearer the keep still has liquid water under the ice. ⭐ **Marigold's staff recharges in it.**

- A fight in the open court is loud: **+1 Cold**. While the glass ravens in the Raven Tower are alive, anything done here is seen.
- ⭐ **Filly:** **Roar** on Thornsworn lasts only one round (the thorn steadies them). Major, on the other hand, is very large and they have never seen a polar bear.
- ⭐ **Vega:** the court's flagstones answer to Shape; sinking a patrol to the knees ends the fight without a death.

## A captured guard

Thorn pulled, he is terrified and helpful:

> *"I don't know why I'm here. I took the Baron's coin to hunt three princesses and then there was a woman in a mirror and then it's — is it winter? It was summer."*

He knows the patrol route (court → barracks → court), that "the wizard" is upstairs, and that *"the black knight never sleeps, he just stands on the stair."* If Filly mentions Tawny's dream, he goes white: "Thorns on his chest. A throne of them. Yes."

## End here

> *Somewhere above you, behind a window in the keep, a curtain moves. Then the ice on the nearest fountain shows a face that is not yours, and a woman's voice, tired and certain, says:*

Roll on [[Rosehold_Whispers]] or use line 1: *"You wear my pin. Did they tell you who made it? No. They wouldn't."*

Cut. Session 14 opens in the court.
