# Session 10 Scene 20: Conclusion — What Comes Out of the Woods

## Choose the ending that actually happened (15–25 minutes)

This scene closes the rescue chapter and connects it to Morwenna. It does not force the castle into the same evening. The landscape backdrop has no fixed cast, so it works for relief, a fragile truce, or a somber departure. Never read the success version if Amy remains trapped or has died.

## Amy restored and safe

> *Beyond the last dark trunks, morning lies across the road. For the first time since you entered the Scorchwoods, nobody is listening for pursuit. Amy pauses at the edge of the light. Scamp climbs onto her shoulder as though correcting a very long mistake.*

**Amy:**

> “There were moments when I thought all I had left was waiting. You came back. Whatever happened out there—you came back.”

Let each PC respond. Ask Amy's player-facing question:

> “What did it cost you? Not just silver. What do I need to understand?”

Use the answers to acknowledge actual bargains, fear and sacrifices rather than reciting predetermined heroics.

### The honor she can give

Amy offers honorary knighthood in Drakenshire. Keep the ceremony intimate unless they want something grander.

> “If the word knight means someone who protects another when it would be easier to walk away, then you have already earned it. I would like my kingdom to know.”

A PC can decline without losing her friendship. Titles do not imply a purse, land, new stats or automatic leveling. Record the honors actually accepted.

## Embra's future — honor the bargain

**If a cooperative dragon leaves with them:**

> *Embra stays just outside the circle of conversation, her wings folded close. She could fill the clearing with a breath. Instead, she waits to be invited to speak.*

> “I am not very practiced at asking for things.”

Amy: “Then practice.” A cautious beginning is enough; don't declare universal forgiveness. Let the players decide what trust and safeguards their agreement needs.

**If Embra remains confined or opposed:** Amy can grieve the friendship without excusing what happened. Resolve who watches her, what terms were offered and whether the barrier remains. Do not narrate her release because it makes a prettier ending.

**Treasure:** record gifts freely given by Embra separately from stolen hoard items and altar gems. Only voluntary dragon gifts avoid the hoard curse. No automatic haul or extra magic item for every PC; use what was actually promised and obtained.

## Return to Aurelius when they choose

If the party wants to go elsewhere first, stop on that choice. Otherwise use a short journey transition after urgent hunt threats are resolved.

> *Aurelius lifts his head as you enter the grove. His gaze moves from your tired faces to Amy's, and the light around his horn steadies.*

**Aurelius:** “You are home in your own life. That is enough for today.”

Amy, after learning what is needed:

> “Morwenna used my kindness as an opening. I won't let her decide that kindness was the mistake.”

**Aurelius:** “My light and your friendship may open the thorns. What happens beyond them will still be a choice.”

This establishes a willing ally for the next arc, not an NPC weapon the PCs can command. Morwenna's PPS-founding secret remains unrevealed here. No new answer to Marigold's southern whisper is assumed.

## If Amy survives but is not restored

Use the cave map or the neutral conclusion backdrop according to where they stop.

**Amy:** “You have given me time. I know that isn't the same as finishing. But I can breathe again.”

Name the remaining obstacle: key, Embra's whereabouts, proximity, or a real complication from play. Ask which one the PCs tackle next. Do not award rescue-complete honors or declare the thorn barrier solved. This is a session ending, not the adventure's conclusion yet.

## If Amy dies

> *The path out of the trees is the same path. That feels wrong. The water still runs beside it. The morning still comes. For a little while, nobody says anything.*

Give space before any hook. A decent knight who learns the truth may pledge to help restore her:

> “We were wrong. Tell me what can still be done, and I will do my part.”

A possible lingering Amy spirit is a future direction, not a guaranteed free resurrection. Ask the players whether they want to pursue restoration, expose Embra, or change the campaign's approach to Morwenna. Aurelius has no instant replacement for Amy's promised help. Preserve the consequences without declaring the whole campaign over.

## Close with each character

- **Filly:** “Which creature or friendship from these woods will you remember?” Record observed animals for a future level-4 discussion; do not grant Wild Form early.
- **Vega:** “What did you build, repair or understand that mattered most?” Keep his real equipment and crafted silver distinct from hypothetical loot.
- **Marigold:** “Whom did you help to hope again—or whose loss are you carrying?” Do not answer for her or impose an emotional response.

**GM close:** confirm current bodies/locations, Amy's HP/state, Embra's bargain, knight loyalties, Ulrik/Damon's fate, curse stages, rewards and consumed resources. Check advancement deliberately; the prep does not award it. Update campaign state only from play, and carry unvisited scenes forward unchanged.
