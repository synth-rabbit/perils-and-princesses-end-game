# Scorchwoods Wrinkles

Roll d6

| Roll | Wrinkle |
|---|---|
| 1 | A group of 2d4 crystal wolves emerges from the trees. They look hungry. |
| 2 | You hear a small voice calling for help. A child? No, a gnome! |
| 3 | You encounter a party of knights looking for the dragon's lair. Roll or choose a party. |
| 4 | You notice a large hole torn in your pack. Did you lose anything important? |
| 5 | In your inattention you got turned around and lost the path. |
| 6 | You suddenly feel very itchy. Poison oak? |
