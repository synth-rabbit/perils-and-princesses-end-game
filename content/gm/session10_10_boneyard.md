# Session 10 Scene 10: Optional: Boneyard Wolves

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]

## Run the scene

**GM map confirmed:** use your existing Boneyard map. No replacement map is needed. The module scene named below is a reference/fallback.

**Foundry scene:** **The Boneyard**. 🔗 [[02_Boneyard]] · [[Crystal_Wolf]]

> *Between the ribs of a fallen dragon, two wolf pups wrestle over a jeweled walking stick. One shakes it triumphantly. An adult lifts its head, diamond teeth catching the light, and watches Tawny very carefully.*

**Direction:** Begin with a territorial warning, not initiative. Roll **3d4 wolves** for the den; make the pups and adults' priorities clear. They want their family safe. Food, another toy, respectful distance, or a clever distraction can solve this without a fight.

⭐ **Filly:** let animal speech turn the standoff into a negotiation. Tawny's presence matters to the wolves, but does not automatically make them hostile. Other PCs can supply the trade or watch the surroundings.

**Discovery:** the recovered stick bears “Gnomish Brilliance Grows Year Over Year.” Give **Handout C** from [[princess_and_dragon_handouts]] without its answer. Gnome craftsmanship points toward its owner; recovering it can earn Pippin's favor. Do not require the party to already know the owner's name to take an interest.

**Good stopping point:** stick recovered and the players decide whether to find its owner. Record how they left relations with the wolves.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Play the wolves as a family

**GM:** The bones belong to Pitch, an ancestor of Embra, though the PCs have not automatically learned that name. The pups are playing; the adults are assessing danger. Keep a safe route of withdrawal visible.

**To Filly, if she speaks with them:**

> Adult: “Stop there. The little ones are behind me.”
>
> Pup: “It shines! It is the best stick.”

If offered food or a replacement toy, let the offer address what they want. Do not impose a universal bribe price. An obviously useful trade with respectful distance can succeed without a roll; a risky grab must be resolved honestly.

**If Tawny steps forward:**

> *Several adult wolves rise together. None advances yet. Their attention follows the lioness, then shifts back to you.*

Ask what Filly signals to Tawny. Do not decide the animals fight before the players respond.

### Inspect the prize

> *The stick is polished smooth where a small hand would hold it. Its jeweled knob is scratched with tooth marks. Six words have been carefully inscribed along the shaft: “Gnomish Brilliance Grows Year Over Year.”*

Give that exact phrase to players. The initials are **G B G Y O Y**, the stump's colored-knothole sequence. A relevant language/code Talent recognizes the phrasing as a code; do not solve it aloud before they get a chance. Scamp can point out where gnomes pass if he is accompanying them; otherwise the tiny workmanship is a lead for asking locals.

**If they return it after trading:** the pups keep the replacement. **If they steal it:** record the wolves' reaction, not an automatic curse or future ambush. **If they retreat empty-handed:** the stump can still be opened by observing a gnome, and the Pool has another entrance.

### Leaving narration

> *Behind you, the den settles into rustling grass and small growls. The jeweled stick catches a thread of light. Somewhere beneath the forest, its owner is almost certainly complaining about its absence.*

## Hunt procedure at this scene

Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
