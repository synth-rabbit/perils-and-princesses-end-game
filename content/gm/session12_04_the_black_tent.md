# Session 12 Scene 4: The Black Tent

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 10–15 min · **Foundry:** **Knight Camp** map, then the Scorchwoods overworld for the walk

A short bridge scene: tie off the Scorchwoods and point north. Play it before the break.

## The tent at the edge of camp

> *The black tent's flaps are tied from the outside. Nobody guards it and nobody will go near it. Inside: no bedroll. No food. A stand for a suit of armour, empty, and round its feet a ring of dried red mud. On a folding table, a single sheet of paper, weighted with a thorn as long as your hand.*

**Sir Damon and Magus Ulrik** left in the night. A guard, if asked: "The black knight? Walked off north about midnight. Didn't take a horse. Didn't take a *lantern*. The wizard went after him looking like he'd had a letter."

⭐ **Vega** recognises the mud: grave-earth and river clay, bound with something that is not water. Whatever stood on that stand was made, not born.

⭐ **Filly's Sniff** or Tawny: wet earth, old blood, and under it something Tawny knows. Her hackles go up. It is the knight from her dream.

> **Read aloud: the paper under the thorn**
>
> *U.*
> *The girl is found. They will undo it. Let them.*
> *Come home. Bring the knight. I want them to reach the wall. I want them to bring her to it.*
> *I want to watch her try.*
> *— M.*
>
> *On the back, in a different, hurried hand: a sketch map. The Fangtooth peaks. A road. A castle drawn as a rose inside a ring of thorns, and one word written beside it:* **ROSEHOLD.**

This is the first time the players see the initial **M** and the castle's name. It is also the Queen telling them she is waiting. Do not explain it. If they ask who M is, nobody in camp knows.

## Sir Finnian, afterwards

**If he was turned:**
> "I have guarded a dragon in a dress for six weeks and called it devotion. I should like to be useful now. Where do you need me?"

He escorts the prisoner to the cave, and he is available as an **Ally Call** at the castle ([[Ally_Calls]]).

**If he was beaten, not turned:** he is shown the key working (scene 5) and is turned then, with a great deal of noise about it.

**Robin** asks, very quietly, whether the lioness would like some water.

## The road to the cave

One keyed move north, the compass in one hand and the pins in the other. **Embra walks, bound, and complains.** Her talk on the road is the players' chance to decide what they will do with her:

- "You have my pendant on. It doesn't suit you. Nothing I own suits anyone else, that is rather the point of owning it."
- "She was kind to me. I know what that makes this. You needn't say it."
- "Go on, then. Ask me. *Would you have let them kill her?*" A pause. "I told myself I'd stop it at the last moment. I have told myself a great many things in two hundred years."
- About the black knight: "The wizard brought the necklaces. He said a lady in the north would be *grateful*. I do not care for being used by people with worse manners than mine."

**Direction:** she is not sorry enough to be forgiven and not wicked enough to make killing her easy. Let the players sit in that.
