# Session 10 Scene 5: Aurelius's Vision

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 20 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** no dedicated grove scene in the module. Keep **Pristine Pond** as a visual stand-in and show Aurelius's portrait; describe the grove and vision aloud.

🔗 [[Aurelius_the_Unicorn]] · [[Princess_Amy]]

> *Aurelius lowers his shining horn. “You have given this forest back its future. Now I must ask you to save someone else's.” In the gold mist above the grass, a young woman with a crooked crown shares a stolen saucer of cream with a cat. A squirrel objects from her shoulder. She laughs—and carefully pours him his own.*

**Direction:** Amy is **22**, as in the book. Use warmth and recognizable kindness, not an extended childhood biography. One or two images are enough: kindness to an overlooked servant; Scamp listening to a story. Then change the image.

> *The light cools. The squirrel is still there, but now it nestles beneath the chin of a black dragon. The dragon presses one claw against an invisible wall at the mouth of a cave. “I'll be brave,” she whispers. “Someone will come.”*

Aurelius names Amy and explains the **campaign tie**: the woman behind the corruption is **Queen Morwenna**, and Amy's Powerful Friendship together with his light may break the Barrier of Thorns. Amy must live. He remains to heal the land; the PCs' rescue matters.

| Question | Answer to give now |
|---|---|
| Where is Amy? | In the Scorchwoods, trapped in a dragon's form. Your pins can lead you to her. |
| Who is Morwenna? | The Queen behind the corruption, ruling from the White-Capped Mountains. This is the deliberate first-name reveal if it has not occurred. |
| Is she Lady Vespera? | No. Vespera was part of the Baron's story; Morwenna is the larger threat. |
| How do we reverse the change? | Amy can tell you what happened; the mechanism needs examination. No claim that the original key was destroyed. |
| Why not attack the fortress now? | Its thorn barrier resists the approach; rescuing Amy gives an ally and a way through. |
| Did Morwenna found the PPS? | Do not volunteer this. Aurelius declines the full old history: “There is much I should tell you. First, we must keep Amy alive.” Treat a direct, persistent investigation as a player choice; the default reveal remains the castle. |

**What stays hidden:** exact necklace solution, key holder, Ulrik's full role, Damon's command limits, and the PPS origin. Those are playable discoveries in the adventure, not a single briefing dump.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Arriving at the grove

> *At the edge of Aurelius's grove, the last dead leaves crumble beneath fresh grass. He is waiting beside a tree whose blossoms have opened out of season. For once, there is no pain in the way he holds his head.*

**Aurelius — warm, measured, grateful:**

> “Broken stone. Poisoned water. Fear where there should have been shelter. You have answered each of them. I will not forget it.”

Pause for the PCs to respond before asking for more help.

> “I wish gratitude were all I had to offer today. There is someone I cannot reach. Her name is Amy.”

Use the two vision passages above, pausing between the young woman's kindness and the dragon's imprisonment. Amy is an adult; do not use the old child portrayal.

### The campaign connection, spoken aloud

> “The Queen behind the corruption is called Morwenna. The road to her ends at a wall of living thorns. My light alone cannot open it. Amy carries a gift of friendship that may succeed where strength fails.”
>
> “But first she must survive. Go to the Scorchwoods. Find her. Listen before you draw your weapons.”

**If they ask why he cannot go:**

> “What you have healed is still fragile. I must stay to help it take root. That is my part. I am asking you to choose yours.”

**If they ask whether Amy is only useful as a weapon:**

> “No. Even if she could never help us, she would still deserve to come home.”

**If they want the entire solution:**

> “I can show you where her spirit is caught. I cannot tell you everything that happened between two people in a cave. Amy can.”

**If Marigold asks about the southern whisper:**

> “I will not give a name to a voice I have not heard. Keep listening carefully. You do not have to answer it blindly.”

This preserves the unresolved thread; it does not identify the whisper as Amy, Dain or Morwenna.

### Close on a decision

**Ask:** “What do you want to know before you agree to go?” Use the answers already in the table above. If they decline, let the conversation continue around their reasons; do not declare the agreement for them. If they accept, Aurelius bows his head, relieved. Move to the omen and dispatch.
