# Session 10 Scene 4: The Pond Rewards

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Run from this note:** narration, dialogue and routine scene rulings are included below. Reference links are optional background.
> **Table time:** 10 min · **Optional reference:** [[princess_and_dragon_runbook]]

## Run the scene

**Foundry scene:** remain on **Pristine Pond**. Show the Lunar Grotto Spear artwork when Filly receives it.

> *The last dark vine loosens its grip on the bank. Clear light rises through the pond, and the pixies begin a song without words. On a stone just beneath the water rests a sea-glass spear. When Filly reaches for it, it becomes a stream of cold water—and gathers itself perfectly into her hand.*

**Direction:** Let Filly describe trying it. Explain the spear using the rules table below, matching its established item entry.

| Reward | Recipient | Rule / continuity |
|---|---|---|
| [[The_Lunar_Grotto_Spear|Lunar Grotto Spear]] | Filly | d6, versatile d8; Returning Current; Gift abilities detailed below. Recharge: tip in pristine water for 1 minute during Picnic/Rest **or** direct moonlight overnight. |
| Moonpetal ×1 | Party chooses holder | Existing campaign reward cures one condition; agree which condition is applicable when used, rather than assuming universal curse removal. |
| Refreshed Fey Blessing | All three PCs | Thistledown renews the established blessing. No added universal combat bonus. |
| Dewdrop Elixir ×1 | Holder chosen at riddle | Already granted if riddle solved; do not award a second copy here. |

On victory, all three footholds are cleared. Give a short forest-healing montage: Mama Bear's cubs play in healthy grass; birds return; Grumbold breathes easily. These are payoff images, not three compulsory travel encounters.

## Narration, conversations and player choices

### Let the thanks belong to the party

**Thistledown, with ceremony abandoned for sincerity:**

> “We were running out of tricks. You stayed anyway.”

To Filly:

> “You and your great friend kept the little ones safe. The pond has something that should travel with you.”

To Vega:

> “The ground listened to you. I think it was relieved to hear someone kind.”

To Marigold:

> “Some people see only the sickness. You keep looking for what can still be saved.”

**GM:** Adjust those thanks to actual actions. If another PC saved the pixies, name that action rather than crediting a scripted deed to Filly. The spear remains her planned gift, not a killing-blow prize.

### Explain the spear without another note

**Thistledown:**

> “Throw it, and it remembers your hand. Let it drink clean water when it grows tired. And when you and Tawny hunt together—well. I would not volunteer to stand in front of you.”

| Feature | Rule to give Filly |
|---|---|
| Basic weapon | d6, versatile d8; thrown 30 feet. |
| Returning Current | Dissolves on impact after a throw and reforms in her hand. |
| Lunar Surge | Roll Gift Dice on a melee/thrown attack: modify the attack roll up to [SUM]. On a hit add [SUM] Cold/Water damage; if Tawny engages the target, add [DICE]d4 Pack Hunt damage. |
| Tidal Wave Thrust | [DICE] creatures within a Stone's Throw take [SUM] Cold damage; GRACE or fall prone and be pushed [SUM] feet. |
| Water Grace | Spend Gift Dice to modify GRACE tests by [SUM] when swimming or climbing wet rock. |
| Dice / recharge | 1–3 returns a rolled die; 4–6 consumes it. Tip in pristine natural water for a minute during Picnic/Rest, or direct moonlight overnight, restores consumed dice. Use the established sheet's pool; do not invent extra dice. |

Explain [DICE] as the number of dice rolled and [SUM] as their total. Resolve doubles through the character's established Gift Mishap rules. Allow a harmless practice throw without spending resources on a compulsory demonstration.

**Moonpetal:** “One petal, one burden lifted. Keep it for when someone needs it.” Agree the applicable condition on use; it is not a promise to erase every major curse. Renew the established Fey Blessing without adding a new universal combat bonus.

### Close this chapter

> *The clearing exhales. Water runs over the stones with a brighter sound. Far away, something sings and another bird answers. Three places hurt by the same darkness have been given back to the forest.*

Ask each player what their character does with this quiet moment. Take the break, then move to Aurelius's grove; the pond backdrop can remain as a visual stand-in.
