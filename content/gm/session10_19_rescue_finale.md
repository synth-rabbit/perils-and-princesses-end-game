# Session 10 Scene 19: The Rescue — Two Necklaces, Two Lives

> **Session hub:** [[session10_prep]] · **Asset checklist:** [[session10_asset_checklist]]
> **Use when reached:** this is reserve material for the entire rescue. Move an unplayed note to a later session folder without changing its basename; update its session field/title then. Nothing here is established as played.
> **Run from this note:** narration, conversations, clues and scene rules are included. Other note links are optional background or continuation scenes.

**Foundry scene:** Use **your existing Dragon’s Cave map** for the cave finale; use your existing **Knight Camp** map if the decisive negotiation happens there.

## Enter when the players' plan converges (30–60 minutes)

Run this when both wearers can be brought together, a key has been obtained, Embra is ready to bargain, or the hunt reaches Amy. These are possible triggers, not conditions you must force into one fight. The existing cave art link below is reference only; keep the GM's map.

**If they return with hope:**

> *Scamp hears you first. His sharp chattering brings Amy's head up from her folded claws. She tries to stand, thinks better of it, and asks very carefully, “Did you find a way?”*

**If the pins warned of an attack:**

> *Steel rings somewhere beyond the tunnel. Amy's voice rises above it: “Please listen!” Your pins burn red against your chests.*

State who is where, Amy's actual HP and what can be seen. The barrier stops the dragon body, not the rescuers or human hunters. Do not pretend it protects Amy from everyone.

## The solution checklist, in play

1. **Key:** Embra's intact original, or Pippin's replacement made with Pool water plus payment/earned fee waiver. No extra key is needed.
2. **Proximity:** both wearers must be **Nearby**. Resolve their travel, bargain, escort or capture; a key used far away does not work remotely.
3. **Unlock, then remove both necklaces.** The locked enchantment otherwise prevents removal.
4. **Barrier separately:** Embra can undo it from outside while human. If restored to her dragon body inside first, she is trapped too, but can instruct an outside helper to dispel it. Hear a different concrete magical plan fairly; don't invent a new ingredient or spell tax.
5. **Hunters:** clarify identities to onlookers and stop anyone attacking the wrong body. A reversal does not automatically make a knight understand what happened.

## Embra's choice

**If she has agreed to cooperate:**

> “I want your promise first. No cheering while they put a sword through me. No handing me to a knight who thinks mercy is an insult.”

Let the players give promises they can actually keep. Amy responds:

> “I wanted to be your friend. I still want you to have a life. You cannot have mine.”

**If she is cornered but not persuaded:**

> “You have your key. You have your witnesses. What exactly do you intend to do with me afterward?”

Allow negotiation, restraint or a convincing alternative. Her attachment to luxury and security is a motive, not a prescribed price. Do not require her to become morally transformed before the enchantment can be reversed.

## Narrate the reversal only when earned

> *The key turns. Both necklaces answer with the same brief note, clear as a glass touched at the rim. For a heartbeat the two figures seem doubled, each holding the other's shadow. Then the light breaks.*
>
> *Amy looks down at her human hands. She opens them, closes them, and begins to laugh through her tears.*
>
> “Oh. My hands. I missed my hands.”

Amy is 22. The dragon is Embra again. Place/update tokens explicitly. Do not reset damage, HP or resources to a fresh encounter maximum without an announced ruling; the source does not give a universal cross-body HP transfer formula. Preserve any established ruling and decide unresolved transfer openly from the actual state.

If the barrier still holds Embra:

> *She reaches toward the cave mouth. Light flashes against her claw. For the first time, she looks at the barrier from Amy's side.*

Embra can bargain for help and explain dispelling from outside. Do not script humiliation as a prerequisite for freedom.

## If the knights are present

**Finnian:** “Stand down. I said stand down. We were sent against our own princess.” Give him credible evidence; he is not magically convinced without it.

**Stella:** “Show me which one needs protecting. And make the explanation good.” Once persuaded, she acts decisively; before that, diplomacy during her attack is at disadvantage.

**Raphael:** “Everyone stop trying to have a heroic moment. We can still get everyone out alive.” He is ready to listen.

**Ulrik:** “Damon. Finish it.” Revealing Amy's identity will not persuade Damon. Ulrik commands within a Stone's Throw; outside it the last order continues. No automatic resurrection or silver-only finishing requirement.

## If it goes another way

- **Hunt neutralized but swap unresolved:** Amy is safer; continue the key problem. Do not manufacture new hunting parties to restore pressure.
- **Embra flees:** follow the real consequences of her escape and remaining tools; don't teleport her to a required final room.
- **Amy dies in the dragon body:** the swap becomes permanent. Embra may use her position to order arrests; decent knights are horrified. Amy may linger as a spirit, but no instant new key undoes the death. Pause for the players' chosen response. The campaign continues toward a restoration quest or a changed plan against Morwenna.
- **Early clever success:** let it succeed. Ulrik need not appear for a forced boss fight.

When the immediate danger is settled, move to [[session10_20_conclusion]].

## Hunt procedure at this scene


Keep the recorded value across scenes. Start at **2**, or **3** if at least half failed the cave-orb RESOLVE test. No elapsed real-world time or session break advances it.

- Travel to a new keyed location: separate **d4 knight encounter** and **d4 Wrinkle** checks; each triggers on 1. A normal successful move adds no point by itself.
- **+1** for Spend Time, a Wrinkle, a wasted-time action/test, or letting encountered hunters leave unhindered. **+2** for an overnight Rest. **−1** for hindering or genuinely allying with a party; **−2** for defeating one. A living captain eating a gem apple gives −1, once willingly; Damon is unaffected.
- Table rulings: do not charge one wasted-time event twice; a separate Wrinkle adds its own point. Overnight +2 replaces ordinary Spend Time. Floor 0. Explain elective time costs first.
- **d6 Wrinkle:** 1: 2d4 hungry crystal wolves (HP4/SAVE6/Armor0; one or two attacks, bite d6/claws d4); 2: gnome calls for help; 3: knight party; 4: tear in a pack suggests a lost item, resolve fairly; 5: lost path; 6: poison-oak itching. Describe the problem and allow a response, rather than assigning an extra invented penalty.
- **d4 knights:** 1 Finnian, 2 Stella, 3 Raphael, 4 Damon/Ulrik. Use [[session10_14_knight_camp]] for their voices and profiles even when encountered on the trail; use the current location's backdrop.

**At 6:** choose among active hunters, excluding defeated, dissuaded or genuinely allied parties. If none remain, the hunt is neutralized. The pins turn red:

> *The silver rose against your chest warms sharply. Red light spills between its petals. The pull toward Amy has not changed, but now it feels terribly urgent.*

Finnian deliberates for one keyed move, then his party deals **4 damage per move/Spend Time** to Amy. Stella attacks immediately for **4**; diplomacy during her attack has disadvantage. Damon attacks immediately for **8**. Raphael causes no scheduled damage. Use Amy's actual remaining HP. Table interpretation: this is direct off-screen damage, without another attack roll or Armor subtraction. Once PCs intervene, play normally instead of adding off-screen damage on top. Do not automatically reset the tracker at 6 or force a particular hunter to arrive.
