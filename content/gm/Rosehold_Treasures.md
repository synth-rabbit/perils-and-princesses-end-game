# Rosehold Treasures

Level 4 is the last level, so these are the party's last growth. Award them where found; none is cursed unless it says so. Room keys refer to [[Rosehold]].

## The founders' keepsakes

Given by a freed Echo (see [[The_Four_Founders]]). Each recharges at dawn.

| Keepsake | Room | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Hilde's Buckler** | A4 | Shield, Armor +1. Once per Rest, as a Reaction, take a hit meant for a friend At Hand; your Armor counts double against it. |
| **Aethelgard's Shears** | B4 | Silver, d6. Cuts any thorn, vine, web or rope without a test. Thorn-sealed doors open to them. Against plant creatures, ignore Armor. |
| **The Songkeeper's Chime** | C3 | 1 GD relic: **Back to Bed** or **Glitter Spray**. Rung with no dice, it simply calms: one frightened or grieving creature will listen to you. |
| **Wren's True Map** | E2 | Shows every room of the castle the bearer names, including secret doors. Outside the castle: once per journey, a Wrinkle while travelling simply does not happen. |
