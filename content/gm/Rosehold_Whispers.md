# Rosehold Whispers

Queen Morwenna speaks through mirrors, sheets of ice and the thorns themselves. Use one line when the Cold rises, when a mirror wakes, or when a scene needs her voice. She is never ranting; she is tired and certain.

Roll d10

| Roll | Whisper |
|---|---|
| 1 | "You wear my pin. Did they tell you who made it? No. They wouldn't." |
| 2 | "Four of them swore they would never leave me. Count the chairs." |
| 3 | *(to Filly)* "You talk to every beast you meet and still think you got here by mistake. I know that feeling. It doesn't pass." |
| 4 | *(to Vega)* "You have died for these people twice already, little prince. How many times do you owe them?" |
| 5 | *(to Marigold)* "You ran from a home too. I only ran further. Ask them what happens when you stop being useful." |
| 6 | "The unicorn used to let me braid flowers into his mane. He did not come either." |
| 7 | "That girl outside thinks friendship is a kind of light. It is. Lights go out." |
| 8 | "Take what you like from the vaults. Everyone does, in the end. It is the only honest thing people do." |
| 9 | "I am not angry with you. I want you to understand that. I am simply going to be proven right." |
| 10 | *(very quietly, and only once)* "...Is someone there?" |
