# Session 12 Scene 7: The Muster Below Rosehold

> **Hub:** [[session12_prep]] · **Assets:** [[session12_asset_checklist]]
> **Table time:** 25–30 min · **Foundry:** module scene **Castle Approach**, or the Level 0 floor plan

The last scene of the session and the first of the castle. Full room notes: [[00_The_Thornwall]], A1.

## The road north

A montage: do not roll for travel. One image per player; ask each what their character does on the road.

> *North, and up. The Scorchwoods' crystal gives way to pine, then to bare rock with snow in the cracks. At every crossroads somebody is waiting: a badger in a waistcoat who falls in beside you without a word; a goat on a bridge who says he supposes he had better come and see what all the fuss is; a bear and two cubs who simply appear on the path ahead and start walking.*

## The muster

> *The last pines give out below a ridge of bare rock, and there the whole of your last year is waiting for you. Somebody has pitched a striped circus tent. A troll is letting two bear cubs climb him. Forty women in travelling cloaks, each with a silver rose at her collar, stop what they are doing and turn. And above them all, filling the pass from wall to wall, black and blue and three storeys high: ice, with thorns in it.*

**Daisy of Silverspire** meets them.

> **Daisy:** "You brought the princess. You brought the *unicorn*. I brought forty operatives and a strongly worded letter." *She looks up at the wall.* "The Society has been trying to find this place for forty years. Nobody remembers why we started looking."

**Promotion:** she raises all three from Initiate to **Field Operative**, on her own authority, in front of everyone. New pins are not needed. "The ones you have will do. They've been pointing the right way longer than mine has."

## Who is here

Everyone the party befriended. Let the players spot them. One line each is enough; full list and rules in [[Ally_Calls]].

- **Nim the Badger:** "You never did call my name. I got tired of waiting."
- **Billy Goat:** "Did you bring crackers."
- **Mama Bear, Pebble and Pit.** Pebble has grown.
- **Thistledown** and a cloud of pixies, already swapping two operatives' voices.
- **Nettie Blackthorn**, with a kettle on. "Old cold magic. I said so. Nobody listens to a hedge witch."
- **Grumbold**, no longer sneezing, with a spare tooth.
- **Bannik**, half out of the ground, singing about a hen. **Grudd**, beside him: "Cold. Cold. Cold."
- **Mari Lwyd**, who bows.
- **Puca**, with a catapult. "Somebody here rolled a twenty last time and I have *never forgotten it*."
- **Pippin Cobbleton**, with an invoice.
- **Sir Raphael, Lady Michelle, Sir Parker**, and the mule. "It's a living."
- **The crystal wolves.** The pups each have a stick.
- **Sir Finnian**, if turned, rehearsing.
- **Morgana Twinkleberry**, Fairy Godmother, on a camp stool, knitting.

## The choice

Each PC picks **two allies** to carry inside as calls. Then the party picks a way in: **the gate, Bannik's tunnel, or Puca's catapult**.

⭐ **Filly:** every animal here came because of her. If her player has ever worried aloud that Filly is not a real princess, Morgana looks up from her knitting at exactly that moment and says nothing at all.
⭐ **Vega:** Grumbold and Bannik both want his professional opinion on the rock under the castle. It is granite over something hollow.
⭐ **Marigold:** her staff has been pulling, very gently, toward the wall since the treeline. Someone on the other side knows it.

## End here

> *Amy takes off her gloves. Aurelius lowers his horn. Behind you, forty princesses, a troll, a goat and a circus fall silent.*
>
> *"Right," says Princess Amy of Drakenshire, and puts both hands on the ice.*

Cut. Next session opens on round one of the Barrier (A3).
