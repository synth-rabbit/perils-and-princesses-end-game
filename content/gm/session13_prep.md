## 1. Where Session 12 Left Off

The Dragonfilch Curse is over for everyone. The party is **level 4**, healed for the castle, and rich enough that nobody counts money; the players were asked to design **special items** and send them to the GM. The party is in the dragon's cave while the knights celebrate Raphael. Finnian was fooled and never learned the truth; **Damon, Ulrik, Stella and the black tent** were never found.

## 5. GM Tools

### The Cold

Starts at **0** on the causeway. Tell the players it exists. +1 for a loud fight in the open or a forced door; −1 when an Echo is freed. At 2 she notices (patrols double, she starts speaking). Full track: [[rosehold_runbook]] section 3; record on [[rosehold_tracker]].

### d6 complications at the wall (use one if a scene is flat)

From [[Rosehold_Wrinkles]], filtered for Level 0. Each also raises the Cold by 1.

| d6 | Complication |
|---:|---|
| 1 | **Patrol.** Four Thornsworn and a sergeant on the gatehouse wall. One round to hide, bluff or strike first. |
| 2 | **Wisps.** d4+1 Frostbind Wisps drift through the ice toward whoever last spent a Heart Die. |
| 3 | **The thorns move.** Black briars creep across the path behind the party. Cut through, burn through, or go round. |
| 4 | **A mirror wakes.** The ice shows the Queen's face. One line to one hero ([[Rosehold_Whispers]]); WITS or Befuddled. |
| 5 | **Glass ravens.** 2d4 at the gatehouse arrow-slits. Any that escape raise the Cold again. |
| 6 | **Deep frost.** RESOLVE or Weary until a warm room. Torch-bearers and anyone wearing Mama Bear's amulet have advantage. |

### d10 whispers

Use [[Rosehold_Whispers]] whenever the Cold rises. Best for tonight: **1** ("You wear my pin. Did they tell you who made it?"), **6** (the unicorn), **7** ("That girl outside thinks friendship is a kind of light. It is. Lights go out."), **3** to Filly, **5** to Marigold.
