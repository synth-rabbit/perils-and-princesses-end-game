# Battlemaps to find or make (GM-sourced)

You said you'd handle maps yourself — this is the shopping list. When you have one,
drop it in `build/art/maps/`, add a `scene(...)` line in `tools/generate_src.py`,
and re-release.

## Needed for session 9

| Map | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Pristine Pond** | ~25×25. Glowing circular pond, tall grass, lily pads, northern treeline where the corruption tendrils enter. The only session 9 battle without a map. |

## Needed for The Princess and the Dragon

| Map | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Dragon Cave Lair (interior)** | The scene backdrop exists (`scorchwoods_dragon_cave_scene.png`) but a gridded interior battlemap is needed for the Sir Damon confrontation — cave mouth, main cavern, space for a huge dragon token. |
| **Gnome Village (ground level)** | Only if you expect the statue fight at the stump to go tactical; the backdrop scene may be enough. Optional. |

## Needed for the castle assault (Act V)

| Map | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Castle approach / gates** | The causeway through the Barrier of Thorns; where the barrier-shattering set piece and the Thornheart Construct gate fight happen. |
| **Great hall (round table)** | The frozen PPS headquarters hall — Echo encounters and the Mirror of Broken Vows. |
| **Throne room** | The finale: throne of thorns, room for the Frost-Crowned phase and the whole party plus Amy and Aurelius. |
| **Castle gardens (optional)** | Briar Hound pack territory if you want an outdoor castle fight. |

## Already covered

Brokenhill Mine (`mine.jpg`) and the Scorchwoods overworld are in the module.
The Rocky Passages map (`cave.webp`) and the two `.dd2vtt` walled scenes
(grave mounds, Rootbound Barrow) stay in the vault — `.dd2vtt` can't ship in a
